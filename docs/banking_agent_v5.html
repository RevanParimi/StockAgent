Regulatory note: Phase 3 requires SEBI algo trading registration through your broker. Under the 2025 NSE circular, retail users can run algos via Zerodha, Angel One, or Fyers. Your broker handles registration — you get an Algo ID. This is procedural, not a financial cost.
Recommended path for your group: Start Phase 1. Run it for 3–6 months. Track signal accuracy manually against actual market movements. If signals prove profitable, Phase 2 is a small upgrade. Only move to Phase 3 when Phase 2 has demonstrated consistent quality over enough real trades.

Split across 10 people, Phase 1 costs Rs. 800–1,200 per person per month — less than most OTT subscriptions. That is the cost of building a professional-grade trading intelligence system from scratch.
