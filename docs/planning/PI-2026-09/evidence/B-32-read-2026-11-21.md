## Why, from the code

1. **The wrong company (DEEPA).** The deep dive calls `analyse(cand.symbol)`; the orchestrator's
   `_resolve_ticker` (`src/backend/shared/pipeline/base_orchestrator.py`) sends any symbol outside
   the managed and tracked lists to a model as free text, and the model mapped "DEEPA" to
   `DEEPAKNTR`. `run_deep_dives` (`core/discovery/deep_dive.py`) files the report under
   `cand.symbol` with DEEPA's close and entry band, and never compares `report.ticker` with it.
   Of the 20 active shelf ideas, DEEPA is the only one misfiled (probe 5).
2. **The band's BUY over the model's NEUTRAL (TVSMOTOR).** With the verdict bound to the composite,
   `verdict_wait` (`core/discovery/shelf.py`) reads the band's verdict, and the shelf's floor reads
   the model's `final_score` (0.55). TVSMOTOR's model said NEUTRAL at 0.62: it passes both. **In
   the last 60 days of `verdict_shadow.jsonl` (1,401 analyses) the band said BUY 538 times; the
   model itself said NEUTRAL in 220 of them and SELL in 2 (41%).** The other way round is rare
   (band NEUTRAL, model BUY: 11).
3. **The price the analysis names is not read (NITINSPIN, SOUTHBANK, EMCURE).** The entry band is
   3% below to 2% above the dive day's close (`entry_levels` in `deep_dive.py`), whatever the
   thesis says about where to buy. A stock at its dive-day price passes even when the analysis says
   to wait 15–25% lower.
