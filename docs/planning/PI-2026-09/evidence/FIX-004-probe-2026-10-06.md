## What it counts

**FUTURE.** The probe date is the IST date when the probe runs. For each `*_dossier.json` under
`data/predictions/` (SA-009's quarantine sits beside it, in `prediction_quarantine/`, and is not
read), it counts every stored date that is after the probe date. These are the fields that
`merge_curator_output` stamps with its `today` argument:
- observations (`obs`);
- guidance (`guid`);
- signature `first_seen`, `last_seen` and `evidence_dates` (`sig_first`, `sig_last`, `sig_ev`);
- `thesis_since` (`thesis`);
- open questions' `raised_on` and `resolved_on` (`oq_raised`, `oq_resolved`).

It also counts:
- the dossier's `last_updated` (`updated`);
- the date part of the event watermark keys (`event_key`), which is the event's own date;
- how many future observations sit in the digest's "Recent observations": `to_digest` sorts
  by date and lists the newest 5 (`obs_in_recent5`);
- how many future observations share a date with a future watermark key
  (`obs_matching_event_key`), which points to event ingestion as the writer.

It prints one line per dossier that has a future date (`sector/TICKER`, the non-zero counts, the
number in the newest 5, the latest date), then totals and a histogram by date.

**RESEARCH** (SA-038 review L2). It counts post-F3 observations (dated on or after 2026-08-07) by
month, split into `[research]` answers, `[research-partial]` findings, any other `[research…`
prefix, and the rest, each with and without a `source`.

## Reading rules (fixed before any production output was seen)

- **Size.** "Isolated" means one dossier with at most one future-dated row of any kind, counting
  watermark keys. Anything more is a pattern. Either way FIX-004 goes ahead (the owner's decision
  of 6 Oct). The size only sets the production check's baseline and the hard review's focus.
- **Writer.** If every future observation shares its date with a future watermark key, event
  ingestion alone explains it (the card's cause). If a future observation has no matching key, or
  a field that event ingestion does not stamp is future-dated, another writer is involved. The
  implementation must then find that writer before it claims the card's cause is the whole cause.
- **Effect.** Each future observation counted in `obs_in_recent5` is in every digest for that stock
  today. That is the read-time rule's measured target.
- **L2.** Across the post-F3 `[research]` and `[research-partial]` rows, if most (over half) have
  no `source`, record a fix as a routed finding for the owner's decision. F3's milestone is not
  restored either way. If there are no research rows, record "unmeasurable", not "met".

### Reading, by the rules fixed beforehand

- **Size: a pattern.** Two dossiers, 4 stored rows and 4 watermark keys. SA-038's probe printed only
  the newest date (2026-10-24), so "one observation" in the FIX-004 card was a lower bound; it is
  now 3 at one stock, plus a guidance item at another.
- **Writer: event ingestion explains every row.**
  - All 3 future observations share their date with a future watermark key. This is measured
    (`obs_matching_event_key` 3).
  - Event ingestion writes guidance through the same `merge_curator_output` call, with the same
    `today` (code). The probe did not pair guidance with keys. But 2026-10-27 occurs only at
    STARHEALTH, as 1 guidance item and 2 keys (×3 in the histogram), so the guidance date is its
    events' date (inferred).
  - No field that only another writer stamps is future-dated. So the card's cause is the whole
    cause on 7 Oct.
- **Effect.**
  - Every IDFCFIRSTB digest lists 3 of its 5 "Recent observations" as dated 24 Oct, until 24 Oct
    passes. This is measured (`in_recent5` 3).
  - STARHEALTH's guidance item shows under "Open guidance", dated 27 Oct, if its status is open.
    The probe did not read the status, so this is unmeasured.
- **For the implementation.** One of the 4 measured rows is guidance, so the read-time rule must
  cover the "Open guidance" section as well as "Recent observations". The card's write-time
  criterion already names guidance.
- **Two keys per dossier.** In each affected dossier, two events on one future date were ingested
  separately. Inference: two NSE entries with the same date and different subjects. Only board
  meetings carry a date in the future, since announcements are dated when they are published. The
  key text was not printed.
