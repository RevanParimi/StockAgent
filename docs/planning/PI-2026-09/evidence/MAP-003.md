## Choices the card left open

- **The slider has one notch per time a job runs** (27: `macro_market_news` runs three times),
  so every notch shows a job, and playing it visits every job, weekly and monthly ones too.
- **The ring is a circle** around the product (a clock face), 00:00 at the back; daily inside,
  weekly, then monthly and yearly. Beads at nearly the same time step outwards.
- **Speeds 1×, 2×, 4×**; at 1× a clock step lasts 2.4 s, a trace or tour step 6 s. Autoplay never
  starts by itself.
- **The tour opens on every fresh visit** by a manager with no part or mode in the address (a
  dismissal is not remembered); a developer or a deep link goes straight to the map.
- **A mode replaces the open part**; only the clock opens a panel. Opening a part, a view key or
  `Esc` leaves the mode. Addresses: `#p=clock&s=16`, `#p=trace&s=6`, `#p=tour&s=1`.
- **What lights is curated**, checked by reading the code (the guard checks only that it exists).
  It is what a run can reach; the panel says a switch or a quiet day can stop it earlier. Shared
  plumbing (the model client, config) is not lit.
- The eight stops are the whole, discovery, research, portfolio, learning, measurement, storage
  and the clock. Data, Events, IPO, Delivery, Interface and Operations have no stop of their own.
