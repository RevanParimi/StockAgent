  nothing in 3D. 761 to 900 px keeps the 3D view with the panel below.
- **Phone:** the modules are one row that scrolls sideways, a mode's player stands above the map
  (also in reading order), stores three abreast, arrows only for a chosen or lit module.
- **Flat views 1 to 4** change what stands out (flows, no storage, planes, storage). `P` is the 3D
  view's. No switch to force one view.
- **Bloom** was looked at on the money path's views and kept (intensity 0.7, threshold 0.55): names
  stay readable and no text is drawn in 3D.

  would ask a Node build and a 2.8 MB page commit each time.
