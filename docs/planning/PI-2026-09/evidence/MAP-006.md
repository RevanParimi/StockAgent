## The causes

All probes ran in headless Chromium 151 with Playwright 1.62.1. Before any edit, the unchanged
viewer rebuilt the committed page byte for byte.

### B-53: the viewer dropped the `/` (galaxy test, `find(ADVISOR)`)

The viewer lost a key a person can press, mostly on a slow machine, so the viewer is fixed. There
were two ways to lose it.

1. **The late `close` report. This one held the pushes.** Chromium shuts a dialog on `Esc` at once
   and reports `close` only on the next frame (probe order: `close()` returns, microtask, 0 ms
   timer, close event, frame callback). Until then the page's state still said "search", so `/` set
   the state it already had and nothing opened.
   - Replay of the failing test's own steps on the old page: **1 of 5 failed with nothing
     delayed, and 4 of 5 with the report held 1.5 s**. Each failure was "the search did not open",
     with `/` aimed at the page and no `close` reported before it.
   - New page, same replay: **10 of 10 passed**.
2. **Focus left in the shut field**, found while reproducing. When the dialog had nothing to hand
   the focus back to, its search field kept it until the next frame. A `/` pressed then was aimed
   at the field, and the key handler ignored it as typing.
   - Old page, real `Esc` then `/` back to back: **lost 10 of 10** (flat and 3D map, with nothing
     delayed).
   - New page: **0 of 10** in each of five set-ups (flat and 3D, normal and slowed frames, and a
     close and `/` in one task).

### B-59: the zoom was read before the map re-measured (flat keyboard test)

The map hears of its box's new width on a later frame (`ResizeObserver`). The test noted the zoom
right after `Esc`, when it could still be the narrow box's (0.75 against 1.19). Reproduced with the
real test file, in scratch copies: every resize report 400 ms late and carrying the width the box
has by then, with holds while the panel is open and before `Fit`. **The old test failed 3 of 3**
(expected 0.75, got 1.19) and **the new test passed 3 of 3**. A first version, without the hold
before `Fit`, depended on timing: the reviewer's run of it passed.

### The same kind of race in the galaxy keyboard test (found by the Opus check)

It paused a fixed 600 ms before noting the camera, after a panel closed and again after one opened.
The camera is framed again only once the 3D view has measured its new width. With resize reports
900 ms late, **the old test failed** (expected camera `5.4,64.7,75.3`, got `5.4,107.4,128.6`) and
**the new test passed**. Fixed in the same pass, since it is the same class of flake in one of the
two files. The remaining 600 ms pauses only check that nothing moved, so a slow box cannot fail
them.

**Behaviour notes.** A second `Esc` pressed while the focus is still in the shut field now acts on
the page (it closes the panel, or leaves the tour or galaxy). That is the documented `Esc` order,
and what already happened when the focus went back to an element. Chromium puts no text into a shut
dialog's field (8 rounds), so a `blur()` tried first was removed: no test could show it did
anything. Reacting to the dialog's `cancel` event alone was rejected: it covers neither other ways
of closing nor the focus case.

## The new tests, tried against broken viewers

Built in scratch copies. Both new tests **fail on the old viewer**, and on viewers with no
`openDialog` fix, with the two state changes batched (no `flushSync`), or with the old typing check
back (the first test then fails only when no frame falls between its real `Esc` and `?`). A viewer
that lets `/` be typed into the new search fails the second. One survives by design: removing the
typing check altogether. Today's page has no text field outside a dialog (the time slider was
already exempt); the check stays for a future one. The reviewer's own mutant, without the guard
against a late `close`, failed too.
