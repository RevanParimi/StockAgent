## Why it was slow

`tests/frontend/no_invented_figures.test.mjs`: 30 cases, run one after another, with 43 page
loads. One instrumented run (150 s): about 73 s in fixed pauses, and most of the rest loading
pages (about 1.3 s each).

The cost nobody saw: **an app page that is only waiting held about one whole CPU.** The rotating
3D sphere, its blur and the pulsing dots repaint on every frame, and headless Chromium draws them
in software. An idle home page used 4.53 and 5.14 CPU-seconds in 5 s with 4 animations running,
and 0.00 with them stopped (the Opus check read 0.91 and 1.02 cores, and 0.00). The box has 2
CPUs, so every open page took half of it, and other work slowed the suite far more than its own
size would suggest. That this is why it passed 900 s at 06:16 on 26 Nov is an inference: that
run's CPU was not measured.
