## What was built

`autopilot/box_health.py` (new) appends one line to `SESSION_LOG.md` about the IST day before,
and reads `~/devbox` without writing it. An example, the preview of 26 Nov at 23:30 IST:

    ### Box health 2026-11-26 (IST day) · peak anon 2,157 MB at 18:50 IST (693 samples, from
    11:59 IST, to 23:30 IST) · OOM kills 0 · restarts 1 · RC exits (link A) 0 (events.log from
    13:10 IST) · sweep freed 17,902 MB (24 sweeps, sweep.log from 13:25 IST)

(one line in the log; wrapped here)

| Figure | From | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| peak anon | `mem.log` | the highest sample, its time and the number of samples; "from"/"to" when more than 2 minutes of either end of the day have none |
| OOM kills | `events.log` | how far the kernel's counter moved (`OOM_KILL count=N (was M)`, and `BOOT`'s `oom_kill=`, for kills no line counted); the counter restarts with the box |
| restarts | `events.log` | a `BOOT` line's time less `pid1_up`, each restart once (OPS-004's reading, reused) |
| RC exits (link A) | `events.log` | `RC_EXIT` lines |
| sweep freed | `sweep.log` | the fall in file cache over each sweep, added up (a rise counts 0) |

A log that cannot be read, or that holds nothing as old as the day, reads "not known" or "not
recorded", never 0. A day without a sample says its counts may miss events. A log that begins
inside the day says from when.

- **Once a day:** the line is its own record. A start that finds the day's heading writes nothing;
  the lock is on `SESSION_LOG.md` itself, so two starts at one moment cannot both write. No state
  file, no new log, no daemon, no `__pycache__`.
- **Trigger:** `autopilot.sh health` (new); `autopilot.sh start` (also when it refuses to start)
  and the runner before each phase, given up after 60 s and never holding anything; and for
  sessions, a bullet in the owner's `CLAUDE.md`, section 8.

## Limits

- Peak anon comes from one sample a minute: a shorter peak is missed.
- A monitor that stopped during a day shows only as "to HH:MM" (BACKLOG B-40 is about noticing it).
- Kills the monitor finds at its start are dated by that line, not by when they fell.
- "Sweep freed" is the fall `sweep.sh` measured around each sweep, not bytes counted per file.
- A manual session may ask permission once to run the command.
