### The probe

`analysis_data/pr035/invest_probe.py` (ignored folder, on the code-server; 626 lines, sha256
`02ed514d953173f5…`). It opens every file read-only (SQLite `mode=ro`), writes nothing, sets
`sys.dont_write_bytecode`, calls no network and no model, and prints counts, symbols and the
owner's paper book's own figures only: no user id, email, raw log or environment value.

| Section | What it prints | Read from |
|---|---|---|
| A. The door | Per review session since 13 Nov: every active shelf idea and watchlist item, with the reason the buy list left it out, or its outcome at the buy step; totals by door; each door's ideas × one position in rupees | `buy_candidates` itself (the list's own code), given read-only readers; the shelf as it stood at the run (`shelf.json` less ideas added after the run's `generated_at`, plus ideas dropped after it, from `shelf_events.jsonl`); holdings before the run and the ledger's exits before it; the digest's `deployment` record |
| B. The entry band (B-3) | Every active BUY idea: sessions above, inside and below its band since its dive, at or below invalidation, and its return since the dive | `shelf.json`, the EOD parquet files |
| C. The yield | The last 8 Saturdays: universe and screen candidates, dives (re-dives, new, IPO or other), BUY at the 0.55 floor (actionable), placed, turned away, refreshed, dropped by kind | `screens/`, score history on Saturday from 12:25 IST, `shelf_events.jsonl` |
| D. The load | The daily-review list against its cap, by origin; the book's holdings and watchlist; shelf sectors at the cap; one deep dive's and one daily review's cost, tokens, minutes and search calls; a month's cost at today's settings and at PR-036/037's provisional ones | `managed_tickers.json`, `portfolio.json`, `telemetry.db` `run_summaries`, `data/logs/api_usage_events.jsonl` |

Two limits, labelled in its output: a shelf idea re-dived after the run is shown with its newer
record (none expected before Saturday 21 Nov's run); "turned away" cannot tell the shelf's size
from the sector cap, because the run logs that and keeps no record of it.
