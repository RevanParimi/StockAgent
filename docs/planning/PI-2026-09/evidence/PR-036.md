   (`rl.scheduler_max_workers: 1`); its harvest budget is 300 s × tickers, so it grows with the
   list and 60 tickers do not time out. At PR-035's 1.7 minutes a stock, 46 enabled (30 held +
   16 original) end about 17:50 IST and 60 about 18:15, then the portfolio pipeline. The jobs
   after it are 17:45 IPO refresh and 18:30 company events, each its own job; close clock times
   are not dependencies (KT section 9). Model cost [PR-035's unit cost, inference]: a review a
   median $0.0189 (mean $0.0156), so 46 a day about $15-18 and 60 a day about $20-24 a month.
2. **Every test that pins a shipped value** was found by searching the suite for each setting:
   three pin `config.yaml` and were updated on purpose (above). The others set their own values
   through `monkeypatch` (SA-049's fixture, FIX-023, FIX-028, FIX-032, PR-006, PR-011) and are
   unchanged.

   the real executor (`execute_advice`) with the breadth, caps, floor and regime shares read from
   `config.yaml` at test time, and checks them against the card's numbers written in the file:
   - `test_the_shipped_values_are_the_cards`: 20/25/30, 5, 60, 90; the caps, floor and shares
     unchanged;

     ₹25,000 short of ₹80 L), nothing on day 7, cash ₹17,06,000; the record's `position_inr`

   - eight holdings and six ideas: five bought; "13 holdings, below the 20 aimed for: only 6

     sessions each at the configured costs, prices drifting, a random regime and up to 40 random

  reaches every portfolio; `primary`'s override sets only `deploy_idle_cash`.

  position in one sector (9 × 3.2% = 28.8%). `discovery.max_ideas_per_sector` (3) limits the
  shelf, not the book.

  are the holdings (held first). Watched and shelf stocks keep NSE filings and disclosures. The

  4), so 90 may take about 20, ending near 08:35, before the 08:45 shock check; the holdings come
  first, within the first minutes.

  for it describe the old book; PR-037 changes the shelf and rewrites them.
- No finding.
