### The rule, predeclared (fixed in this commit, before the read)

1. **Which dives.** Every deep dive that put an idea on the shelf or kept it there: an `added` or
   `redived` event in `shelf_events.jsonl`, or the dive a shelf record carries when no event names
   it (one per stock and IST day). Only dives whose analysis rated the stock a BUY or STRONG BUY:
   the idea's record for its latest dive, otherwise the `score_history` analysis of that stock on
   the dive's day whose final score equals the dive's conviction. A dive whose verdict is not
   found is left out of the test and counted. Not filtered: the data gate, the sector key, the
   shelf's size or the other doors of the buy list. The question is the price alone.
2. **The dive's close and band.** The close the dive read: `close_at_add` for the dive that shelved
   the idea, `entry_high / 1.02` for a re-dive with a recorded band, else the last EOD close on or
   before the dive's day. The band and invalidation level as recorded, else `entry_levels` (the
   production function) on the 90 sessions up to the dive's day.
3. **A fresh session.** An EOD session after the dive's day, at most 30 days after it
   (`discovery.redive_after_days`), not after the stock's next dive, and before the day the idea
   left the shelf (dropped or promoted). The would-be buy is booked at that session's close.
4. **Where the close stood.** At or below invalidation; below the band; inside it (`entry_low` ≤
   close ≤ `entry_high`); above it, by the close against the dive's close: to 5%, 5–10%, 10–15%,
   over 15%.
5. **The return.** At 10, 30 and 60 EOD sessions: the close N sessions later against the
   session's close; against the Nifty, that less the NIFTY 50's return over the same two dates.
   Only matured returns count (the N-th session exists and the stock has a close on both days).
6. **The unit of the test.** One would-be buy per dive and side: its first fresh session inside
   the band; its first fresh session above the band and at most 10% above the dive's close (the
   proposed limit). A dive can count on both sides.
7. **The test (the card's step 3).** With at least 15 matured 30-session returns on each side, the
   10% limit ships if the mean 30-session return against the Nifty of the "above" side is no
   lower than the "inside" side's. If it is lower, the band stays and the card closes with the
   tables: "no benefit". The 30-session test decides whenever it can.
8. **Too few at 30 sessions** (a case the card leaves open). The same test at 10 sessions, with at
   least 15 on each side, decides provisionally. A pass ships the limit with a dated row that
   reads the 30-session test once both sides reach 15; a fail then sets the limit back to 2.0. A
   fail at 10 sessions keeps the band, and the same row reads the 30-session test. With fewer than
   15 on either side at 10 sessions as well, the band stays, nothing is built, and a dated row
   re-reads on the date the split projects for 15 on each side.
9. **Another limit.** The owner may choose 2%, 5%, 15% or none from the limits table; the test is
   then the same with "above" read as above the band and at most that limit.
10. **Reported, not tested.** Every fresh session by where it stood (the card's step 1); for each
    limit, the dives it buys (the first fresh session above invalidation and at most the limit
    above the dive's close; today's rule at `entry_high`), how many more than today and how many
    sooner, and their returns, own and against the Nifty, also for the extra dives alone; the
    test again with the dives of unknown verdict added.

What it cannot say: a dive is a what-if on the paper lane, not a purchase (the daily limit, the
sector key, the target and cash are not applied); a stock dived again counts once per dive; the
price is the session's close, which PR-016 measures against prices that could really be had; the
recomputed invalidation uses today's `advisor.stop_atr_mult`.
