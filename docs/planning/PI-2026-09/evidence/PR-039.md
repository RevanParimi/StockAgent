## The places, as the report reads them

A stock's place is the furthest it got in the window; the date is the first time it got there.

| Place | Read from | Reason on record |
|---|---|---|
| 1 outside the floors | the week's screen `rejected` (the guards), else the bhavcopy floors as of the screen's date (series EQ, price ₹20, a company in the listing, 60-session median traded value ₹5 cr, the upper-circuit streak) | the screen's gates, or the floor missed |
| 2 below the cut | inside the floors, not among the screen's candidates | candidates, universe and shortlist sizes |
| 3 not analysed | a candidate with no deep dive that Saturday | rank, the run's dive count against the budget, an analysis in the 30 days before |
| 4 turned down or turned away | the score history (the dive memo when it cannot be read): not a BUY, below the 0.55 floor, or the data gate not actionable; a deep-dive BUY not shelved that day | verdict and conviction; "the shelf's size or the sector cap; the run does not record which" |
| 5 never bought | on the shelf (`shelf_events.jsonl`, `shelf.json`), or a daily-review BUY | the digests' `deployment` record: the outcome and why for that symbol, "not offered by the buy list" when deployment ran without it, or "the switch was off" |
| book | the ledger: held at the start, or bought in the window (and sold, when it was) | — |

A stock the guards rejected from the shortlist counts as further down than one ranked below the
cut: its signals ranked it high enough, and a floor stopped it. The deep dive is read from the
analysis time (Saturday from 12:25 IST; the job runs at 12:30) and the daily review from a
weekday 16:25 to 23:00 IST (the job runs at 16:30); both checked against
`services/scheduler/python/scheduler.py` and `config.yaml` (`feedback_cron`) in phase 2.

- The screen file's keys (`candidates`, `rejected`, `universe_size`, `shortlist_size`) match
  `ScreenResult` as `core/discovery/screen.py` persists it; `rejected` is the guards'
  `dict[str, list[str]]`.

  `core/portfolio/autopilot.py` and `core/portfolio/deployment.py` write them.

  the screen reads; test `test_floors_match_the_screens_own_gates` holds them to the screen's
