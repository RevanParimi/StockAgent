## Decisions (taken as recommended, CLAUDE.md section 2)

- **D1. A screen's lead** is the cut's mean return less the rest's: what a position put into every
  stock of each group made on average (the card's money example is a mean). The rule's "mean lead
  and median lead" are the mean and median of that one figure over the screens. The gap between the
  two groups' medians is shown beside it and not tested.
- **D2. Judged as printed:** a screen's lead is the difference of its two means to two places, and
  the rule reads the pooled figures to two places.
- **D3. Entry** is the first stored session after the screen's recorded `run_date`. Files written
  before PR-017 record none: they are taken as run on the Saturday after their session. A recorded
  day before the screen's own session is not believed (it would look ahead).
- **D4.** Two screen files that enter on the same session measure one window: the later stands, and
  the other is named.
- **D5. The rest** needs a session on the screen's date and passes `floors_out` as of it. The
  listing is today's, as in PR-039; the stocks left out only for that are counted per screen.
- **D6. Its own script**, not `missed_risers.py --cut`. It does not call `load_sources`, which also
  reads the score history, the shelf and the owner's ledger: the cut needs none of them.
- **D7. One price row a stock a day**, its equity series' (EQ, BE, BZ, SM, ST, SZ). The others that
  share such a day are counted in a note (Opus L2; BACKLOG B-57).
- **D8. Shown, not tested** (Opus M1, C1, L3): restated closes per group and the leads without
  them; every candidate that moved 50% or more in 30 sessions; the lowest and highest screen lead;
  and the leads of cuts of 40 or fewer apart from wider ones. **None of them changes the rule.**
