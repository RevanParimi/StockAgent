### The decision: stop, and the owner chooses

The card's step 1: "If dates or the full result list do not come back, stop and say so in the
receipt and the summary: the owner chooses between an Exa account of its own (dates and filters)
and stopping. A result without a date cannot be tested against the decision day." The full list
comes back; the date does not. So step 2 is not built, and PR-043 is `blocked` on the owner's
choice:

- **A. An Exa account of its own** (recommended). Exa's own API is the documented route to a
  publish date and a start-date filter, so the read's test, "published in the 30 days before the
  decision", is exact. The owner signs up, reads Exa's price list there, and puts the key into
  Railway (or says to). The next session then builds step 2 against Exa's API; the rest of the
  card stands.
- **B. Through OpenRouter as probed,** with each item dated by its URL or excerpt and an undated
  item counted as not new. This changes the read's rule, which the owner fixed; it can only
  undercount what Exa adds, since 42 of 100 items here had no date.
- **C. Stop PR-043.** PR-044 waits on it and stops with it.

Why A: the read's whole question is whether Exa finds material, recent items that Serper's five
miss. With B, almost half the items would be judged by a guessed date or by none.
