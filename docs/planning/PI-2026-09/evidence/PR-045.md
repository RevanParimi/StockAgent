## Your questions (the owner, 21 Nov)

**"I don't think any new buy was done on 20 Nov, but the verdict changes show BUY and SELL on
20 Nov."** Both can be true. A verdict change is a re-analysis of a stock (`/ui/learnings`, from
`scores.db`), not a trade, and a BUY verdict buys nothing by itself (KT section 6, "A BUY report
does not automatically purchase shares"). Buys come only from the 16:30 review's idle-cash step,
and an idea must pass every check there (KT section 6, "Cash deployment"). Two timing rules
matter:

- The 16:30 run of day T books its trades at the **previous** session's close and dates them
  with that session. Friday 20 Nov's run reviews Thursday 19 Nov, so anything it bought is dated
  19 Nov on the screen.
- An analysis made after a session's close counts only from the next session's close. A BUY
  verdict from Friday 20 Nov's evening analysis can be bought at Monday 23 Nov's close at the
  earliest, by Tuesday 24 Nov's run, and only if it is a shelf idea or on your watchlist, inside
  its entry band, cleared by the data gate (now `enforce`) and NSE's surveillance report.

"Score moved -0.01 → now SELL" does not explain the flip: the card shows the change in the model's
own score (`final_score`), while the verdict follows the composite's band (KT section 4, step 6),
a separate number (BACKLOG B-31). Nothing is sold on a verdict alone; a holding is sold by the
advisor's exit rules.

What Friday's run actually bought was not read: the read-only probe below was refused by this
session's permission check (production is read only at your word). After the next push the new
card "New buys at the last review" shows it (dated 19 Nov). For an answer sooner, say "run the
20 Nov buy probe" in a session on the code-server; it runs
`railway ssh 'python -' < analysis_data/owner_q_1121/probe.py` from the StockAgent folder, which
reads `data/portfolio/primary/digests/2026-11-16..20.json`, `transactions.jsonl` and
`portfolio.json` and prints, per digest, the trades, the regime, the share invested before and
after, the holdings, what was bought, the reasons and each idea's outcome; the ledger's trade
count per date; and whether OLAELEC and WAAREEENER are held or watched. It writes nothing.

**"I don't see the history of the rates each stock was bought and sold at, the profit or loss
