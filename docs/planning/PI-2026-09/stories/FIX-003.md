# FIX-003 — Read a bank's income statement as complete

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **routed** by the [SA-003 enforce decision](../evidence/SA-003-enforce-decision-2026-10-01.md)
(2026-10-01, NO-GO) · Estimate: **1 point**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: none. It blocks the
next SA-003 enforce decision. Related: [SA-002](SA-002.md) (the fundamentals contract) and
[SA-045](SA-045.md) (which must read substituted zeros as missing).

## Intended result

A bank's income statement has no operating-income line. `get_financials` looks for
"Operating Income", then "EBIT", then "Gross Profit"; a bank has none of them. So it substitutes
zeros, lists `operating_income` in `missing_rows`, and the fundamentals section becomes
`fallback`. The decision gate treats `fallback` fundamentals as unusable essential data and
abstains. Every analysis of a bank therefore abstains, and the prompt shows an EBITDA margin of
0.0%.

**Example.** On 1 Oct, YES Bank's fundamentals came back with the June quarter's revenue
(₹4,655.98 Cr), dated 30 Jun 2026: fresh and complete for a bank. The section was still
`fallback`, so the October forecast abstained in record mode. Under `enforce`, YES Bank would
get no October envelope and no learning.

**Measured** (read-only logs, 28 Sep to 1 Oct): YESBANK, IDFCFIRSTB, RBLBANK and FEDERALBNK
abstained on every analysis, 17 of 78 (22%). That alone breaks SA-003's 20% no-go threshold.
PAYTM, in the same sector, has `EBIT` and was never affected.

## Acceptance criteria

- **A bank-shaped statement is complete.** A bank-shaped statement has `Interest Income` and
  `Net Interest Income`, and no "Operating Income", "EBIT" or "Gross Profit" row. When it has
  revenue and a fresh newest quarter, `get_fundamentals_result` gives `ok` (or `stale` by the
  usual age rule), not `fallback`. Its `reason` names the bank format.
- **No invented zero.** For a bank-shaped statement, the prompt shows no 0.0 operating income
  and no 0.0% EBITDA margin. Either:
  - it states that operating margin is not reported for banks; or
  - it uses a named bank measure (for example pre-tax income), labelled as such.

  The implementation chooses one and records why.
- **SA-002's contract holds everywhere else:**
  - a non-bank statement without an operating-income row stays `fallback`;
  - a bank-shaped statement without revenue stays `fallback`;
  - a present row with a blank newest quarter stays `fallback` (review M2).
- **The gate follows.** On fixtures shaped like these banks' statements, the data-health
  row's `essential_unusable` is empty. The decision gate for such a run is `actionable` or
  `degraded`, never `abstain` for fundamentals.
- **Shape, not sector.** The test is the statement's shape, not the ticker's sector or its
  name. PAYTM has `EBIT` and `Net Interest Income` and stays an ordinary statement.

Use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports; no real yfinance call in tests.
The independent invariant is a table of statement shapes against expected statuses, not a copy
of the implementation's branch.
