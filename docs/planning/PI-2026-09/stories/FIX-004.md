# FIX-004 — Never date dossier knowledge after the day it was learned

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **routed** by SA-038's production probe (2026-10-06; finding F1 in the
[SA-038 implementation receipt](../evidence/SA-038-implementation.md)) · Estimate: **1 point**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: none. Owner decision
2026-10-06 ("yup i agree"): a fix story of its own, taken after SA-038's review and commit, before
the normal queue resumes. Related: [SA-046](SA-046.md) (its reader weights dossier events by
recency; its card carries the first note on this) and [SA-013](SA-013.md) (information cutoff at
issue).

## Intended result

A dossier note must carry the date the system learned it, never a later date. Today, event
ingestion can stamp a note with a date in the future, and the prompts then present it as the most
recent thing that happened.

**Example.** On 6 Oct a company announces a board meeting for 24 Oct, to consider its Q2 results.
- **Today:** the weekly event scan picks it up, and the dossier stores
  "2026-10-24: board meeting to consider Q2 results". On 7 Oct a forecast agent reads it under
  "Recent observations" and can take the results as already out.
- **After the fix:** the note is dated 6 Oct and says the meeting is scheduled for 24 Oct.

**Measured** (SA-038's owner-run read-only probe, 6 Oct 10:41 IST): one dossier observation is
dated **2026-10-24**. The probe printed counts only, so the ticker, the text and how many other
future-dated rows exist are **not** known.

**Measured again** (this card's own count probe, 7 Oct 11:43 IST; see the
[probe receipt](../evidence/FIX-004-probe-2026-10-06.md)):
- Of 25 dossiers, **2** hold future-dated rows. **IDFCFIRSTB** has 3 observations dated 24 Oct,
  all 3 in its digest's newest 5. **STARHEALTH** has 1 guidance item dated 27 Oct.
- Each dossier has 2 watermark keys on that date. All 3 observations match a key, so event
  ingestion explains every row.
- This is a pattern, not one note. Because one row is guidance, the read-time rule below covers
  "Open guidance" as well as "Recent observations".

**Cause (code):**
- `EventIngestor.find_qualifying_events` scans NSE announcements **and board meetings**. It drops
  events older than the lookback, but has no upper bound (`event_ingestor.py:111`).
- `EventIngestor.run` then merges the LLM digest with `today=event["date"]`
  (`event_ingestor.py:239`). `merge_curator_output` stamps every row it writes with that date:
  - observations (`date=today`);
  - guidance (`date=today`);
  - response signatures (`first_seen`, `last_seen`, `evidence_dates`).
- `TickerDossier.to_digest` sorts observations by date and shows the newest five
  (`dossier.py:122`). A future-dated note therefore sits in every prompt for that stock as the most
  recent observation until real notes pass its date.

## Acceptance criteria

- **No stored date after the run date.** Whatever the event's date (past, today, future), every
  observation, guidance and signature date that event ingestion writes is on or before the day of
  the run.
  - For a future event, the date is the day it was learned, and the scheduled date stays in the
    text. Or the event is skipped until it happens. The implementation chooses one and records why.
- **Past and same-day events are unchanged.** An announcement from two days ago keeps its own date,
  as today. Re-ingestion stays prevented: the watermark key `date|subject` is unchanged.
- **Existing future-dated rows are handled at read time, not by a data migration.** The digest never
  presents a row dated after the digest date as a past observation, whether it excludes, re-labels
  or clamps it. This covers "Recent observations" and "Open guidance"; the 7 Oct probe found
  future dates in both. Production data is not rewritten (no migration is authorised).
- **The daily curator and the research loop are unchanged.** They already stamp the run date.

Tests use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports, with no real NSE, Tavily or LLM
call. The independent invariant is a table of event dates against the run date, with expected
stamps computed from the rule above, not copied from the implementation. Add a digest test with
the clock pinned: a row dated after it is never listed as a recent observation.

## Implementation choice (2026-10-08)

Recorded in full in the [implementation receipt](../evidence/FIX-004-implementation.md).
- **Write time: skip an event dated after the run until it has happened.** It is not
  digested and not watermarked, so the first scan on or after its date picks it up. Why:
  - The ingestion prompt tells the model "A corporate event just happened". Digesting a
    meeting that has not been held invites it to report results that are not out.
  - The fundamentals and earnings agents already see upcoming board meetings in the live NSE
    feed, so the dossier loses nothing they lack.
  - A future meeting no longer takes one of the scan's 3 event slots.
  - The lookback is 8 days and the scan is weekly, so every meeting date falls inside one
    scan's window.
- **Read time: leave the row out.** The digest picks its 5 "Recent observations" and its
  "Open guidance" from rows dated on or before its own date (default today). Why: a re-label
  would need a new section for text written under the "just happened" prompt, and a clamp would
  show the wrong date.

## Fresh review (2026-10-10): accepted

Recorded in the [review receipt](../evidence/FIX-004-review.md). No critical, high or medium
finding. Both rules are upheld. One low finding becomes a follow-up:

- **L1: the morning brief reads guidance without the date rule.** Its "Earnings within 3
  sessions" section adds a watch line from the newest open guidance item
  (`core/delivery/brief.py:346`, `_earnings_watch`), with no date check.
  - **Example.** STARHEALTH holds one guidance item dated 27 Oct, written before the fix. If
    STARHEALTH is held, the briefs of about 22–26 Oct show that text as the watch line for the
    27 Oct results, before the meeting has happened. The reviewer reproduced the shape on a
    fixture.
  - After FIX-004 no writer can create such a row, so only rows stored before it are affected.
- **Change 1 (todo): apply the digest's rule in `_earnings_watch`.** Choose the newest open
  guidance item dated on or before the brief's date. Tests: a future-dated item is never the
  watch line; and, for the digest, a dossier with 6 or more open guidance items, so that "filter,
  then take the last 5" is pinned (review I1). Its own implementation phase,
  then its own fresh review. It is worth landing before about 22 Oct; after 27 Oct it is only a
  guard.

## Change 1 (2026-10-10): accepted

Recorded in the [implementation receipt](../evidence/FIX-004-implementation.md), section
"Change 1". Committed as `61c85d6` and deployed before its review, at the owner's word. The
fresh review **accepted** the commit on 10 Oct ([review receipt](../evidence/FIX-004-review.md),
section "Change 1 fresh-session review"). It found nothing critical, high or medium, and it
upholds the "before" rule below. Its own trace sends briefs from 19 to 28 Oct through the real
brief job, between the curator's and the Saturday scan's writes, and the 27 Oct row never shows.
One test gap (I1) is routed to SA-031.
- **The watch line counts guidance dated before the brief's date, not on or before it.** This
  differs from the scope's wording ("the digest's rule"). The reason:
  - the brief goes out at 08:50 IST, Monday to Friday. The only writers of guidance are the
    curator at the 16:30 review and, on Saturdays, event ingestion and the research loop. So at
    08:50 no row dated that day can have been written yet by today's code;
  - the brief's earnings window includes the meeting day itself. With "on or before",
    STARHEALTH's stored 27 Oct row would still be the watch line on the 27 Oct morning, before
    the meeting. The window's briefs are Mon 26 and Tue 27 Oct, so that rule would fix only one of
    the two.
- **Same rule, one place.** The dossier gains `open_guidance(as_of)`: open items dated on or
  before `as_of`, in stored order. The digest lists its last 5. The brief takes its last item as
  of the day before the brief. "Latest" stays the latest stored, as before.
- **Example.** A held stock has results on Tue 27 Oct. Its dossier holds open guidance dated
  15 Sep and a pre-FIX-004 item dated 27 Oct. The briefs of 26 and 27 Oct show "watch: <the
  15 Sep item>"; from 28 Oct the 27 Oct item counts.
- **Tests.** A brief table and a sweep across the meeting, the review's L1 reproduction, one end
  to end build and render, and the review's I1: a digest with 7 known open items lists the newest 5.

## Rollout and rollback

A code change reaches production only on deploy; this card authorises no deploy, production
variable or data migration. Rollback: revert the commit. Stored rows written after the fix carry
past dates, so a revert leaves them valid.
