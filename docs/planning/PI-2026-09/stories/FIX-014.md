## What was found

PR-011's discovery signal `insider_buying` leaves out every promoter-group market **sale** while
promoters hold more than 75%, so that a sale made to keep 25% public does not count against the
company. It keeps the group's **purchases**. When the group both bought and sold in the window,
for example an inter-se transfer done through the market, the sale that offsets the purchase is
dropped and the purchase alone is scored.

- **Example (recorded filings, a supposed holding):** PATINTLOG's promoter group bought 2,63,832
  shares for ₹36,62,165 and sold 2,63,832 for ₹35,90,951 (filings 3886/3893, 23–29 Sep). The net
  is +₹71,214. At a 78% promoter holding the event stays positive and low with that net, because
  the event's 75% rule applies only to a net sale. Discovery scores **+₹36.6 lakh**, 51 times the
  event's figure.
- **Size:** on 23 Oct DALBHARAT's promoter group bought and sold ₹17.0 crore each. Above 75% that
  would score +17.0, the top of the signal, while its event is neutral.
- **Today it is latent.** On NSE's list for the 28 days to 23 Oct, 3 companies had both promoter
  purchases and sales (PATINTLOG 35.0%, DALBHARAT 55.84%, NATCOPHARM 49.39%), and none of the 17
  companies with a promoter sale was above 75%.

## Intended result

Above the cap, the promoter group's market net counts only when it is positive:
`max(0, promoter purchases − promoter sales)`. Directors' trades count as now.

- A pure sale still counts nothing (unchanged).
- A pure purchase counts in full (unchanged).
- A wash counts its net, if positive: PATINTLOG at 78% gives +₹71,214.
- A net sale counts nothing: ₹30 lakh sold and ₹20 lakh bought gives 0, not +₹20 lakh.
