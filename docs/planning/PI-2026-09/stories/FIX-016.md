## What was found

1. **A SELL verdict has no age limit.** `latest_actionable_verdict` takes the newest actionable or
   degraded analysis run on or before the review day, however old, and even one run before the
   buy. **Example:** you add a stock by hand although its analysis two weeks earlier said SELL.
   Ten sessions later `case_broken` sells it on that old SELL, against your own choice.
2. **A news article can sell alone.** `recent_negative_events` counts any high-materiality
   negative event. For news, the bulk-tier classifier
   ([classify.py](../../../../core/events/classify.py)) sets direction and materiality; only the
   insider, pledge, substantial-acquisition and ownership events are typed by fixed rules
   ([disclosures.py](../../../../core/events/disclosures.py)). One mislabelled article sells a
   holding.
3. **Sales are graded against the Nifty while the proceeds sit in cash.** `grade_disagreements`
   grades the excess over the Nifty. A plain EXIT's proceeds stay in cash until SA-049 (KT
   section 6, "Cash deployment"). **Example:** the stock falls 3% and the Nifty 5%. The grade says
   selling was wrong, yet selling into cash saved 3%.
4. **The gate counts hits, not money.** `gate_look` passes at 50% right. **Example:** right on 8 of
   15. The 8 saved 2% each and the 7 wrong ones cost 10% each: −3.6% per decision, and the gate
   passes. 50% of 15 is also what a rule with no skill reaches about half the time, so a pass
   shows "not worse", not "better".

## Intended result

1. **A fresh verdict.** A research verdict counts only when the analysis ran on a later IST day
   than the buy and within the last **22 sessions** up to the review day (a constant beside
   `CASE_EVENT_SESSIONS`). Every held stock is managed, and the 1st-of-month forecast re-analyses
   every managed stock, so each holding gets a fresh verdict every month. An analysis the buyer
   could have seen before buying never counts.
2. **Only a filed or rule-typed event sells.** An event from an NSE filing
   (`nse_announcement`, `nse_board_meeting`) or a rule-typed disclosure (insider, pledge,
   substantial acquisition, ownership) can make `case_broken`. A `news` event never sells on its
   own. It is recorded in the shadow row as a watch item, which PR-005 can use to mark a pillar
   challenged and PR-008 to show in the brief. The stops still protect in the meantime.
3. **The money's real alternative.** Each decision's benchmark is fixed on the decision day and
   stored in its row:
   - `cash` when the selling rule's verdict is a plain EXIT and the portfolio has no cash
     deployment (every portfolio until SA-049). Graded on the stock's own return.
   - `nifty` when it is a SWITCH (the proceeds were reinvested), or once SA-049's deployment is on
     for that portfolio. Graded on the excess over the Nifty, as today.
   The scorecard shows both numbers; the gate reads the row's benchmark. A later config change
   never re-grades a stored decision.
4. **A money check in the gate.** For each decision and horizon, the evidence rule's result is
   +gap when it kept the stock and −gap when it sold (gap = return against the row's benchmark).
   The gate passes only when, **at 10 and at 30 sessions**, the evidence rule is right on at least
   50% (unchanged) **and** its mean result is at least 0. A missing grade never helps it pass.
   - The pass reads **"not worse"**, and the switch recommendation stays on it (owner, 2026-10-24:
     not worse in count and money is enough, because the evidence rule is simpler and explains
     itself).
   - The line adds **"better"** when the rule is right on at least 70% at both horizons with a
     positive mean (11 of 15, unlikely by chance). It is a label only.
   - The gate constants are re-fixed with this story's date, still before any shadow row exists.

Not in this story: the stops, PR-005's case and its invalidation bounds, the label-accuracy bench
(PR-012), the loss-by-exit-reason line (PR-013).

## Acceptance criteria

- **Verdict:** a SELL run before the buy does not sell; a SELL 23 sessions old does not sell; a
  SELL run after the buy and 22 sessions old sells; a newer BUY over an older SELL does not sell.
- **Events:** a high-negative `news` event does not sell and appears as a watch item; the same
  event from `nse_announcement` sells; a rule-typed pledge invocation sells.
- **Benchmark:** stock −3%, Nifty −5%: a plain-EXIT decision grades selling as right (vs cash), a
  SWITCH decision grades it as wrong (vs the Nifty). The benchmark is read from the row; changing
  the config afterwards re-grades nothing.
- **Money check:** an independent oracle on hand-built populations: the 8-right/−3.6% example
  fails; a population at 50% with a positive mean passes; turning a graded decision into a missing
  one can never turn a fail into a pass; the "better" label appears only at 70% with a positive
  mean.
- PR-009's story file passes, with each changed contract named in the receipt.
- KT sections 6 and 7 state the rules as current design; guide 07-L and 09-H are updated;
  `check_kt_docs`; PDF. Story file, changed files and the full suite pass.
