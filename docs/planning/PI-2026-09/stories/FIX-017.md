# FIX-017 — The holding sync reads every NSE corporate-action row it is given, under NSE's code

fresh re-review (2026-10-29); routing calls delegated on 2026-10-13 · Estimate: **2 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. No dependency. Queued after
[FIX-015](FIX-015.md), at the end of the product track, so the owner's order is unchanged. **Move it
ahead** if a held stock lists one of the rows below with an ex-date after its buy, or if a
production holding is `TVSMOTORS` or `CANARABANK`. Evidence: **code inspection** and NSE's public
corporate-action lists for the 202 configured symbols, read on 25 and 29 Oct 2026 (read-only, no
production state read).

**Checked in production on 29 Oct 2026** (read-only, at the owner's go-ahead): one account
holds REDINGTON (bought 9 Oct) and WELCORP (bought 1 Sep). Neither is an alias, no bonus or
split has been applied, and NSE lists no row for either after its buy, so no holding is
affected today.

FIX-016 fixes the same rows for the exit rule's **grading** only. This card is the **live** holding
sync, which adjusts each holding's quantity and average price before the stops and targets run
(`corp_actions.sync_corp_actions` → `parse_action` → `apply_actions_to_holding`).

## What was found

1. **A row naming a bonus and a split is read as its bonus alone.** NSE's BAJFINANCE 2016 row:
   "Bonus 1:1/Face Value Split (Sub-Division) - From Rs 10/- Per Share To Rs 2/- Per Share". The
   quantity doubles where it should grow tenfold, so the holding shows about −80% and its stop
   fires.
2. **A bonus of preference shares or debentures is read as an equity bonus.** The share count does
   not change, yet the sync multiplies it:
   - ZEEL 2014, "Bonus Preference Shares 21:1": quantity ×22, average price ÷22;
   - TVSMOTOR 2025, "Scheme Of Arrangement - Bonus Ncrps 4:1": ×5;
   - DRREDDY 2011, "Scheme Of Arrangement - Bonus Debentures 6:1": ×7.
   The holding then shows a gain of hundreds or thousands of percent.
3. **A split in NSE's short wording is not seen.** JSWSTEEL 2017, "Fv Splt Frm Rs 10 To Re 1", and
   KNRCON 2016, "Fv Splt Frm Rs 10 To Rs 2", name neither "split" nor "sub-division". The quantity
   is not adjusted, so the holding shows −90% or −80%, and its stop fires.
4. **The sync asks NSE under the app's code.** `fetch_corp_actions(holding.symbol)` asks for
   `TVSMOTORS` and `CANARABANK`, which NSE does not list (0 rows on 29 Oct). Their registry codes
   `TVSMOTOR` and `CANBK` list 20 rows each, including Canara Bank's 1:5 split of 15 May 2024.
   The close is already read under the registry's code (`close_verifier.py`, `nse_symbol`).
   **Example:** a Canara Bank holding bought before May 2024 at ₹500 is still recorded at ₹500
   after the split, against a close near ₹100: −80%, and the stop sells it.

## Intended result

- The sync fetches NSE's list under `symbol_resolver.nse_symbol(holding.symbol)`.
- A row is applied as a ratio only when it is a bonus of equity shares or a face-value split it
  fully reads. A row naming a bonus and a split applies both ratios, or holds the symbol with a
  named gap; the implementer decides and records it.
- A bonus of preference shares, debentures or NCRPS is never a share-count change. It is a payout
  like a dividend, or a named gap.
- A row in NSE's short split wording is a split.
- A row the sync cannot read is logged, as today, and leaves the holding unchanged, with a named
  data-health line.
- The grading record (`action_record`, FIX-016) and the sync agree on every row.

## Acceptance criteria

- A table test of every NSE subject quoted above, plus the plain bonus, split and dividend rows,
  each with its expected effect on a holding, worked by hand.
- A Canara Bank holding through the real sync, with a fixture that answers only NSE's own code.
- `apply_actions_to_holding` stays idempotent by action key; a row already applied under the old
  reading is not applied twice. The implementer states what happens to such holdings.
- KT section on corporate actions, CODEBASE row, a guide case; story file, changed files and the
  full suite pass.
