## What was found

1. **One decision can block the gate for good.** The gate counts every decision whose 30 sessions
   have ended, with no window. A decision with no result at 10 or 30 sessions makes its money check
   fail. Some results can never be known: a demerger or scheme between the two closes, a bonus or
   split the record cannot read, a basis change the instrument registry records, or a stock NSE
   lists nothing for.
   **Example:** the shadow makes 20 decisions over four months. One is a sale of a stock that
   demerges three sessions later. From then on the gate fails every week, even if the other 19
   show the evidence rule clearly better.
2. **The most common cause is an empty list.** On 29 Oct NSE answered with an empty list for 14 of
   the 202 configured symbols, read under NSE's codes: AAVAS, ACCCEMENT, APL, DEEPAKNITRI, GATI,
   ICICIPRULI, IDEAFORGE, LTIM, MAHINDHOTEL, MCDOWELL-N, MTAR, PVR, STARHEALTH and ZOMATO. Code
   lookups are [FIX-019](FIX-019.md).
   **Inference, assuming decisions fall evenly across the 202:** among 15 decisions, at least one
   lands on these 14 about two times in three (1 − (188/202)^15 ≈ 0.66).
3. **Grading cannot tell an empty list from a failed fetch.** `fetch_corp_actions` returns `[]` for
   both. So a fetch that failed on one Sunday reads the same as a list that is empty for good.

## Intended result

1. **Two kinds of "no result".** Each ungraded grade is marked as lasting or temporary.
   - **Lasting:** re-reading next week cannot change it.
     - NSE's list was read and is empty.
     - The list starts after the decision's close.
     - A bonus or split went ex between the close's bar and the review day.
     - A share-count row was not read as a ratio.
     - Two ratios fall on one day.
     - A change no ratio converts, or a row of no known kind, lies between the closes (the record's
       `basis_changes`).
     - The registry records a new basis (`registry_break`).
   - **Temporary:** everything else.
     - The list could not be read.
     - No close, an undated close, or a close from before the decision.
     - A Yahoo-only close with a bonus or split after its bar.
     - Any other exception.
2. **A decision with a lasting gap is set aside, at both horizons.** This applies when the gap is
   at 10 sessions, at 30, or at both. The gate judges the decisions that are left: the 15-decision
   minimum, the 8-week span, the share right, the money check and the one-stock share.
3. **A cap.** At most `GATE_MAX_SET_ASIDE = 2` decisions may be set aside, and at most
   `GATE_MAX_SET_ASIDE_PCT = 10%` of the decisions past 30 sessions.
   - Beyond the cap, the gate's status is **"cannot judge"**. It replaces only a pass. A look that
     already fails or does not have enough decisions keeps that status.
   - "Cannot judge" is never a pass, so it breaks the two-week run (`recommends`).
4. **Named.** The look stores each decision set aside, with its stock, decision day and reason.
   The scorecard line names each one, for example: "set aside: XYZ, decided 3 Nov, a demerger ex
   12 Nov".
5. **Temporary gaps fail as today.** Such a decision counts as not right, and that week's money
   check fails.
6. **The fetch tells failure from empty.** Grading's read of NSE's list separates a failed read
   (temporary) from an empty answer (lasting). The implementer decides how and records it. The
   holding sync keeps today's `[]` for both until [FIX-017](FIX-017.md).
7. **The constants are fixed with this story's date,** still before any shadow row exists.

**Examples** (each population right on at least 50%, with a mean of +0.8% at both horizons, and no
stock above 30%):

| Decisions past 30 sessions | Set aside | Gate today | Gate after this story |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 graded + 1 demerger | 1 of 16 (6%) | fails every week | judged on 15: passes, "not worse" |
| 15 graded + 2 set aside | 2 of 17 (12%) | fails every week | cannot judge (above 10%) |
| 18 graded + 2 set aside | 2 of 20 (10%) | fails every week | judged on 18 |
| 14 graded + 1 set aside | 1 of 15 | fails every week | not enough (14 of the 15 it needs) |
| 15 graded + 1 set aside + 1 with no NSE close this Sunday | 1 of 17 | fails | fails this week (the 16th has no result) |
| any size with 3 set aside | 3 | fails every week | cannot judge |

Not in this story:
- A stock whose close stays missing (suspended, delisted) still fails the money check, as the KT
  limit says, unless the registry records a new basis.
- The holding sync's reading of NSE's rows (FIX-017).
- Mapping the stale codes (FIX-019).

## Acceptance criteria

- **An independent oracle.** Hand-built populations with the gate status worked by hand: every row
  of the table above, plus three set aside at 20, 30 and 50 decisions (cannot judge each time).
- **Invariants:**
  - Turning a graded decision into a temporary gap never turns a fail, "not enough" or "cannot
    judge" into a pass (as today).
  - No look passes with more set aside than the cap, whatever the remaining decisions show.
  - Which decisions are set aside depends only on the stock's prices, NSE's list and the registry.
    Swapping which rule sold leaves the same decisions set aside.
  - A decision graded at 10 sessions with a lasting gap at 30 is set aside at both horizons.
- **Through the real price path** (FIX-016's market model):
  - A demerger between the closes sets the decision aside.
  - An empty NSE list sets it aside; a failed read fails the week.
  - A Yahoo-only close fails the week.
- **The sync is unchanged:** it still receives `[]` for a failed fetch and for an empty list.
- **Regression:** PR-009's and FIX-016's story files pass, with each changed contract named in the
  receipt.
- **Documentation:** KT section 6 (the gate, and the limit that one decision fails it "for good",
  now stated as the cap), guide cases 07-L and 09-H, `check_kt_docs`, the PDF.
- The story file, the changed files and the full suite pass.

## Hard review

Can something the evidence rule did or read decide which decisions are set aside? The rule reads
NSE filings, so it may sell on a scheme announcement, the very event that sets the decision aside.
The cap bounds this; the review checks that nothing else does. Is the cap the only route by which a
missing result can help? Can a temporary gap be taken for a lasting one, such as a failed fetch read
as an empty list? Can "cannot judge" ever count toward the two-week run?
