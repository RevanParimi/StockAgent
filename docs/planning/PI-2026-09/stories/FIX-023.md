## What was found (SA-049's review)

**M1, medium: the regime that deployment trusts can be made of fallback values.**

- **How a fallback becomes a label.** `RegimeDetector` (`core/intelligence/regime/detector.py`)
  returns `VIX_FALLBACK` (17.0) when Yahoo's `^INDIAVIX` answers nothing (lines 147-156), and
  `FII_PROXY_FALLBACK` (0.0) when `^NSEI` does (158-174). When `detect` raises, the daily
  review keeps a default NORMAL snapshot (`daily_review.py:555`).
- **How it is stored.** `update_sticky_regime` stores whichever label the first review of the
  day brings (`daily_review.py:592`).
- **How it is spent.** Deployment reads that label through `label_recorded_for`.
- **Reproduced** through the real detector, the real state file and the real executor
  (`analysis_data/sa049_review/test_rv_sa049.py`, `test_rv2_*`). The portfolio is ₹12 L, 30%
  invested, with three qualifying ideas:

| Yahoo answers | Live inputs say | Label recorded | Deployment |
|---|---|---|---|
| VIX 30, Nifty −3.0% over 5 sessions | MACRO_CRISIS | MACRO_CRISIS | buys nothing |
| `^INDIAVIX` empty, Nifty −3.0% | MACRO_CRISIS | RISK_OFF (VIX 17.0) | buys 2 × 83 shares, to 43.8% |
| `^INDIAVIX` and `^NSEI` empty | unknown | NORMAL | buys toward 80% and the ₹10 L floor |

- **Bound.** The sticky layer keeps a crisis it has already entered for two milder days. The
  third (`RL_REGIME_CALM_DAYS`, 3) exits it. So one or two dark days inside a crisis buy
  nothing. A dark day at a crisis's onset does buy, and so does a third dark day in a row. Each
  day opens at most 2 positions.
- **Frequency: unmeasured.** Yahoo answered all three tickers at 09:08 IST on 2 Nov (the
  reviewer's probe). No production log ("VIX fetch failed") was read.

**L3, low (issue time): the label written for a review session is read from the run day's bar.**

- **When the label is read.** The 16:30 job on day T reviews the previous session
  (`trading_days_ago(T, 1)`), after T's own session has closed. The detector takes `iloc[-1]`
  of Yahoo's history whatever `as_of_date` says.
- **What that means.** A buy booked at session D's close is decided on D+1's VIX and Nifty. A
  crash on D+1 that turns the label severe withholds the buy at D's close, and the paper record
  is flattered. Pinned by `test_rv2b_*`.
- **Not new.** The advisor's ADD gate and its crisis exit read the same label.

## Intended result

- **A label built on a fallback opens no position.**
  - The detector reports which inputs fell back. A failed fetch gives no value, never a
    midpoint.
  - The daily review records that with the day's sticky update.
  - Deployment reads such a day as no regime. Nothing opens, and the run says why. Example:
    "market regime unavailable for 2026-10-30: India VIX did not answer".
  - A real VIX of exactly 17.0 is not a fallback.
  - Decide and record whether the learning loop's use of the label changes.
- **L3: decide the label's session.** Either read each input's bar on or before the review
  session, so the label for D is D's, or keep the run day's bar and say so in the KT. Record the
  choice, and pin it with a test.

## Acceptance

- RV2's shapes: with `^INDIAVIX` empty, no position opens and the reason names the input; with
  live inputs the MACRO_CRISIS control is unchanged.
- A crisis already entered stays (RV2's bound).
- A detector exception (the NORMAL default) opens no position either.
- L3 decided and pinned (RV2b's shape).
- KT section 6 "Cash deployment" (the regime paragraph) and section 12's row; guide 07-G gains
  the dark-input case.

## Routed input — 2026-11-02 SA-049 re-review (RR-L1, low)

SA-049's rework 1 bounds the **dive** by the review session (`buy_candidates`: latest dive on or
before it). The **close** a buy is priced at can still be older than the dive.

- **How.** `session_close` takes the latest bar on or before the session asked for. When the
  review session's bar is missing, it carries an earlier one forward. A manual
  `POST /portfolio/run-advisor` with no date, run before the day's close, does the same. In
  `record` mode (the checked-in mode), SA-003's gate only notes "its close is from bar …, not
  the review session", and the buy goes ahead.
- **Reproduced** through the real pipeline
  (`analysis_data/sa049_rereview1/test_rr_sa049.py::test_rr3_*`). Tuesday's run reviews Mon 2
  Nov, but NEWX has no Monday bar, so its close is Friday 30 Oct's (₹1,000). NEWX was first
  dived on Sat 31 Oct. Record mode buys 83 × ₹1,000, dated 2 Nov, at a close from before the
  dive. Enforce withholds it.
- **Frequency: unmeasured.** It needs a missing bar for the review session at the next day's
  16:30 run, or a manual run before the close. SWITCH legs share the path. That part is
  pre-existing (PR-004, SA-003 record mode).
- **Intended.** A buy that opens a position (a deployment buy or a SWITCH leg) whose close's bar
  is before the idea's latest dive (or the watchlist analysis day) opens nothing, in either
  gate mode. The reason names both dates, for example "close of 2026-10-30 is before the deep
  dive of 2026-10-31". A carried-forward bar on or after the dive keeps SA-003's record-mode
  behaviour.
- **Acceptance.** RR3's shape buys nothing in record mode, and the reason names both dates. A
  bar on the dive's day or later is unchanged. The KT section 6 sentence (SA-049's commit-time
  edit) moves from a limit to the rule.
- **RR-T1 (low test gap, pre-existing PR-004).** The watchlist lane's own bound
  (`watchlist_idea`: `age < 0`) is pinned only by ignored reviewer files. Mutant
  `watchlist_no_lower_bound` survives the SA-049 story file, PR-004's file and the SA-003
  portfolio and pipeline files. Add one tracked case: a watchlist BUY analysed after the review
  session is not offered, and the next run offers it.
