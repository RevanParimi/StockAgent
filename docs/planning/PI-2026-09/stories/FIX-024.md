## What was found (FIX-023's review)

**M1, medium (issue time, pre-existing: PR-004's watchlist lane and SA-049's date bound): an
analysis made after the review session's close is booked at that close.**

- **The rule today.** The 16:30 job on day T reviews the previous session D and books its buys
  at D's close. A candidate counts for D when its deep dive or analysis is dated on or before D
  (`buy_candidates`, [candidates.py](../../../../core/discovery/candidates.py) lines 154-159 and
  222). FIX-023's `close_before_dive` passes a bar on the dive's day. Both compare **days**.
- **The shape (RV5).** A user's watchlist analysis of W runs on Mon 2 Nov at 20:00 IST (after
  NSE's 15:30 close) and gives BUY. Tuesday's run reviews Monday: W is offered, its band comes
  from Monday's close (₹500), and a SWITCH leg (or deployment, once on) buys W at ₹500, dated
  2 Nov. The analysis read news from after that price. In results season companies file after
  hours, so the gap can hold the quarter's main news.
- **Where it reaches.** SWITCH destinations (live), idle-cash deployment (ships off) and the exit
  shadow's switch test all read the one list. The weekly discovery dives on Saturdays, so shelf
  ideas meet it only through the owner's manual `POST /discovery/run` on a weekday evening.
- **Not FIX-023's defect.** FIX-023's card keeps "a bar on the dive's day" by design.

**L1, low: on a day the regime is unavailable, ADD reads the last established label (RV6).**

- On such a day the review keeps the sticky label and writes it into the feedback log the
  advisor reads ([daily_review.py](../../../../core/intelligence/rl/workflows/daily_review.py)
  lines 613 and 1611; [advisor.py](../../../../core/portfolio/advisor.py) lines 239 and 581).
- **Shape.** Sticky NORMAL from Thursday. Friday: India VIX dark, the Nifty down 3% in five
  sessions. FIX-023 records Friday unavailable and keeps NORMAL, so an UP envelope with 7-day
  accuracy 0.70 ADDs. Before FIX-023, the fallback VIX of 17.0 recorded RISK_OFF, which closed
  ADD. Deployment opens nothing that day; ADD adds 25% of a holding.
- **The crisis exit** (`crisis_regime_bearish`) reads the same kept label. Through a run of dark
  days a crisis already entered stays, so that exit stays armed. FIX-023 intends this (F23-D3).
- Bounded: the 10% position cap, the 5-session cooldown, an UP envelope and accuracy ≥ 0.60.

**L2, low (fails closed): a fresh state file loses its own established day (RV3).**

- With no state file (the first run) or a corrupt one, an established review writes a fresh
  state with no `_last_update` marker
  ([state.py](../../../../core/intelligence/regime/state.py) line 164). `label_recorded_for`
  counts it for that day (by `since`). A later dark review the same day does not see the day as
  established (line 228 compares only the marker), so it marks the day unavailable.
- **Shape.** No state; Friday's first ticker answers NORMAL; the second finds India VIX empty.
  Friday then has no label, and deployment opens nothing.

## Intended result

- **M1.** A buy at session D's close counts only an analysis or dive made before D's close
  (15:30 IST). One made later waits for the next session's review. A watchlist analysis has
  its `run_at`. A shelf dive dated a trading day, with no time, counts as made after that
  day's close. A Saturday dive is unchanged. The reason names both times, for example "analysis
  of 2026-11-02 20:00 IST is after the close of 2026-11-02". The same rule applies to the
  SWITCH destination, the SWITCH leg, deployment and the shadow's switch test.
- **L1.** On a day whose regime is unavailable, ADD is held with a note naming the day and
  the reason (as SA-003's DATA_GATE holds ADD). Decide, and record, whether the crisis exit
  keeps reading the kept label (recommended: yes, since a sale is never blocked by a missing
  input).
- **L2.** `record_unavailable_day` treats a state established for its day the way
  `label_recorded_for` does: a fresh state with `since` equal to the day is established.
