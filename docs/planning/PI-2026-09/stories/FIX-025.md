## Owner decisions (2026-11-04, "agreed on all")

> "No need hard 10 positions, the LLM has its intelligence to decide, because in real time when
> a user builds trust on this product based on the current portfolio profits, he invests more
> and the hard 10 is silly. I may have told to be it configurable but you can consider like a
> minimum."

- **D1. The LLM decides which stocks, and so how many.** Holdings are as many as the ideas the
  analysis rates BUY that pass the checks, up to `portfolio.max_holdings` (15).
  `portfolio.min_holdings` (10) becomes a **soft minimum**: the run says when it is not met and
  why ("8 holdings, below the 10 aimed for: only 8 qualifying ideas"), but it never forces a
  buy.
- **D2. The rules decide how much.**
  - The target invested is the regime's share of total equity (80 / 50 / 0).
  - The ₹10 L `portfolio.min_invested_inr` no longer raises it.
  - A new position asks for a share of equity, the calm share divided by
    `portfolio.target_holdings` (80% ÷ 12 ≈ 6.7%), so it grows when the user adds money.
  - The caps and the cash floor still apply.
  - Sizing by conviction (bigger for stronger ideas) waits until the weekly scorecard shows
    that conviction predicts returns. On 11 Oct, 13 of 21 shelf ideas beat the Nifty at 30
    sessions: a small sample.
- **D3. A top-up checks NSE's surveillance report too.** An ADD into a stock that NSE lists
  under any ASM or GSM stage, or as not active, is skipped. The shares are kept (no forced
  sale), and the note says why: "top-up skipped: under LT-ASM in NSE's surveillance report of
  2026-11-03". With no report from the last 7 days, no top-up either, as for a new position.
- **D4. A sold stock is re-analysed the next Saturday.**
  - Today a shelf idea the autopilot sold waits for its routine re-analysis, which runs once
    its last analysis is 30 days old: a few days to about 5 weeks.
  - Instead, a stock sold since its latest analysis is due for re-analysis on the next
    Saturday, inside the weekly analysis budget.
  - If it is still a BUY, it can be bought again from the first run that may use that analysis
    (FIX-024's rule). If not, the idea leaves the shelf as today.

## Owner decisions (2026-11-06, answering the implementation's three questions)

> "no position is a hard rule, its all about investments turning inti profits"
> "even the 2L"
> "3 - do as per suggested"

- **A thirteenth position is fine.** Whole shares usually leave twelve positions just short of
  the target, so a thirteenth opens and invested ends near 86%. The number of positions is not the
  aim (F25-D5 stands).
- **No ₹2 L deposit.** No rule needs it, and SA-049's rollout loses that step. The deposit action
  stays, for any later money. At ₹10 L the target is ₹8 L and a position 66 shares at ₹1,000; ten
  buys from the 27 Sep shape reach ₹8.5 L (85%).
- **A stock sold after its idea left the shelf comes back through the screen (F25-D14).** The
  implementation had written no suggestion for this question, so the choice is the implementer's,
  under the owner's delegation. Once the autopilot has sold a stock and no portfolio holds it, the
  managed skip no longer applies, and it takes its rank in the screen like any other stock.
  Dived 1 Aug, idea rotated out 3 Oct, sold 14 Oct: if the screen ranks it on Sat 17 Oct it is
  analysed, and at 0.55 or more it is a new idea. It jumps no queue and adds no analyst call a
  screen rank would not make.
- **Asked, not assumed:** `portfolio.max_holdings` (15), from D1 above, stays until the owner says
  otherwise. It only binds when money arrives on a full book or holdings have shrunk.

## Intended result

- `deployment.target`: the regime's share only. `stop_reason`: never past the daily limit or
  `max_holdings`; otherwise open while invested is below the target. A buy can then pass the
  target by at most one position everywhere, and the card's original property holds without
  SA-049's T3 exception.
- `Breadth.position_inr`: total equity × the NORMAL share ÷ `target_holdings`. Decide and
  record whether a position bought in RISK_OFF uses the same size (recommended: yes; RISK_OFF
  only stops sooner).
- `portfolio.min_invested_inr`: no longer used by deployment. Remove it from config and
  settings, or keep it for reporting only; record the choice.
- The run's record: the soft-minimum sentence replaces "holdings floor unreachable" and
  "invested floor unreachable".
- The ADD path in `_execute_buys` reads the run's one surveillance report (already read once a
  run) before sizing, and the skip goes into the ADD's log and the digest.
- Discovery's re-analysis selection (`ShelfStore.redive_due`) puts an active idea whose stock
  the autopilot sold on or after its latest dive first, ahead of the 30-day ones, within the
  budget.
