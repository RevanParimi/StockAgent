## What was found

**M1, medium: a holding with no review entry can top up on a recorded RISK_OFF or MACRO_CRISIS day.**
FIX-025's L2 asks `label_recorded_for(day)` when the holding has no entry of its own for the day
([advisor.py](../../../../core/portfolio/advisor.py) lines 258-261). It acts only on None. Any other
answer is dropped, and `sig.regime_label` stays the older entry's.

*Example.* AAA's own review failed today. Its last entry, from yesterday, says NORMAL. Another stock's
review recorded MACRO_CRISIS for today. ADD refuses both RISK_OFF and MACRO_CRISIS (line 601), but it
reads NORMAL, so 25% more of AAA is bought. The crisis exit (line 574) reads the same older label, so
it does not fire either.

**M2, medium: a SWITCH can buy a stock the autopilot sold the day before, with no new analysis.** The
re-buy rule, "buying a stock back after a sale needs a new dive", is only in deployment's check
([autopilot.py](../../../../core/portfolio/autopilot.py) line 447). The SWITCH leg (line 277) and
`evaluate_switch_candidates` do not read the ledger. A SWITCH is live with the deployment switch off.

*Example.* X is dived on Sat 31 Oct. Tuesday's run sells X. On Wednesday's run Y exits, and X is still
the shelf's best idea: Y's money buys X back, paying costs on both sides, on the analysis the sale
already overruled.

**M3, medium: a stock in the daily review is rarely offered from the watchlist.** This comes from
FIX-024's rule meeting the daily review, and it fails safe: nothing wrong is bought. The 16:30 job on
day T reviews session T-1. A review re-runs the stock's analysis
([daily_review.py](../../../../core/intelligence/rl/workflows/daily_review.py) line 773), at about
16:35 IST on T, and writes it to the score history, unless the forecast's direction was right and its
error under 0.5%. The pipeline then builds the list for T-1, and `watchlist_idea` reads only the newest
analysis ([candidates.py](../../../../core/discovery/candidates.py) line 159). Unless that is at least
two days old, it is after T-1's close, so the stock "waits for a review of a later session".

*Example.* W is on your own watchlist and in the daily review, a BUY at 0.70 every day. Monday's job
reads Monday's 16:35 analysis for Friday's close: late. Tuesday's reads Tuesday's for Monday's: late. W
is offered only after two reviews in a row skip their analysis. How often that happens is **not
measured**: the local logs hold only 10 reviews, 1 of which would have skipped. KT section 6's example,
"first offered by Wednesday's run", holds for a stock with no newer analysis: one outside the daily
review, or a weekly one between its runs.

**Fix:** read the newest analysis made before the review session's close, not the newest of all.

**M4, low today; the owner chose Option A: a stock on the user's own watchlist can come back days after its sale.**
A sale adds a watchlist row with source "autopilot", which never qualifies, only when the stock has no
row ([autopilot.py](../../../../core/portfolio/autopilot.py) line 196). A row the user added stays
"user", and any analysis newer than the sale counts as a new dive. M3 mostly hides this today. With M3
fixed it is the normal path, and the stock stays in the daily review ([FIX-028](FIX-028.md) keeps a
watchlist stock there).

*Example, as it can happen today.* You added W yourself, and the autopilot bought it. Tuesday's job
sells W at Monday's close. Wednesday's review re-runs the analysis at 16:35: still a BUY. Thursday's and
Friday's reviews skip theirs. Friday's job reviews Thursday and reads Wednesday's analysis, which is
before Thursday's close and newer than the sale: with deployment on, W is bought back at Thursday's
close, three sessions after it was sold, and nothing limits repeats. So KT section 6's "at most once a
week" holds only for a shelf idea.

- **Option A, the owner's choice of 2026-11-06:** the same bound as a shelf idea. A stock the autopilot
  sold is not offered from the watchlist until the Saturday after the sale, however new its analysis is.
- **Option B, not chosen:** leave it. The stock is on your own list, so a fresh BUY is enough, and the scorecard
  line in [PR-013](PR-013.md) shows what the round trips cost.

**L1, low: a shelf idea's verdict is not read.** The owner's D1 says "the ideas the analysis rates
BUY", and D4 says "if it is still a BUY". The shelf's rule is `final_score` at or above 0.55, the
model's number. The verdict is bound to the composite, a separate number (KT section 3). So an idea
with verdict NEUTRAL or SELL at conviction 0.70 stays on the shelf
([shelf.py](../../../../core/discovery/shelf.py) line 367), is offered
([candidates.py](../../../../core/discovery/candidates.py) line 244) and is bought. A watchlist idea
needs both. **Measured, before FIX-013's model change:** in the local `data/rl/verdict_shadow.jsonl`
(1,541 analyses, 17 Jul to 27 Sep), all 1,364 with a score at or above 0.55 had a BUY bound verdict, so
the two have not disagreed yet. This closes the gap before they do.

## Intended result

- **M1:** with no entry of its own for the day, the recorded label replaces the older one in
  `sig.regime_label`; None still holds the ADD, as now.
- **M3:** `watchlist_idea` reads the newest analysis made before the review session's close, by a
  score-history reader that takes that bound.
- **M2, M4 (Option A), L1:** one rule in `buy_candidates`, the list every door reads. A stock sold
  since its latest analysis is not offered (the SWITCH then takes its next-best idea, not a plain
  EXIT). From the watchlist, a stock the autopilot sold counts only an analysis made on or after the
  Saturday after the sale. Neither is a shelf idea whose verdict is not a BUY. An idea with no stored verdict is
  re-dived first, not bought, as a pre-gate idea is. `apply_redives` drops a re-dive that is no longer
  a BUY, with the reason.
- KT section 6's and PR-013's "at most once a week" then hold, or say which doors they cover.

## Acceptance

  their strict xfail marks removed.
- `decide` over the regime labels and an own or older entry: an older label never allows what today's
  recorded label refuses, and today's calm label frees what an older crisis label held.
- A sold stock is offered again by the first run that may use a new dive (FIX-024's rule), and a
  hand sale still blocks as it does in deployment today.
