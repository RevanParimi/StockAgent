## What it costs

On Sat 14 Nov the deep dive asked about **DEEPA**. The orchestrator analysed **Deepak Nitrite**
(`DEEPAKNTR`, BUY 0.64), and the shelf filed that BUY under DEEPA, with DEEPA's close (₹219.48)
and entry band. Tue 17 Nov's run bought 308 DEEPA at Monday's close, ₹211.86 (₹65,253): a stock nobody analysed
as itself, on another company's case. Its thesis's levels (₹1,508, ₹1,602) are Deepak Nitrite's.
The one analysis of DEEPA that day ("Deepak Automobiles Ltd") said NEUTRAL 0.51. By 19 Nov it was
−7.0%. Any Saturday dive of a symbol outside the managed and tracked lists can do it again, and
Saturday's ideas are first bought by Tuesday's run.

## Why

`run_deep_dives` (`core/discovery/deep_dive.py`) calls `get_orchestrator(route).analyse(symbol)`.
`_resolve_ticker` (`src/backend/shared/pipeline/base_orchestrator.py`) treats any symbol outside
the managed and tracked lists as free text and asks a model which listed company it means; the
model answered `DEEPAKNTR`. The dive then builds its `DeepDiveResult` from `cand.symbol`, never
comparing it with `report.ticker`. SA-003's gate checks the price's identity, not the report's.

## Intended result

1. **An exact NSE symbol is not re-resolved.** The screen's candidates, a shelf re-dive and a
   watchlist analysis pass a listed symbol; the orchestrator analyses that symbol (checked against
   the exchange's list, as `_verify_ticker` does) and never asks a model to guess. Free text from
   the chat or the Analyze box keeps the resolver.
2. **A report about another company is refused.** If `report.ticker` differs from the symbol asked
   for, the dive keeps no idea and no memo entry for it, counts it, and logs both symbols ("DEEPA:
   the analysis is of DEEPAKNTR; not shelved"). The same check on every door that buys from an
   analysis: the shelf, the watchlist candidate, a SWITCH leg and deployment, so an idea already
   misfiled is not bought again.
3. **The misfiled idea now on the shelf.** DEEPA's active idea: re-dived as DEEPA by the first
   Saturday run after the deploy, or dropped with that reason. A one-off read lists any other
   (probe 5 of the read found none among 20).

## Acceptance

- A dive whose orchestrator returns another ticker shelves nothing, with the reason; a fixture
  with the DEEPA → DEEPAKNTR answer.
- A listed symbol outside the managed list makes no resolver call (the transport is blocked).
- Every buy door refuses an idea whose analysis is of another symbol (a planted shelf row).
- Living documents: KT sections 4 and 8; a testing-guide case.
