# MAP-002 — The 3D world and the detail panel

Priority: **P2** · Scope: **owner request** of 2026-11-23 · Estimate: **5 points** · Lane:
**light** (developer documentation and tooling; nothing the app runs, ships or reads)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependency: [MAP-001](MAP-001.md).

## Why

The owner wants the flow of diagram 0.2 "visually stunning", in 3D, "to check multi
directions", with depth of detail. The 3D world shows where things are and how they connect; a
flat panel beside it keeps the logic readable.

## What it does

1. **The viewer project**, `tools/architecture_map/viewer/`: its own `package.json` and lockfile
   (versions pinned; `npm ci` reproduces it), Vite, React 18, three, @react-three/fiber,
   @react-three/drei, @react-three/postprocessing, elkjs and a single-file build plugin. It reads
   `docs/architecture-map/model.json` at build time and writes **one self-contained file**,
   `docs/architecture-map/index.html`: it opens by double click, offline, and makes no network
   request. `node_modules` stays ignored.
2. **The world**, laid out from the model (plane by kind, column by flow order; no hand-placed
   coordinates): the three planes (what people see; the product flow; storage), modules as
   glowing blocks, flows as tubes with moving particles in the flow's direction, storage pipes
   from writers to stores, the outer ring for M11 and M12 with dotted beams to what they watch,
   and M1 as the base frame.
3. **Camera presets** 1 to 4 (Front, Top, Side, Below; 5, Clock, arrives with MAP-003), `R`
   reset, `/` search, `Esc`, `?` help; limited orbit; a click flies the camera to its target.
4. **Click-to-focus:** unrelated things fade, the module unfolds into its sub-modules, the panel
   opens. Hovering a flow shows what travels on it. Deep links `#m=<id>`.
5. **The detail panel:** the sub-module's logic tree as an ELK-laid flowchart (decisions as
   diamonds, outcomes as ends), its files and functions, config keys with repository default and
   cited production value, stores read and written, jobs, flows in and out. The renderer is
   generic over the model; this card checks it in depth on the money path: M3 (3.1 to 3.5), M4.2,
   M5.2, M5.3 and M6 (6.1 to 6.5).
6. **Audience toggle:** manager (plain words) and developer (paths, functions, keys).
7. Labels are HTML overlays facing the camera; no long text is drawn in 3D.
8. **Look and checklist first** (design, "Look and usability"): before drawing anything, write
   `docs/architecture-map/DESIGN.md` (colours per module and plane, type, glow limits, panel and
   flowchart styles, motion, contrast) and `docs/architecture-map/UI-CHECKLIST.md` (from Vercel's
   Web Interface Guidelines, in our words, at a recorded commit; never fetched at build or test
   time).
