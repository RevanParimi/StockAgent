## Why

The product is driven by its clock: the 16:30 job reviews session T-1 and books trades at T-1's
close, discovery runs on Saturday, the forecast on the 1st. A developer or a manager understands
the product fastest by watching a day happen, then one stock's life, then a short tour.

## What it does

1. **The model** gains, curated and checked by the guard: each job's flows and modules (what
   lights up when it runs); the trace (the seed's finale: Saturday dive, shelf, Tuesday buy, daily
   reviews, exit, Sunday scorecard) as steps naming modules and flows; the tour, about 8 stops
   (camera preset, target, a one-line caption).
2. **The clock** (preset 5): a ring of the day's jobs at their generated times, with weekly and
   monthly tracks; a slider with play, pause and speed. At each job's time its modules glow and
   particles run along its flows; the panel names the job, its trigger and its gate flag.
3. **Trace a stock:** step by step with captions, Next and Back, the path highlighted.
4. **Guided tour:** the default start in manager mode; Next, Back, autoplay; `Esc` leaves it.

## Acceptance

- The guard fails on a job without a mapping, and on a trace or tour step naming something
  absent (unit tests on fixtures).
- The Playwright test grows: playing the day visits every job marker; the trace and the tour run
  to the end without a console error.
- Screenshots (scale 2) of the clock at 16:30, a trace step and a tour stop, shown to the owner.
- The clock, trace and tour follow `docs/architecture-map/DESIGN.md` (a new visual element is
  added to it first), and the slider, Next, Back and autoplay work from the keyboard. The receipt
  lists each UI-CHECKLIST.md item for what this card adds as passed or not applicable.
