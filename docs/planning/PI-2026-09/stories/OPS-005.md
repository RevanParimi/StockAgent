## What it does

Once a day, append one line to `/home/coder/project/SESSION_LOG.md`: **peak anon, OOM count,
restarts, RC exits, and MB freed by the sweep**. **No new daemon:** it is computed when the
autopilot or a session starts.

## Known before starting

- Sources: `~/devbox/mem.log` (one sample a minute, so a short peak can be missed) and
  `~/devbox/events.log` (`BOOT`, `OOM_KILL`, `ANOMALY`, `RC_EXIT`). Their times are UTC; say which
  day the line covers, in IST.
- **MB freed by the sweep: no source found yet.** On 26 Nov neither `mem.log` nor `events.log`
  names the sweep, and `boot.log` only says it started. Find where it is recorded among the logs.
  If it is recorded nowhere, the line says "not recorded" and the owner is asked; it is not
  estimated, and `~/devbox` is not changed. What can be seen: on 26 Nov `file` fell from 1,588 to
  588 MB between 13:40:21 and 13:41:16 with nothing running, which looks like a sweep (an
  inference).
- A restart is counted by its time (the `BOOT` line's time less `pid1_up`; see OPS-004).
- A session start needs a trigger that is not a daemon: a CLAUDE.md instruction or a session-start
  hook. Choose in the story.

## Constraints (owner, 26 Nov 2026)

Do not modify `~/devbox/*` or `~/entrypoint.d/*`; read their logs only. No new background
processes. Any new log is capped. Read-only checks except the test runs this story needs. Leave
the web link and the tmux sessions `rc`, `memlog`, `sweep` and `login-b` untouched.
