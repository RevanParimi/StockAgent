# PR-002 — A weekly results scorecard with a "sold too early" check

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **planned**, product results track · Provisional estimate: **3 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: none. Source: section 1
of the [product results review](../../../audit/2026-10-11-product-results-review.md), owner
request of 2026-10-11 ("we need to track at least weekly to see if it's wrong").

## Intended result

Every Sunday the owner, and every member, sees in plain words whether the portfolio is making
money, whether its sales were good, and whether anything crossed a red line.

**Why now.** The nightly auditor already grades each EXIT, TRIM and SWITCH against the benchmark
at 10, 30 and 60 sessions (`core/audit/outcomes.py`, `excess_pct`), but no report a person reads
shows it. The weekly email's 28-day "advice scoreboard" drops exited stocks (September audit,
F06), so it cannot answer "did we sell too early?".

**Example (from the July snapshot).** 8 measurable sales. Over the next 20 sessions, 6 of the
stocks beat the Nifty; the median excess was +3.0 points. The scorecard line would read:
"Sold too early: 6 of 8 sales (75%) — the stocks we sold beat the Nifty afterwards. Red flag."

**Contents, one row per ISO week** (Monday–Friday exchange sessions, actual dates shown):

| Line | Definition |
|---|---|
| Closed trades this week and since start | Count, win rate, average win %, average loss %, **expectancy** = win rate × average win − loss rate × average loss, labelled "before costs" until SA-048 |
| Sold too early | Matured EXIT/SWITCH/TRIM rows from the auditor at 10 and 30 sessions: share whose stock beat the benchmark afterwards; pending count shown |
| Exit reasons | Count per trigger, in plain words (`explain_triggers`) |
| You vs the Nifty | Money-weighted: each deposit buys Nifty units at that day's close; this week and since start |
| Breadth | Holdings count and invested share, against the floor once SA-049's floor exists |
| Holding period | Median sessions held for positions closed this week |
| Red flags | Predeclared below; one plain sentence each at the top |

**Red flags (fixed now, not tuned after seeing results):**

- expectancy below 0 for 3 consecutive weeks;
- sold too early above 60% at **30 sessions** (an investor's six weeks; the 10-session share
  shown beside it), over exits matured in the trailing 8 weeks, with at least 8 of them.
  Production on 11 Oct: 6 of 7 at 30 sessions, against 3 of 10 at 10 sessions (review
  section 11);
- `thesis_break` above 50% of exits over a trailing 4 weeks;
- behind the Nifty by more than 3 points over 4 weeks;
- median holding period of closed positions under 10 sessions.

A flag reports; it changes no decision.

**Delivery.**

- A pure module (proposed `core/portfolio/results.py`) computes the row. SA-051 reuses its
  money-weighted Nifty function for the chart; whichever lands first owns it.
- The row is stored in `data/portfolio/<user>/weekly_scorecard.jsonl`, idempotent by week key.
- The Sunday weekly review (`core/delivery/weekly.py`) leads with it, text and HTML, and
  `GET /portfolio/scorecard` returns the rows for the signed-in user only.
- The weekly email's 28-day advice scoreboard is **replaced** by the auditor-based lines, so
  there are not two scoreboards that disagree. SA-019 still owns the forecast-suggestion cohorts.

## Acceptance criteria

- A fixture ledger of closed trades gives win rate, averages and expectancy equal to hand figures;
  an open position is never counted as closed.
- A fixture auditor store gives the "sold too early" share by horizon; an unmatured row counts as
  pending, never as a win or loss; an exited stock stays in the denominator.
- Two deposits on different dates and a hand-built Nifty series give the hand-computed Nifty
  equivalent; a missing benchmark reads "unavailable", never a flat line.
- Each red flag fires on a fixture built to cross it and stays silent one unit below it.
- Re-running the same week rewrites nothing and appends nothing.
- A member's scorecard reads only that member's ledger (the FIX-005 invariant).

## Unit and integration evidence

Hand-computed fixtures for each line; a property test that expectancy, win rate and counts agree
with a direct recomputation over random ledgers; the weekly job end to end on fixtures with
transports blocked.

Use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports. Relevant existing tests plus these
independent invariants are required; do not merely assert current implementation outputs.

## Hard review

Can the scorecard flatter the portfolio? Check that exited stocks stay in every denominator, that
horizons count exchange sessions, that the Nifty comparison is money-weighted and includes cash
drag, and that a red flag cannot be silenced by missing data (missing data is shown, not
skipped).

Apply [REVIEW.md](../REVIEW.md) in a fresh conversation after implementation.

## Rollout and rollback

Report only; no trading change. The first Sunday after deploy, the owner reads the email and the
endpoint and records the first row in the receipt. Rollback is a revert. This card does not
authorize sending extra emails beyond the existing weekly review.
