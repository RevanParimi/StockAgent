# PR-003 — Fresh overnight news: no repeats, published-date freshness, global cues

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **planned**, product results track · Provisional estimate: **2 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: none. Source: section 6
of the [product results review](../../../audit/2026-10-11-product-results-review.md), owner
request of 2026-10-11 ("the daily brief on overnight reasons has pretty much same stuff every
day").

## Intended result

The brief's overnight section shows only news that is new since the last brief, judged by when
it was **published**, plus the overnight market numbers a trader checks before 09:15.

**The defect (code).**

- `services/background/macro_news_cache.py` `_recent_entries` treats "last 24 hours" as "in
  today's or yesterday's **fetch file**". The same URL fetched again on a new day is a new entry,
  since URLs are de-duplicated only within one day's file.
- `services/background/macro_news_fetcher.py` `_normalize_serper_date` turns any unparseable date
  into **today**.
- So a week-old article that Google keeps ranking for "India RBI SEBI policy announcement"
  returns every morning as overnight news.

**Measured (July snapshot):** the RBI/rupee story was in all 8 briefs from 20 to 29 July, and 14
of the 21 overnight items from 21 to 29 July repeated the previous day's.

**Changes.**

1. **Freshness by publish time.** An item enters the brief only if its `published_date` is on or
   after the previous brief's date. An unparseable date is stored but never shown as overnight.
2. **No repeats across days.** Each shown item gets a story key: the URL, plus a salient-token
   fingerprint, reusing `_salient_tokens`. The brief stores the keys it showed. An item whose key
   matches anything shown in the user's last 5 briefs is suppressed. The suppressed count is
   recorded in the brief JSON.
3. **Overnight market numbers, not news.** A "Before the open" line: S&P 500 and Nasdaq closes,
   Nikkei and Hang Seng when open, Brent, USD/INR, US 10-year yield, each with change and as-of
   date, from Yahoo (`services/data/fetchers/macro.py` already reads INR and commodities). A
   missing figure is omitted with "unavailable", never zero. GIFT Nifty only if a reliable free
   source is found; otherwise not shown.

## Acceptance criteria

- The same URL fetched on 3 consecutive days appears in at most one brief.
- An article published 3 days before the brief but fetched today is not shown as overnight.
- A reworded headline of an already-shown story (same salient entities) is suppressed; a
  genuinely new development on the same topic (new entities, newer publish date) is shown.
- An unparseable date never reaches the overnight section.
- The market line shows each figure with its as-of date; a failed figure is absent and named
  "unavailable".
- Chat and the daily review, which also read the macro cache, keep working; their freshness rule
  changes the same way and is documented.

JSON's suppressed counts). Expect fewer overnight items on quiet days: that is correct. Rollback
is a revert.
