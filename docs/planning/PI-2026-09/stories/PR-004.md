# PR-004 — Enough researched ideas to fill 10+ positions

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **planned**, product results track · Provisional estimate: **3 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: none. Related:
[SA-049](SA-049.md) (deploys cash into these ideas, with the breadth floor). Source: section 4 of
the [product results review](../../../audit/2026-10-11-product-results-review.md), owner request
of 2026-10-11 ("we will be in critical data issue if we don't research on more stocks").

## Intended result

The discovery shelf always holds enough verified, diverse ideas to fill and maintain at least
10 holdings.

**Today (config).** The weekly funnel runs `discovery.deep_dive_count: 10` deep dives and keeps
at most `shelf_size: 10` ideas at conviction ≥ 0.55. Filling 10 positions plus replacements
needs roughly 15–20 qualified ideas across 5+ sectors, and nothing stops the shelf filling with
one sector.

**Changes.**

- `discovery.shelf_size` 10 → **20**; `discovery.deep_dive_count` 10 → **20** a week (IPO slots
  stay within it).
- New `discovery.max_ideas_per_sector: 3`. When a 4th idea in a sector qualifies, it replaces
  that sector's weakest only if its conviction is higher; the event says so.
- **Watchlist BUYs qualify** (answers SA-049's open question; owner to confirm at
  implementation). A stock on the user's watchlist whose latest deep dive is an actionable BUY or
  STRONG BUY within `discovery.stale_days` is a candidate with the same checks as a shelf idea.
- **Stale ideas are re-checked before use.** An idea whose deep dive is older than 30 days is
  re-dived before it can be bought, within the weekly budget.
- **Measured cost.** The receipt records the measured cost per deep dive from the run logger and
  the monthly projection at 20 a week.
- **Idea quality line.** The weekly scorecard (PR-002) gains one line from the auditor's shelf
  lane: how shelf ideas did against the Nifty at 10 and 30 sessions after shelving.

**Additions from the production baseline (2026-10-12)** (review section 11):

- **The shelf of 11 Oct:** 9 active ideas.
  - Gates: 7 shelved before the data gate existed (unverified), 1 abstained (LIQUID1), and only
    AUROPHARMA verified actionable.
  - 5 of the 9 are tagged `generic`.
  - Prices on 9 Oct: AUROPHARMA was in its band and REDINGTON 0.3% above it. DYCL, MARKSANS,
    DEEPINDS, STYLAMIND and ALIVUS were 6–12% below their bands but above invalidation.
  - So the deploy step would find about 6 stock candidates in record mode, and 1 under the
    strict gate.
- **Only companies.** LIQUID1 is the Kotak Nifty 1D Rate Liquid ETF, a cash fund. The screen must
  exclude ETFs, index and liquid funds, REITs and InvITs by instrument type, from the NSE series
  or the instrument registry, never by name.
- **Cap by real industry.** `max_ideas_per_sector` counts the NSE industry (SA-026's lens key when
  it exists), never the graph label `generic`.
- **Re-dive the pre-gate ideas.** The first weekly run after deploy re-dives every active idea
  whose `data_gate` is empty, within the budget, before adding new ones. An idea that then
  abstains is dropped, with the reason.
- **Owner, 12 Oct: "I need more data and so as the stocks".** The shelf must support 12
  holdings plus rotation: 20 active ideas is the floor of this story, not a ceiling.

## Acceptance criteria

- With a fixture screen of 40 candidates in 8 sectors, the shelf fills to 20 by conviction with
  at most 3 per sector.
- The deep-dive budget is never exceeded, IPO slots included.
- A watchlist BUY within the staleness window is a candidate; one older, or abstained, is not.
- An idea older than 30 days is not a buy candidate until re-dived.
- Config values off (10/10, no sector cap) reproduce today's shelf exactly (characterization test).
