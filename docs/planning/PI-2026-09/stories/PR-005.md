## Intended result

Every holding carries a short, written **investment case**, recorded when it is bought. It says
why we own the stock, what we expect, what would prove us wrong, and what we watch next. The case
is what explanations, the brief and the exit rules refer to.

**Today.** Shelf ideas have a `thesis`, an entry band and an invalidation price. A **holding**
has none of this: once bought, its only reasons are rule codes. The "thesis" the exit rule
checks is the 30-day price path, not the business case.

**Example (illustrative).**

```json
{"symbol": "ABCBANK", "opened_on": "2026-10-20", "entry_price": 212.0,
 "pillars": [
   {"claim": "Loan growth with steady asset quality",
    "evidence": [{"fact": "Loans +18% YoY, GNPA 1.9%", "source": "results", "date": "2026-10-17"}]},
   {"claim": "Valuation below its own history",
    "evidence": [{"fact": "P/B 1.3 vs 5-year median 1.5", "source": "fundamentals", "date": "2026-10-19"}]}],
 "expectation": {"low": 245, "high": 255, "horizon_months": 9},
 "invalidation": {"price": 190, "business": ["GNPA above 2.5% at Q3 results"]},
 "watch_next": [{"what": "Q3 results", "date": "2027-01-18"}],
 "written": "at_purchase", "source_run_id": "…", "version": 1}
```

**How.**

- **At purchase** (SWITCH buy, SA-049's deploy buy, a manual add with an analysis): build the
  case from the inputs already stored:
  - the deep dive's thesis, drivers and risks;
  - the shelf idea's entry band and invalidation;
  - the dossier's guidance, catalysts and next results;
  - the latest fundamentals;
  - recent PR-006 events.
- One reasoning-tier call writes it from those inputs. **Code checks every number in the output
  against the inputs** (reuse the unified analyst's grounding rules). It rejects a case that
  invents one, and falls back to a deterministic case from the shelf idea's fields.
- **Immutable history.** The case is stored per user and symbol
  (`data/portfolio/<user>/cases/<SYMBOL>.jsonl`). A change appends a new version and never edits
  an old one.
- **Pillar status.** A new PR-006 event that contradicts a pillar marks it `challenged`, with
  the event cited. A results day re-checks every pillar. PR-009 reads this status.
- **Existing holdings** get a case built from their latest analysis, marked
  `written: after_purchase`, so nobody mistakes it for the original reason.
- `GET /portfolio/cases` returns the signed-in user's cases. PR-010 shows the one-line case on
  the portfolio screen.

## Acceptance criteria

- A case built from a fixture bundle contains only numbers present in that bundle; a stubbed model
  that invents a number is rejected and the deterministic case is stored instead.
- A case never cites an input dated after its creation time (the FIX-004 rule).
- Each buy path writes exactly one version 1 case; a re-run of the same review date writes none.
- A contradicting fixture event marks the matching pillar `challenged` and cites the event.
- A backfilled case is marked `after_purchase`.
- A member reads only their own cases.

## Routed input — 2026-10-24 PR-009 re-review (RR-L1, T1)

PR-009's evidence exit rule compares the close with a shelf idea's invalidation level in the
review day's price basis (`core/portfolio/exit_shadow.py`, `level_in_basis`). The level is divided
by every bonus or split that went ex after the dive, or it is not used, with a named gap. When this
story gives that rule the case's own invalidation price, the price goes through the same rule, and
two gaps close with it:

- **RR-L1.** The rule sees only bonuses and splits. A demerger, a capital reduction or a rights
  issue that goes ex between the dive (or the case's day) and the buy lowers the price, but not the
  level. Example: dived at ₹500 with the level ₹455, a demerger takes the price to ₹470, and the
  entry rule allows the buy there. A 3.2% fall to ₹455 then fires `case_broken`, with the 12% stop
  far away. A basis-changing row without a share-count ratio, in NSE's list or as a basis change in
  the instrument registry, from that day on, must leave the level unused with a gap. Add a fixture
  for each kind, with a neighbour one day outside the window. FIX-016's rework 2 made both signals
  available to grading: `ActionRecord.basis_changes` (the (ex-date, kind) of such rows, from
  `corp_actions.basis_change_kind`; NSE's wording in `tests/unit/test_exit_fix016.py`) and
  `exit_shadow.registry_break`. The level does not read them yet.
- **T1.** Pin a split or bonus that goes ex **on the buy day** in
  `tests/unit/test_exit_evidence_pr009.py`. The buy is at the post-split close, so the sync does
  not apply it, and the level must still be divided. The code does this today, but a mutant that
  drops the buy day (`dive < ex < buy`) passes the story file.

Both must be done before any switch to `advisor.exit_rule: evidence`. See
[the PR-009 review](../evidence/PR-009-review.md), the re-review's RR-L1 and T1 (repro
`analysis_data/pr009_rereview/test_rr_pr009.py`, RR4, in the reviewer's ignored folder).

## Routed input — 2026-10-24 owner decision (exit follow-ups 1, 5 and 4A)

From the owner's walkthrough of PR-009 (session `stockagent-main-cb`; the owner: "i go with the
recommendations"). Losers still run to the stop (average loss −11.9%), and the invalidation price
is the evidence rule's only price trigger. Three requirements for the case's invalidation price:

- **Stored in the buy day's price basis.** At purchase, the shelf idea's level is converted with
  PR-009's `level_in_basis` (its dive-to-buy part, including RR-L1's refusal for a demerger,
  capital reduction or rights issue). When the basis cannot be established, the case has no
  invalidation price and names the gap; it never stores the dive's raw level. From then on only the
  holding's own applied actions adjust it, so NSE's reach back to the dive day no longer matters.
- **Bounded by the stop and by noise.** The price is used only when it lies **above the holding's
  stop price** on the purchase day (otherwise the stop already covers it) and **at least 1.5 ×
  ATR% below the entry** (otherwise one ordinary day fires it), with the advisor's own ATR
  (`advisor.atr_period`). Outside that window it is not used, and the gap says which side.
  **Example:** entry ₹500, ATR 2%, so the stop is clamp(3 × 2%, 8%, 12%) = 8%, or ₹460, and the
  noise line is ₹485. A level of ₹470 is used; ₹455 (below the stop) and ₹490 (noise) are not.
- **News cannot sell through a pillar.** A `news` event that contradicts a pillar marks it
  `challenged`, but only an NSE filing or a rule-typed disclosure makes `case_broken`
  ([FIX-016](FIX-016.md), item 2).

Acceptance additions: a fixture for each side of the window and for each basis gap; a case whose
level was converted at purchase is not converted again; a challenged pillar from a `news` event
never sells. PR-013 then reports the average loss by exit reason, so the effect is measured.
