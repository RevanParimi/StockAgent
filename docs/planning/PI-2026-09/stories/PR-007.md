# PR-007 — Plain, fact-grounded buy and sell explanations

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **planned**, product results track · Provisional estimate: **3 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: [PR-005](PR-005.md)
(the case) and [PR-006](PR-006.md) (events). Source: sections 2, 3 and 7 of the
[product results review](../../../audit/2026-10-11-product-results-review.md), owner request of
2026-10-11 ("the reason for sell and buy is very code oriented … the end customer is normal non
technical").

## Intended result

Every buy, sell, trim, hold-through-a-fall and switch is explained in an investor's words from the
stock's own facts, in four short parts: **what we did, why, what would change our mind, what
happens next.**

**Today (code).** `core/portfolio/narrator.py` gives the model six things: the verdict, the
symbol, the close, the regime label, the trigger codes and the note codes. It runs on the bulk
tier and appends "Your position: −6.2% vs a 12.0% stop." It can only paraphrase codes. July's
one stored reason: "The thesis for IDFCFIRSTB has broken down under current market conditions …
This deterministic verdict reflects a regime shift to normal …"

**Target (illustrative):** "Sold XYZ Tech at ₹5,420 (−6.2%). Revenue fell 4% on the quarter and
management cut its outlook for the automotive design business, the main reason we owned it. The
stop (₹4,980) was not hit; we sold because the reason broke. ₹1.1 L moves to DEF Capital Goods."

**Changes.**

- **Inputs:**
  - the advice record and the holding's investment case;
  - PR-006 events since the last review;
  - price facts: entry, close, the stop as a **price**, and the move against the Nifty and the
    sector since purchase;
  - for a SWITCH, the destination's case; and where any cash goes.
- **Output:** JSON `{did, why, wrong_if, next}`, each at most two sentences.
- **Grounding check:** every number in the output must appear in the inputs. A failing output
  falls back to a deterministic template built from the case and the trigger, written in plain
  words: the trigger texts are rewritten for investors.
- **Banned words in user-facing text:** envelope, ATR, regime codes (`MACRO_CRISIS`, …),
  reversion prior, conviction streak, data gate, rationale hash, agent, verdict codes without
  explanation. A test enforces the list on every template and on stubbed model outputs.
- **Model:** a new `advisor.narrate_model`, defaulting to the reasoning tier; PR-012 may change
  it on evidence. The cache key includes the case version and the event ids.
- **Everywhere the reason appears:** the transaction `reason`, the digest, alerts
  (`advice_alert_fields`), the brief's flags and the weekly review use the same text.

## Acceptance criteria

- For each verdict (EXIT by stop, trailing stop, case broken, TRIM, ADD, SWITCH, HOLD through a
  fall) a fixture produces the four parts, and every number traces to an input.
- A stubbed output with an invented number, or a banned word, is rejected; the fallback is used
  and is itself free of banned words.
- The stop appears as a rupee price, not a percentage of an internal band.
- Narration failure never blocks the pipeline; the fallback is readable.
- Text for one user never contains another user's position (FIX-005).

## Unit and integration evidence

Fixtures per verdict with stubbed model outputs; a property test of the banned-word list over all
templates and random trigger/note combinations; the pipeline end to end with transports blocked.

Use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports. Relevant existing tests plus these
independent invariants are required; do not merely assert current implementation outputs.

## Routed input — 2026-10-18 SA-050 review (R4)

SA-050 measures weights against total equity. A portfolio with no cash on record (a manual
one; only the autopilot seed sets cash) has the holdings alone as its equity. So in a manual
portfolio of five equal stocks, each is 20% and every holding now carries `POSITION_LARGE`,
whose text reads "above the position cap of the whole portfolio, so the autopilot will not add
to it". The verdicts did not change: ADD was blocked the same way before. When this story
rewrites the explanations, word that note for a portfolio without cash: say "over 10% of your
holdings" and do not mention the autopilot. Add a fixture with no cash on record. See
[the SA-050 review](../evidence/SA-050-review.md), finding R4.

## Routed input — 2026-10-23 PR-009 review (L2)

Under `advisor.exit_rule: evidence`, a `case_broken` sale's alert and narrative say "a material
negative company event, an actionable SELL research verdict, or the price at its invalidation
level", without saying which one fired. The specific reason is only in
`data/portfolio/<user>/exit_shadow.jsonl` (`case_broken_reasons`). When this story rewrites the
explanations, the sale names its reason: the event and its date, the verdict and its day, or the
close and the level (for example "closed at ₹225, at its invalidation level of ₹227.50, which
is ₹455 adjusted for the 1:1 bonus"). Add a fixture for each of the three. It must be done
before any switch to `evidence`. See [the PR-009 review](../evidence/PR-009-review.md), finding
L2.
