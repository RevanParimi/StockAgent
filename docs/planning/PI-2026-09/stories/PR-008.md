# PR-008 — "Your stocks overnight" at the top of the morning brief

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **planned**, product results track · Provisional estimate: **3 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: [PR-003](PR-003.md)
(fresh macro items) and [PR-006](PR-006.md) (company events). Source: section 6 of the

## Intended result

The brief opens with what happened to **your** companies since the last brief, then the market.

**Today (code).** `core/delivery/brief.py` fills "Overnight" only from the macro cache's HIGH
items. The macro reviewer's prompt rates "sector or company news" as MEDIUM, so company news can
never reach the brief. In the July snapshot, no overnight item concerned a held company in 8
consecutive briefs, while two holdings were sold during those days.

**Brief order after the change:**

1. **Your stocks.** For each holding, then watchlist, then shelf idea (cap
   `delivery.brief_max_company_items`, proposed 8), the PR-006 events published since the previous
   brief, ranked by materiality, then by holding weight. Each line gives the company, what
   happened, one clause on why it matters to the case (from PR-005 when present), and the date.
   Results due within 3 sessions follow. Stocks with nothing new collapse into one line: "No news
   on your other 7 holdings."
2. **Before the open** (PR-003's market numbers).
3. **Market news**, at most 3 fresh macro items (PR-003).
4. Ideas, IPOs and lock-ins, as today.

The headline names the most material company item first. The narration uses the
`advisor.narrate_model` tier (PR-007), with the same grounding check and banned words.

## Acceptance criteria

- A fixture with 10 holdings, 3 with events, lists those 3 first in materiality order, then
  "No news on your other 7 holdings".
- An event shown in yesterday's brief is not shown again.
- A user's brief lists only that user's holdings and watchlist (FIX-005); shelf ideas are shared
  market facts.
- With the event store empty or failing, the brief still builds, with the section omitted and
  said to be unavailable.
- The HTML and text renderers and the brief view show the same order.
