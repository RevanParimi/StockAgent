# PR-009 — Exit on evidence, not on a price-path miss (shadow, then switch)

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **planned**, product results track · Provisional estimate: **5 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: [PR-002](PR-002.md)
(grading and the weekly line) and [PR-006](PR-006.md) (company events). Uses [PR-005](PR-005.md)'s
pillars and invalidation when present. Source: section 1 of the
[product results review](../../../audit/2026-10-11-product-results-review.md), owner request of
2026-10-11 ("the sell and profit of shares history is not positive, most of it is at loss").

## Intended result

A holding is sold when its **risk limit** is hit or its **reason** breaks, not because one day's
close missed a 30-day price path. The replacement rule runs in shadow first and replaces the old
one only after a predeclared comparison says it is not worse.

**Today (code).** The advisor's `thesis_break` fires when the latest thesis review says "not
intact" and the remaining path ends more than 1% below the close. That review runs after a daily
**price-path miss** larger than `max(1.5%, 1.5 × ATR%)`. It is one short LLM call that, by its
own docstring, fetches no new data.

**Measured (July snapshot):** all 9 sales came from this trigger, after 4 to 17 days. Of the 8
measurable, 6 stocks beat the Nifty over the 20 sessions after the sale (median +3.0 points).

**Example.** Bought at ₹500 for its order book. Day 4: the market falls 2%, the stock 4%. The path
expected ₹505, so the miss triggers a review, which says "not intact". The re-forecast path ends
at ₹478, so the legacy rule EXITs at ₹480. The evidence rule asks: is there a negative company
event? Is the latest actionable research verdict a SELL? Is ₹480 below the invalidation line
(₹455)? Has the stock been held 10 sessions? All four answers are no, so it **holds**.

**The evidence rule** (a pure function beside `decide`):

- **Unchanged:** `stop_breach`, `trailing_stop_breach`, `crisis_regime_bearish`,
  `shock_reforecast`, and the identity and data-gate holds.
- **`thesis_break` is replaced by `case_broken`**, which needs at least one of:
  1. a **high-materiality negative company event** (PR-006) in the last 5 sessions that
     contradicts a case pillar (or, with no case yet, any high-materiality negative event);
  2. the latest **actionable** research verdict is SELL or STRONG SELL;
  3. the close is **below the invalidation price** (case, or the shelf idea's level).
- **A minimum holding period**, `advisor.min_hold_sessions` (proposed 10), applies to
  `case_broken` only. Stops are never delayed.
- **It also cuts losers sooner.** Since August, winners were cut at an average of +7.5% while
  losers ran to the full stop at −11.9% (expectancy −2.2% per trade). When the reason breaks (a
  material negative event, or an actionable SELL), `case_broken` can exit a loser before the stop.
  The scorecard shows the average loss so this effect is visible.

**Shadow and switch.**

- `advisor.exit_rule: legacy | shadow | evidence`. It ships as `shadow`: the legacy rule decides,
  and both verdicts are recorded each review day in
  `data/portfolio/<user>/exit_shadow.jsonl`, one row per holding per review date (idempotent),
  with each rule's reasons.
- **Grading.** Each disagreement is graded on the stock's excess return over the Nifty at **10 and
  30 sessions** after the decision date, using the auditor's benchmark code:
  - legacy EXIT, evidence HOLD: keeping was right if excess ≥ 0;
  - evidence EXIT, legacy HOLD: selling was right if excess < 0.
- **Why two horizons (production, 2026-10-12, review section 11):** the auditor's exits since
  10 Jul look right at 10 sessions (7 of 10: the stock kept falling) and wrong at 30 sessions
  (6 of 7 sold stocks then beat the Nifty, median +4.7 points). A single horizon would flatter one
  rule or the other, so the gate needs both.
- PR-002's scorecard shows the running tally at both horizons.
- **Switch gate, fixed now:**
  - at least **15 matured disagreements at 30 sessions**, over at least **8 weeks**;
  - the evidence rule is right on **at least 50%** of them at **both** 10 and 30 sessions (not
    worse at either);
  - no single stock is more than 30% of the disagreements;
  - the pass holds at **two consecutive weekly looks**.
  - With 12 or more holdings (SA-049), expect the first possible pass about 10–12 weeks after
    the shadow starts.
  The report recommends; **the owner switches** to `evidence`. A result of "no difference" is
  valid, and then the simpler rule may still be chosen on the owner's judgment, recorded.
- **After the switch**, `thesis_break` remains computed and recorded for one month for
  comparison, then the thesis-review → exit path is retired (the reviewer may stay for the
  learning loop).

## Acceptance criteria

- The ₹500 example: legacy EXIT, evidence HOLD, both recorded with reasons.
- The gate fixture: a tally that passes at 30 sessions but fails at 10 does not recommend a switch,
  and neither does the reverse.
- Each `case_broken` path fires on its own fixture and not one unit short of it; within the
  minimum holding period only stops fire.
- **Property test:** over random signals, the evidence rule fires `stop_breach` and
  `trailing_stop_breach` exactly when the legacy rule does.
- In `shadow`, decisions and transactions are byte-identical to `legacy`.
- Grading uses only prices after the decision date; an unmatured disagreement is pending, never
  scored.
- One shadow row per holding per review date, however often the pipeline re-runs.

## Unit and integration evidence

Hand-built fixtures for each rule and the example; the property test; a pipeline end-to-end run in
each mode with prices and events stubbed; a grading fixture with a hand-computed tally that crosses
and misses the gate.

Use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports. Relevant existing tests plus these
independent invariants are required; do not merely assert current implementation outputs.

## Hard review

- Can the evidence rule trap a falling stock? Stops must be untouched (the property test).
- Can a stale or misattributed event fire `case_broken`? Check the event's date and symbol identity.
- Is the gate's threshold applied to matured rows only, with exited stocks retained?
- Is the switch really off (`shadow`) by default?

Apply [REVIEW.md](../REVIEW.md) in a fresh conversation after implementation.

gate. Rollback: `legacy`. This card does not authorize changing production variables.
