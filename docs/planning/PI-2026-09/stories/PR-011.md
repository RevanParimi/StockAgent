## Intended result

The system sees what the company's insiders and the big funds are doing, which practitioners in
Indian equities watch closely and the product cannot see today.

**Today (code, `config.yaml` `discovery.signal_weights`):** `insider_buying` (0.10) and
`mf_holding` (0.05) are **dark**: their weights exist, and the fetchers return None.
`discovery.max_promoter_pledge_pct` "ships dark in v1 (no free data source yet)". Shareholding
(promoter, FII, DII %) is read from Yahoo but never compared quarter to quarter.

**Sources to prove first** (free, public disclosures):

| Data | Source | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| Promoter and insider trades (SEBI PIT) | NSE insider-trading disclosures | Event `insider` (buy or sell, who, value); discovery `insider_buying` signal |
| Substantial acquisitions (SAST) | NSE SAST disclosures | Event `deal` |
| Promoter pledge | NSE pledge disclosures | Event `pledge` when it rises; the discovery pledge guard switched on |
| Shareholding pattern, quarterly | NSE shareholding filings (or Yahoo holders as fallback) | Quarter-on-quarter change in promoter, FII, DII and MF share; event `ownership` on a move of 1 point or more |

**Proof before wiring.** NSE endpoints have behaved differently from cloud hosts: the IPO ladder
fetch broke in production in September. For each source, a read-only probe from the production
container (run by the owner, as with earlier probes) records whether it returns data. A source
that fails stays out, and its discovery weight is **deleted** rather than left dark. The
remaining weights renormalise, as they already do for missing signals.

## Acceptance criteria

- Parsers turn recorded fixture payloads into the documented events, with the source's date.
- A quarter-on-quarter fixture (promoter 52.1% → 50.6%) emits one `ownership` event.
- An unavailable source gives "unavailable" and never a neutral score; a deleted weight no longer
  appears in config or ranking.
- The production probe's result per source is recorded in the receipt, sanitized.

## Unit and integration evidence

Recorded fixtures per source, including malformed and empty responses; the discovery ranking
with signals present, absent and deleted.

Use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports. Relevant existing tests plus these
independent invariants are required; do not merely assert current implementation outputs.

## Hard review

Is a filing's disclosure date distinct from its transaction date, and is the right one used for
"when we could have known"? Is a promoter's sale to meet a regulatory float requirement told apart
from a discretionary sale, or at least not labelled more confidently than the data allows?
