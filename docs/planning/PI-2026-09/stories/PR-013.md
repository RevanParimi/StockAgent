# PR-013 — Show where losses come from and what waiting costs

Priority: **P2** · Scope: **owner decision** of 2026-10-24 (a walkthrough of PR-009 in session
`stockagent-main-cb`) · Provisional estimate: **2 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: [PR-002](PR-002.md) (the
weekly scorecard) and [PR-009](PR-009.md) (the shadow rows). Ordered after [PR-005](PR-005.md), so
its case-based exits are in the counts. Report only: it changes no decision.

## Why

Since August the average win was +7.5% and the average loss −11.9%, an average of −2.2% per trade
(section 11 of the [product results review](../../../audit/2026-10-11-product-results-review.md)).
PR-009 should keep winners longer, and PR-005's invalidation price should cut some losers before the
stop. Nothing shows which exit produces the losses, or how deep a fall the evidence rule sits
through when it keeps a stock the price-path rule sold.

**Example.** Bought at ₹500. The price-path rule sells at ₹480 (−4%). The evidence rule would have
kept it. If the stock then falls to ₹440 before recovering, the scorecard should show that the
evidence rule's choice sat through a 12% fall from the buy, even if it was right at 30 sessions.

## Intended result

The Sunday scorecard adds three lines, in plain words, for the account's own portfolio:

1. **Closed trades by exit reason**, over the last 13 weeks and since the start: count, wins and
   average win, losses and average loss, and the average per trade, for each reason (stop,
   trailing stop, price-path exit, case broken, crisis, shock, SWITCH sale, TRIM). From the
   transaction ledger and the advice record's trigger codes.
2. **What waiting cost.** For each PR-009 decision in which the evidence rule kept the stock, the
   lowest close below the decision close before 10 and before 30 sessions: the median and the
   worst.
3. **Pace.** Decisions per week over the last 4 weeks, and the earliest week the switch gate could
   pass at that pace, labelled as arithmetic, not a forecast.

Example lines:

- "Closed trades, last 13 weeks: 8. Wins 4, average +7.5%. Losses 4, average −11.9% (stop 3,
  average −13.1%; price-path exit 1, −8.4%). Average per trade −2.2%."
- "What waiting cost: 3 stocks kept by the evidence rule. Lowest point before 10 sessions: median
  −3.1%, worst −7.4%."
- "Pace: 1.5 decisions a week. At this pace the gate can first pass in the week of 8 Feb 2027
  (arithmetic)."

## Acceptance criteria

- Hand-computed oracle on a fixture ledger: counts and averages per exit reason match; a trade with
  no trigger code is counted under "other", never dropped.
- The waiting-cost line uses closes only up to each horizon's day; a pending horizon is shown as
  pending.
- The pace arithmetic matches PR-009's gate constants.
- A member sees only their own trades; the line changes no advice or trade.
- KT section 7 states the lines as current design; a guide case; PDF; `check_kt_docs`; full suite.
