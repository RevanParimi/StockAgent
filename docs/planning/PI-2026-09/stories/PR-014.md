Priority: **P0** · Scope: **owner decision** of 2026-11-14 ("yes" to R1 of the
[profit-path review](../../../audit/2026-11-14-profit-path-architecture-review.md)) · Estimate:
**2 points** · Lane: **rollout** (no code; one setting)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: [FIX-030](FIX-030.md) and
[FIX-031](FIX-031.md), both accepted. **Order:** first, ahead of [FIX-032](FIX-032.md).

## Why

The owner, 14 Nov: "I don't see any profit, neither investments." With the switch off, the autopilot
opens a position only by swapping out a holding. The book has stood at 2 holdings and about 81% cash
since September. Everything the switch waited for (FIX-023, FIX-024, FIX-025) was live by 7 Nov.

The book is paper money. A wrong buy costs a row in a practice ledger. A closed switch costs the
only evidence the goal needs: what the product buys, and what that earns against the Nifty.

**Example.** ₹10 L with 19% in stocks: a 10% rise earns about ₹19,000. At the 80% target the same
rise earns about ₹80,000.

## Steps

The owner's word for the switch was given on 14 Nov. Each push still happens at a time the owner
agrees in the rollout conversation, in a job-free window.

1. **`FIX-030-M1`** (STATE's pending check), from about 14:00 IST on Sat 14 Nov. If M1 occurs, its
   one-point card comes before the deploy of FIX-030.
2. **Commit FIX-031**: its 16 manifest files, then the docs with the five commit-time edits in
   [its review receipt](../evidence/FIX-031-review.md). The docs commit also carries the 14 Nov
   planning files. In the same docs commit, KT section 12 and AUTOPILOT_GUIDE section 16 take the
   14 Nov decisions (they could not be edited before: both files are in FIX-031's manifest).
3. **Full suite** on the tree to be pushed, return code 0.
4. **Push**, which deploys FIX-030, FIX-031 and CLEAN-001 together. CLEAN-001 changed
   `requirements.txt`, so the build resolves every unpinned package again: read the build's result,
   `/health` and the first scheduled job after it before going on.
5. **Read the paper lane's record** of shelf ideas against the Nifty (read-only, counts). It is
   recorded, not a gate: on paper money, buying is the test.
6. **Switch on**: `portfolio.overrides.primary.deploy_idle_cash: true` in `config.yaml`, committed
   and pushed. By default this is a second push, after step 4's deploy has been read; one push for
   both is the owner's choice.
7. **First run with the switch on**: read the digest's `deployment` record. Write down what was
   bought, the reason the run stopped, and the count of ideas by refusal reason (counts and symbols
   only).
8. **Add three dated checks** to STATE's `pending_production_checks`, with their dates:
   - **`PR-014-D1`, the door read**, after 10 review sessions with the switch on. Per day: ideas
     offered, bought, and refused by reason. **Rule:** if more than a third of the BUY ideas offered
     were refused "above entry band" (counted per idea per day), open a 2-point card for the band:
     its top scaled by the stock's own volatility, or an out-of-band BUY takes Saturday's first
     re-dive slots. Otherwise close it as not occurring. Name the reason that most often stopped the
     run.
   - **`PR-014-R1`, results at 4 weeks**, and **`PR-014-R2`, at 8 weeks**: share invested,
     holdings, the book against the Nifty since switch-on, closed trades by exit reason
     ([PR-013](PR-013.md)'s line once built), refusal counts. The owner reads the page. The next
     design changes are decided from it: whether the factor engine is promoted, whether the
     learning rebuild returns, whether the daily price-path loop shrinks.
9. **Remind the owner of the setup actions deferred to rollout** (HANDOFF's resume checklist):
   Railway alerts, the OpenRouter key limit, Healthchecks.io with Telegram, SA-007's backup bucket.

## Acceptance criteria

- Production runs FIX-030 and FIX-031, and a 16:30 run's digest carries a `deployment` record.
- The first run's record is in the receipt.
- `PR-014-D1`, `PR-014-R1` and `PR-014-R2` are in STATE with dates.
- KT section 1's production table and section 12, AUTOPILOT_GUIDE section 16 and guide case 07-G
  say the switch is on.

Not an acceptance criterion: that anything was bought on the first day. A run that buys nothing and
says why is a valid first result, and it is what `PR-014-D1` reads.
