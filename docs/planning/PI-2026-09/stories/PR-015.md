# PR-015 — The evidence exit rule acts

[profit-path review](../../../audit/2026-11-14-profit-path-architecture-review.md)) · Estimate:

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: [FIX-015](FIX-015.md) (the

## Why

[PR-009](PR-009.md) built the evidence rule and a gate for switching to it: 15 matured disagreements
at 30 sessions over at least 8 weeks. The gate was sized for 12 or more holdings. With 2 holdings it
cannot fill for many months, and all that time the old rule keeps selling. On 11 Oct, 6 of the 7
stocks the old rule sold then beat the Nifty at 30 sessions.

The owner decided on 14 Nov not to wait for the gate. The book is paper money, the stops are the
same under both rules, and the old rule's verdict is still recorded every day, so the same
comparison runs with the roles swapped.

**Example.** Bought at ₹500. The old rule sells at ₹480 on a price-path miss. The evidence rule
keeps the stock, because no filing, SELL verdict or invalidation price says its case broke. If it
recovers to ₹560, the evidence rule earned ₹80 a share more. If it falls to its stop at ₹440, it
lost ₹40 a share more.

## Steps

1. Confirm from the code as it stands that `evidence` mode still records the old rule's verdict in
   `exit_shadow.jsonl` and that the scorecard's "Exit rule test" line reads both.
2. `advisor.exit_rule: evidence` in `config.yaml`, committed and pushed at a time the owner agrees,
   in a job-free window.
3. Living documents in the same commit: KT sections 1, 6 and 12, AUTOPILOT_GUIDE, guide case 07-L.
4. After the first review day, read-only: one shadow row per advised holding, the acting rule
   `evidence`, and any SELL's triggers (counts and trigger names only).
5. `thesis_break` stays computed and recorded for one month, as PR-009 says. Retiring the
   thesis-review exit path is a later decision, not this card.

## How it is judged

At `PR-014-R2` (8 weeks after deployment's switch-on) the owner reads the scorecard's tally at 10
and 30 sessions and [PR-013](PR-013.md)'s "what waiting cost" line, and keeps the rule or rolls it
back. "No difference" is a valid result.

## Delegated call

**PR-005's invalidation window is not a precondition.** Until [PR-005](PR-005.md) is built, the
rule reads the idea's invalidation price as PR-009 built it. That price is at least 8% below the
close the deep dive read (`deep_dive.entry_levels`), so one ordinary day does not fire it.
**Revisit** if PR-013 shows price-trigger exits within one day's usual move of the buy price.

## Rollback

`advisor.exit_rule: shadow` and push. The old rule decides again from the next review.
