# PR-017 — Grade every deep dive at the conviction it was issued with, beside the screen's other candidates

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **owner decision** of 2026-11-15 (edge roadmap, stage 2) · Estimate:
**5 points** · Lane: **decision path** (it defines how ideas are graded; fresh-session review)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. No dependency. **Order:** after
[PR-016](PR-016.md).

**New conversation? Read first:** [EDGE-ROADMAP.md](../EDGE-ROADMAP.md) sections 1 to 3, 6 and 7.

## Why

The product's core claim is: "our deep dive finds stocks that beat the Nifty, and the higher its
conviction, the better." The product researches about 20 ideas a week, so this claim can rest on
about 1,000 graded calls a year. Today it rests on much less, and partly on a flawed pairing.

## What exists, and what is wrong with it

`core/audit/outcomes.py` `grade_shelf_lane` already grades shelf ideas against the Nifty at 10, 30
and 60 sessions (lane `shelf`, rows in the auditor's append-only store, job `audit_nightly` at
23:45). Found by code inspection on 15 Nov 2026; how often each happens is **not measured**:

1. **The conviction on a grade is not the one the idea was issued with.** The lane reads
   `shelf.json` when a horizon matures and takes `idea["conviction"]`. A re-dive overwrites that
   value in place (`core/discovery/shelf.py` `ShelfStore.apply_redives`:
   `idea.conviction = dive.conviction`), and ideas are re-dived after 30 days.
   **Example.** Added on 3 Oct at 0.62. The stock rises 15%. Re-dived on 7 Nov at 0.80. Graded at
   30 sessions as "a 0.80 idea that rose 15%". The conviction check then looks better than it is.
2. **Only ideas on the shelf are graded.** A deep dive that came out NEUTRAL, SELL or under 0.55 is
   never graded, so nothing shows whether BUY ideas do better than rejected ones. Check whether a
   dropped idea stays in `shelf.json` until its horizons mature; if it is pruned first, failed
   ideas leave the record.
3. **The screen's other candidates are never graded.** Nothing shows whether the deep dive adds
   anything over the cheap screen. This is the claim an investor will test.
4. **The entry price is from before the dive.** `close_at_add` is the close the dive read
   (Saturday's dive reads Friday's close). The first price to act on is Monday's open.

## What to build

1. **Every dive, as issued.** `DeepDiveResult` (`src/backend/shared/schemas/discovery.py`) carries
   symbol, sector, graph, conviction, verdict, close, dive date and data gate.
   `ShelfStore.record_dives` and `shelf_events.jsonl` record dives. Check that every dive, shelved
   or not, is kept with those fields unchanged. If not, start an append-only `dives.jsonl` in the
   discovery data folder, and state the date from which the record is complete.
2. **Two new auditor lanes** beside `grade_shelf_lane`. The old `shelf` rows stay as they are.
   - `dive`: one reference per dive, `dive:<dive date>|<symbol>`; conviction and verdict as
     issued; entry at the open of the first session after the dive, with the dive's own close kept
     beside it.
   - `screen`: each week's screen candidates that were not dived
     (`data/discovery/screens/<date>_screen.json`, `ScreenResult.candidates`, with `composite` and
     `signal_ranks`), reference `screen:<screen date>|<symbol>`, same entry rule.
   - `bare`, **the owner's second judge** (15 Nov 2026): for each new deep dive, one extra call to
     a bare model that gets the same dated evidence and none of the product's method, and answers
     BUY or SKIP with a conviction. Reference `bare:<dive date>|<symbol>`, graded the same way. It
     cannot be backfilled; it starts with this card. At most 20 calls a week. This is the daily
     control lane's idea (`core/intelligence/rl/agents/control_lane.py`, a bare model against the
     product's price forecast), moved to the decision that spends money. The report then answers:
     does the product's method beat a plain model asked the same question?
3. **Horizons** 10, 30 and 60 sessions as today, plus 90 and 180 (`audit.horizons_td`). Check every
   reader that assumes three horizons.
4. **Corporate actions** between entry and grade are adjusted the way FIX-016 does for exit
   grading (`level_in_basis`, `core/portfolio/exit_shadow.py`).
5. **The report**, in the audit report (`core/audit/report.py`) and one scorecard line
   (`core/portfolio/results.py`): at each horizon, with n, the mean and median excess and the share
   beating the Nifty for **BUY dives**, **rejected dives**, **screen-only candidates** and **the
   bare model's BUYs**, and BUY dives split by graph (native, generic).
6. **Backfill** from the stored dives and screens as far back as they exist.

## Acceptance criteria

- An invariant test: the conviction on a `dive` row equals that dive's own, whatever re-dives
  followed (the example above as a fixture).
- The entry bar is dated after the dive; a dive with no price is skipped and counted.
- Idempotent per reference and horizon; the old `shelf` rows are byte-for-byte unchanged.
- Every figure carries its n; under 30 it reads "too few to judge".
- Living documents: KT section 7 (the auditor), the testing guide.
