# PR-018 — Put the conviction check in front of the owner, with a rule for trusting it

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **owner decision** of 2026-11-15 (edge roadmap, stage 2) · Estimate:
**2 points** · Lane: **light** (a report on rows PR-017 grades; it changes no decision)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependency: [PR-017](PR-017.md).
**Order:** directly after PR-017.

**New conversation? Read first:** [EDGE-ROADMAP.md](../EDGE-ROADMAP.md) sections 1 to 3, 6 and 7.

## Why

Every deep dive ends in a conviction between 0 and 1. The shelf takes ideas at 0.55 or more, and
deployment buys the highest first. Nobody has shown the owner whether a 0.80 idea does better than
a 0.60 one. If it does not, the ordering is arbitrary. The owner's condition of 4 Nov 2026:
position size may follow conviction only after the scorecard shows that conviction predicts
returns.

**Example.** At 30 sessions: ideas at 0.75 and above +4.1% against the Nifty (n 34), ideas at 0.55
to 0.65 +0.3% (n 41). Conviction carries information. If both read about +1%, it does not.

## What exists

`core/audit/metrics.py` has `conviction_calibration(rows, horizon=30, buckets=5)` and
`calibration_spread`, and `core/audit/report.py` prints them. Their limits:

- they read the `shelf` lane, whose conviction is not the issued one (PR-017, finding 1);
- five even buckets from 0 to 1 put nearly every shelf idea into two buckets, because shelf ideas
  sit between 0.55 and about 0.9;
- the result is not on the weekly scorecard, so the owner does not see it.

## What to build

1. Read PR-017's `dive` lane, at the conviction each dive was issued with.
2. Buckets that fit the data: rejected dives (under 0.55), 0.55 to 0.65, 0.65 to 0.75, 0.75 and
   above. Keep the old function's behaviour for its existing callers.
3. One scorecard line, on the first Sunday of each month (`core/portfolio/results.py`): for each
   bucket the mean excess at 30 sessions and its n; "too few to judge" under 30.
4. **The rule, written now.** Conviction "predicts" when, at 30 sessions, the top bucket's mean
   excess is at least 2 points above the 0.55 to 0.65 bucket's, each with n of 30 or more, at two
   monthly looks in a row. The owner may change the 2 points before the first look.
   - When it passes: record it in STATE and name [PR-029](PR-029.md) (size by conviction) as ready
     to promote.
   - When 300 dives are graded and the gap is flat or negative: say so plainly on the scorecard.
     The owner then decides between an independent challenge ([PR-024](PR-024.md)), two runs that
     must agree ([PR-025](PR-025.md)) and a better model ([PR-012](PR-012.md)).

## Acceptance criteria

- The line's figures equal the audit report's for the same rows (a test).
- A fixture for each outcome of the rule: pass, too few, flat.
- The rule's two looks use different months' rows only once each (no row counted as two looks).
- Living documents: KT section 7, the testing guide.
