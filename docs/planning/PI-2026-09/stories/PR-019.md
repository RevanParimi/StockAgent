## Why

An idea is bought only if it passes about a dozen checks: a BUY verdict, conviction of 0.55 or
more, a deep dive at most 30 days old and made before the session's close, not sold since, the data
gate, a close for the session, NSE's surveillance report, the entry band, above its invalidation
price, a named sector, the position and sector caps, at most 2 new positions a day, and a market
regime recorded for the day. Each is sensible. `PR-014-D1` counts how often each one refuses. This
card shows what the refused ideas did next, in money.

**Example.** "Entry band: 14 ideas refused. They then beat the Nifty by 3.1% at 30 sessions (n 14).
The ideas that were bought beat it by 1.2% (n 22)." That rule is refusing the better ideas.

The entry band is the first suspect: its top is 2% above the close the deep dive read and stays for
up to 30 days, so an idea that rises is refused while one that drifts down is bought
([BACKLOG](../BACKLOG.md) B-3).

## What exists

- `core/portfolio/deployment.py` `run_record`: each run with deployment on records, per idea, one
  outcome: `bought`, `waiting`, `held`, `sold`, `verdict`, `gate`, `price`, `before_dive`,
  `surveillance`, `band`, `invalidated`, `size`, `ledger`. It goes into the day's digest
  (`core/portfolio/digest.py`).
- `core/discovery/candidates.py` `buy_candidates` returns the ideas it did not offer and why. Today
  that is only logged (`[portfolio_pipeline] ... not offered`).

## What to build

1. **Keep each day's per-idea outcome** where it can be read later. Check how long digests are
   kept; if not long enough, append them to `data/portfolio/<user>/deployment_outcomes.jsonl`,
   together with the candidate list's not-offered reasons.
2. **Join to PR-017's `dive` grades.** For each refusal reason: the ideas it refused, each counted
   once per dive (its first refusal day), and their excess at 10 and 30 sessions from that day,
   beside the ideas bought over the same weeks.
3. **One scorecard block**, monthly (`core/portfolio/results.py`): one line per reason with n.
4. **A flag, written now.** A reason whose refused ideas beat the bought ones by 2 points or more
   at 30 sessions, each with n of 30 or more, is marked "costing money" and goes to the owner as a
   decision with its example. Nothing changes by itself.

## Acceptance criteria

- An idea refused on five days counts once; an idea refused, then bought, counts as bought.
- A reason with n under 30 reads "too few to judge".
- The block's counts reconcile with the digests' counts for the same days (a test).
