# PR-020 — One page that shows the record

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **owner decision** of 2026-11-15 (edge roadmap, stage 2) · Estimate:
**3 points** · Lane: **light** (a screen and a read-only route)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: [PR-013](PR-013.md)
(losses by exit reason) and [PR-016](PR-016.md) (real prices). **Starts when** the pending check
`PR-014-R1` has been read (4 weeks with deployment on).

**New conversation? Read first:** [EDGE-ROADMAP.md](../EDGE-ROADMAP.md) sections 1 to 3 and 6.

## Why

The owner is selling the idea. The first thing anyone will ask for is the record, on one page, in a
form they cannot pick apart. Today the facts are spread over the weekly email, the portfolio screen
and the audit report.

## What the page shows

For the owner's portfolio, from deployment's first run (16 Nov 2026) to today:

1. **The book against the Nifty**, money-weighted, in total and month by month
   (`core/portfolio/results.py` `portfolio_vs_nifty`).
2. **The worst fall** from a peak, with its dates (`value_history.jsonl`).
3. **Share invested and number of holdings** over time.
4. **Closed trades:** count, wins and average win, losses and average loss, and the split by exit
   reason (PR-013).
5. **Costs paid and the tax estimate** (`core/portfolio/costs.py`).
6. **The same return at prices that could really be had** (PR-016).
7. **The ideas' record and the conviction check**, when their n allows (PR-017, PR-018).
8. **Each open position** with the date bought, the result so far and its one-line reason.

Always on the page: "paper portfolio", the date range, the n behind each figure, and the wording
`docs/LEGAL_AND_COMPLIANCE.md` requires (research, not advice).

## What exists

`GET /portfolio/performance` and `GET /portfolio/scorecard`
(`services/api/routes/portfolio_api.py`); the portfolio screen
(`src/frontend/prototypes/`, the `portfolio` module); the weekly scorecard rows
(`weekly_scorecard.jsonl`).

## What to build

- `GET /portfolio/record`: the caller's own portfolio only, every figure with its date.
- A "Record" view in the app that prints cleanly from the browser (no new PDF library).
- Nothing is computed twice: the page reads the functions the scorecard already uses.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every figure equals the scorecard's for the same dates (a test).
- A figure that cannot be computed says so; no sample data (PR-001's rule).
- With one week of data the page works and reads "too few to judge" where it must.
- A member sees only their own portfolio (a test with two accounts).
- Living documents: KT section 10, the testing guide.

the browser suite and the guards pass.
