## Why

A senior analyst reads five to ten years of a company's numbers and knows when each was published.
The product reads the latest few quarters from Yahoo each time it analyses a stock, keeps none of
it, and does not know when a figure became public.

**Example.** A company's sales grew 18% last quarter. Over five years its cash from operations has
been half its reported profit. The first fact is in today's bundle; the second is not, and it is
the one that warns.

Three later cards need this store: accounting red flags ([PR-023](PR-023.md)), the replay of past
years ([PR-026](PR-026.md)), which may read only what was public on each day, and the written case
([PR-005](PR-005.md)), which cites dated facts.

## What exists

- `services/data/fetchers/fundamentals.py`: Yahoo statements through yfinance (quarterly P&L,
  margins, shareholding), bank formats included (FIX-003). `get_fundamentals_result` returns the
  text the analysis reads, with a status and an as-of.
- `services/data/context/bundle_builder.py`: the analysis's single fetch pass.
- `services/data/fetchers/nse_announcements.py`: NSE filings, with their filing times.
- `core/discovery/insider.py`: already reads NSE's XBRL documents (PR-011). The same approach
  reads NSE's financial-results XBRL.

## What to build

1. **A store of reported figures** per company and period (quarter and year): sales, operating
   profit, net profit, cash from operations, receivables, debt, other income, share count. Each row
   carries its source, the period end and **the date it became public** (the filing's date).
2. **Sources in order:** NSE's financial-results filings first (public, dated), Yahoo to fill gaps,
   marked as such. Decide the order from a measurement of coverage on 30 stocks.
3. **A reader that answers "as known on day D"**: only rows published on or before D.
4. **The analysis's bundle** gains a multi-year block from the store. Shadow first: record the
   block beside today's text for two weeks before the analysis reads it.

## Acceptance criteria

- An invariant test: a read "as known on D" never returns a row published after D.
- A restated figure is a new row; the old one is kept.
- A company the store cannot fill says so by field; nothing is imputed.
- Coverage is reported: stocks with 12 or more quarters, with 5 or more years.
- Living documents: KT sections 3 and 4, the testing guide.
