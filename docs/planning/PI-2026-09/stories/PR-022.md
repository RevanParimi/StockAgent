## Why

A professional reads the results press release, the investor presentation, the earnings-call
transcript and the annual report. The product reads a little of them, for some stocks.

**Example.** A company guides to lower margins on its earnings call. The filing's title says only
"Transcript of earnings call". The product sees the title and one web page about it. A stock that
is only an idea on the shelf gets nothing at all.

## What exists

- `core/intelligence/rl/agents/event_ingestor.py`: for a results, con-call, guidance or
  presentation filing it reads "announcement text + one Tavily page" and merges a digest into the
  stock's dossier (`merge_curator_output`, `dossier_curator.py`). It runs for **managed** stocks:
  on Saturday at 10:00, and the same day when the company-event feed stores such a filing.
- `core/events/feed.py`: twice each trading day, every held, watched and shelf stock's NSE filings,
  typed and dated.
- `src/backend/shared/schemas/dossier.py`: the dossier; `open_guidance(as_of)` and a dated digest.
- **Removed on 13 Nov (CLEAN-001):** the old document pipeline (`core/intelligence/rag/`, a vector
  store, embeddings, a PDF library). Nothing used it. Do not rebuild it by reflex.

## What to build

1. **Fetch the attached document** of a results, presentation, transcript or annual-report filing
   (NSE's attachment link) and extract its text. Add one PDF library only if needed, pinned.
2. **One reasoning-tier call per document**, with the whole text in context (no vector store):
   dated facts with a page reference. Guidance, margins, capital spending, order book, debt, risks
   the management names, questions analysts pressed.
3. **Store the facts in the dossier** with source, page and the filing's date. A shelf idea gets a
   dossier too, so its next deep dive reads them.
4. **A weekly budget** of documents, newest and largest holdings first, with the cost per document
   measured in the receipt.
5. **Shadow first:** two weeks of facts recorded before any analysis reads them, and ten of them
   checked by hand against the document.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every fact cites a document, a page and a date; a fact with no page is dropped.
- Nothing is dated after the run that read it; a reader "as of day D" sees only filings up to D.
- One document is read once (idempotent per filing).
- A document that cannot be fetched or read is counted and named; the run goes on.
- Living documents: KT sections 4 and 8, AGENTIC_DESIGN, the testing guide.
