## Why

Some losses can be seen coming in the accounts. An analyst checks a short list by habit. The
product has two such checks (promoter pledges and NSE's surveillance list) and no others.

**Example.** Reported profit ₹100 crore a year for three years; cash from operations ₹30 crore a
year. Profit that never turns into cash is the oldest warning there is.

## What exists

- `core/discovery/guards.py`: liquidity, price and circuit gates, NSE's surveillance report, and
  the pledge guard (`discovery.max_promoter_pledge_pct`, `discovery.pledge_guard`).
- `DiscoveryCandidate.flags` (`src/backend/shared/schemas/discovery.py`): a list a check can add to.
- Insider trades and pledges as events (PR-011, `core/discovery/insider.py`, `core/events/`).

## What to build

Fixed rules, no AI, from PR-021's store, each "as known on day D":

1. Cash from operations under 60% of net profit over three years.
2. Receivables growing 15 points faster than sales for two years.
3. Other income above 30% of profit before tax.
4. Debt rising while cash from operations is negative for two years.
5. An auditor's resignation or a qualified opinion in the last 12 months (from NSE filings).

The thresholds above are starting values. Set them from a measurement on the stocks the store
covers, and write the final ones in the receipt.

**Record first, block later.** Each flag is stored on the idea and shown in the deep dive's
context, the written case and the brief. No flag stops a buy until [PR-017](PR-017.md)'s grades
show that flagged ideas did worse (30 or more flagged ideas, 2 points or more worse at 30
sessions). Then the owner decides, flag by flag.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each rule has a fixture on each side of its threshold and a "data missing" case that raises no
  flag and says why.
- A bank or lender is exempt from rules that do not fit its accounts, by name of rule.
- A flag is dated: it reads only figures public on the day it was raised.
- Living documents: KT section 8, the testing guide.
