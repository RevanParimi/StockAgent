## Why

The live book produces a few dozen trades a year. Testing "should the entry band be 2% or 5%?" on
live results takes months per answer. Replaying the rules over stored history answers it in hours.

**What can and cannot be replayed.** The screen, the entry rule, the sizing, the stops and the
costs are fixed rules: they can be replayed honestly. The deep dive's verdict cannot: an AI model
asked today about March 2025 already knows what happened next. So the replay tests the rules
around the analysis, not the analysis.

## What exists

- `services/data/stores/eod_store.py`: one parquet file per trading day (open, high, low, close,
  volume, delivery); `discovery.history_days: 550`, about 2.2 years.
- `core/discovery/signals.py`, `screen.py`, `guards.py`: the screen, with no AI.
- `core/portfolio/sizing.py`, `deployment.py`, `advisor.py` (stops: `compute_stop_pct`),
  `costs.py`: the rules to replay.
- `core/intelligence/rl/eval/harness.py`: a replay of recorded forecasts. It measures the frozen
  learner, not these rules. Do not extend it; it is a different question.
- No portfolio replay exists (a search for "backtest" finds none).

## What to build

1. **A read-only harness** that walks the stored days in order. On each day it may read only data
   dated on or before that day. It runs the screen weekly, treats the top candidates as ideas (or
   uses [PR-017](PR-017.md)'s recorded verdicts where they exist), applies the entry rule, sizing,
   stops and costs, trades at the next session's open (PR-016), and tracks the book against the
   Nifty.
2. **A rule set as input**, so two versions can be compared: band width, stop multiple, new
   positions a day, sector cap, signal weights.
3. **Output:** return against the Nifty, worst fall, turnover, costs, trades, for each version,
   with the period and n.
4. **An honest label on every result:** "rules only; the analysis is not replayed", and the first
   and last day used. Tune on the first two thirds of the history, report on the last third.

The paused factor engine (SA-045 to SA-047) would plug in here as a replayable stand-in for the
analysis. That is the owner's later decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- An invariant test: no read of a day later than the day being simulated (a guard that raises).
- The replay of the live rules over the weeks since 16 Nov 2026 lands near the live book; the
  receipt explains each difference.
- Survivors only is a known bias: a stock delisted since is in the history on the days it traded.
- Nothing writes to a production store.
- Living documents: KT section 13, the testing guide.
