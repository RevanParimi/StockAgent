# PR-027 — Holdings that fall together

Priority: **P2** · Scope: **paused** (owner decision of 2026-11-15, edge roadmap, stage 5) ·
Estimate: **2 points** · Lane: **light** for the report; a rule that stops a buy is on the
**decision path**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependency: [PR-014](PR-014.md).
**Starts when** the book has held 10 or more stocks for 4 weeks and the owner promotes it.

**New conversation? Read first:** [EDGE-ROADMAP.md](../EDGE-ROADMAP.md) sections 1 to 3 and 6.

## Why

Twelve stocks that all fall on the same day are one bet, not twelve. The screen favours rising
stocks, and rising stocks often rise for the same reason. The caps in place look at one stock and
one sector at a time.

**Example.** Twelve holdings in nine sectors, each under the caps. Eight of them are mid-cap
momentum names. On a day mid-caps fall 4%, the book falls nearly 4%, as if it held one stock.

## What exists

- `core/portfolio/sizing.py`: the position cap (`advisor.max_position_pct: 10`) and the sector cap
  (`advisor.max_sector_pct: 30`), on total equity, for every autopilot buy.
- `discovery.max_ideas_per_sector: 3` on the shelf.
- `services/data/stores/eod_store.py`: daily closes for the returns.
- Nothing measures how holdings move together (a search for "correlation" in the portfolio code
  finds nothing).

## What to build

1. **A weekly measurement** from 60 sessions of daily returns: the average correlation between
   pairs of holdings, the largest group of holdings that move together and its share of equity, and
   how much the book moves when the Nifty moves 1%.
2. **Two scorecard lines** (`core/portfolio/results.py`), in plain words: "Your 12 holdings behave
   like about 5 separate bets. The largest group that moves together is 38% of the book."
3. **A flag on each buy candidate**, recorded only: "would raise the largest group above 40%".
4. **No rule acts** until 8 weeks of the flag are recorded and the owner decides, with the
   flagged buys' results beside the unflagged ones.

## Acceptance criteria

- Fixtures: twelve identical series read as one bet; twelve unrelated series as about twelve.
- A holding with under 30 sessions of history is left out and named.
- The measurement reads only closes up to the review session.
- Living documents: KT section 6, AUTOPILOT_GUIDE, the testing guide.
