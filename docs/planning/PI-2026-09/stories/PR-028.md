# PR-028 — A loss limit for the whole book

Priority: **P2** · Scope: **paused** (owner decision of 2026-11-15, edge roadmap, stage 5) ·
Estimate: **2 points** · Lane: **decision path** (it stops buying; fresh-session review)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependency: [PR-014](PR-014.md).
**Starts when** the book has held 10 or more stocks for 4 weeks and the owner promotes it.

**New conversation? Read first:** [EDGE-ROADMAP.md](../EDGE-ROADMAP.md) sections 1 to 3 and 6.

## Why

Each stock has its own stop. Nothing watches the book as a whole. Twelve positions can each sit
just above their stops while the book is down 9%, and the product goes on opening new positions.
A fund has a line at which it stops adding risk.

**Example.** The book peaks at ₹11.0 L and falls to ₹9.9 L, down 10%. No single stop has fired. The
market regime still reads NORMAL, so tomorrow's run buys two more stocks.

## What exists

- `core/portfolio/deployment.py`: `target` and `stop_reason` decide whether another position opens.
  The only market-wide input is the regime label (`autopilot.deploy_target_invested_pct`: 80% in
  calm regimes, 50% in RISK_OFF, none in MACRO_CRISIS).
- `core/intelligence/regime/state.py`: the sticky regime label, from VIX and the Nifty's move.
- `data/portfolio/<user>/value_history.jsonl`: the book's value by day.
- `core/portfolio/advisor.py`: per-stock stops scaled by volatility.

## What to build

1. **Measure:** the book's fall from its highest value so far, each day, in the digest and on the
   scorecard.
2. **A rule, recorded only at first:** when the book is X% below its peak, no new position opens
   until it has recovered half of the fall. Nothing is sold by this rule; the stops still act.
3. **Choose X from evidence**, not by feel: [PR-026](PR-026.md)'s replay if it exists, else the
   book's own history. Until then record what X = 10% would have done.
4. **The owner switches it on** after reading 8 weeks of the record. One setting, per portfolio
   (`portfolio.overrides`), off by default.
5. The run's record says so in words: "book 10.2% below its peak of 3 Feb: no new positions".

## Acceptance criteria

- An invariant test: the rule never sells and never blocks a SELL.
- A deposit or withdrawal does not count as a rise or a fall (the money-weighted series in
  `core/portfolio/results.py` already handles flows).
- With the setting off, behaviour is byte-identical to today's (a test).
