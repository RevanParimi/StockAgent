# PR-030 — Which signal earns the money

Priority: **P2** · Scope: **paused** (owner decision of 2026-11-15, edge roadmap, stage 6) ·
Estimate: **3 points** · Lane: **light** (a report; it changes no weight)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependency: [PR-017](PR-017.md).

**New conversation? Read first:** [EDGE-ROADMAP.md](../EDGE-ROADMAP.md) sections 1 to 3 and 6.

## Why

The screen blends several signals with fixed weights, and the analysis blends several dimensions.
Nobody knows which of them point at stocks that go on to beat the Nifty, and which are noise.

This is the honest form of "learning". The product once adjusted its weights by itself every day;
that was found unreliable and frozen (SA-039), and its rebuild is off the plan. Here a person reads
a report on hundreds of graded ideas and changes a weight by hand, then checks it on later ideas.

**Example.** "Insider buying, top third against bottom third: +2.8 points at 30 sessions (n 104
each). Volume breakout: −0.3 points (n 98 each)." The first earns its weight; the second does not.

## What exists

- `config.yaml` `discovery.signal_weights`: momentum 0.30, delivery surge 0.15, volume breakout
  0.15, bulk and block deals 0.15, 52-week-high strength 0.10, insider buying 0.10.
- `data/discovery/screens/<date>_screen.json`: each candidate's `signal_ranks`
  (`ScreenResult`, `src/backend/shared/schemas/discovery.py`). Nothing reads them after the screen.
- `services/data/stores/score_store.py`: each analysis's score per dimension.
- `core/audit/attribution.py`: why SWITCH decisions went wrong. SWITCH only.
- PR-017's `dive` and `screen` lanes: the graded outcomes to join to.

## What to build

1. **Join** each graded idea to its signal ranks and its dimension scores, as they were on the day.
2. **For each signal and each dimension:** the excess at 30 and 90 sessions of the top third
   against the bottom third, with n; and the same within BUY ideas only.
3. **A monthly report** for the owner, plain sentences, strongest first, "too few to judge" under
   30 a side.
4. **A written rule for changing a weight:** the owner changes it, in `config.yaml`, by a reviewed
   commit; the report then shows that signal's result on ideas issued **after** the change, kept
   apart from the ideas that suggested it.
5. **No automatic adjustment**, here or as a follow-up.

## Acceptance criteria

- A signal that was missing for an idea (a "dark" signal) is left out of that signal's row, not
  counted as zero.
- The ranks and scores joined are the ones stored on the idea's own day.
- Ideas before and after a weight change are reported apart.
