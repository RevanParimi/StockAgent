## Why

A paper record shows the idea works on paper. Anyone weighing the product will discount it until
some real money has followed the same decisions and the two records agree. A small mirror, with
orders placed by hand, proves the decisions can be acted on, at what price, and with what effort.

**Example.** Paper book +6.0% in three months. Real mirror, same decisions, +5.1%. The 0.9 points
are real prices and costs. That pair of numbers is worth more than either alone.

## What this is not

- **Not a broker integration.** The product places no order. `core/portfolio/autopilot.py` makes
  no broker call anywhere, and that stays.
- **Not for anyone but the owner.** The compliance checklist is plain: running an autopilot for
  other people is never done without licences.

## What exists

- The daily digest lists the day's decisions (`core/portfolio/digest.py`).
- `POST /portfolio/holdings` and the CSV import (`services/api/routes/portfolio_api.py`) record a
  user's own trades.
- Per-user portfolios (`data/portfolio/<user>/`), so a second book can sit beside the paper one.

## What to build

1. **An order list each morning before the open**: what to buy and sell, how many shares, from the
   decisions already made on the last close. This needs the decisions ready before 09:15 IST, so
   check first whether the daily run can move before the open (see PR-016's finding).
2. **The owner places the orders** in their own broker account and enters the fills (price,
   quantity, time), by form or CSV.
3. **A second book, "real"**, beside "paper", for the same account, with its own ledger.
4. **A weekly line:** real against paper, and where the gap came from (price, an order not placed,
   a partial fill, costs).
5. **A small amount** the owner chooses, and a written stop: the mirror ends if it trails the paper
   book by more than a set amount over 8 weeks.

## Acceptance criteria

- The real book never writes to the paper book's files, and the reverse.
- A day with no fills entered reads "not placed", never "filled at the paper price".
- The order list shows whole shares and never asks for more than the cash entered.
- Living documents: KT sections 6 and 10, AUTOPILOT_GUIDE, LEGAL_AND_COMPLIANCE.md, the testing
  guide.
