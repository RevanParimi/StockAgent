## The gap, in one example

A cement company and a hospital chain are both analysed on the general path (every stock outside
automobiles, banking, IT and renewables). Today both get the same sector search, "India stock
market {month} {year} sector news policy demand", and a macro search that reads "India **generic**
sector macro economy INR USD crude oil RBI ...". Neither search mentions cement prices or hospital
occupancy. The prompt then tells the analyst to "first infer the company's industry from the
bundle".

The four sector paths do better only because someone wrote their search words by hand. Example,
banking: "India banking sector RBI policy credit growth banking regulation news {month} {year}".

45 of the 56 ideas in the shelf file came from the general path, and 382 of 1,361 analyses in 60
days ran on it (measured 15 Nov 2026).

## What exists (do not build it again)

- **Web browsing in every analysis**, on every path
  (`services/data/context/bundle_builder.py` `build_sector_bundle`):
  - three web searches (company news, sector and policy news, macro news). Each returns five
    results, and the analyst sees each result's title and snippet, limited to the last month
    (`services/data/fetchers/news.py` `fetch_news_result`; `data_fetch.news_articles_per_query: 5`,
    `news_search_recency: qdr:m`);
  - one page extract, cut to 600 characters, cached for the calendar month
    (`services/clients/tavily_fetcher.py`; `data_fetch.tavily_max_content_chars: 600`).
- **The search words per path**: the table `_SECTOR_BUNDLE_CFG` in the same file, one row for each
  of the five paths.
- **The stock's industry**: Yahoo Finance's label, the product's one industry source since FIX-011.
  The peers step reads it from an on-disk profile cache (`services/data/context/peers.py`
  `choose_peers`, `PeerChoice.industry`), and the deep dive reads it through
  `services/data/fetchers/surveillance.py` `market_classification`.
- **The adopted design.** Section 4.1 of the lenses design: sector knowledge as data, one small file
  per industry group with `kpis`, `news_terms`, `policy_query` and `macro_drivers`. SA-026 is the
  card for all of it and stays paused. **This card builds only the part the gap needs, in SA-026's
  file shape, so SA-026 later extends these files and never makes a second format.**

## The design

### 1. A lens is one small data file per industry group

`config/lenses/<id>.yaml`. The fields this card uses:

```yaml
id: cement
display: Cement
industries: ["Building Materials"]      # Yahoo industry labels this lens covers
kpis:                                   # the few numbers that move this industry's profits
  - realisation per tonne
  - sales volume growth
  - power and fuel cost per tonne
  - capacity utilisation and new capacity
news_terms: "results volumes realisation prices capacity"
sector_query: "India cement prices demand volumes capacity news {month} {year}"
policy_query: "{company_name} cement plant capacity expansion results commentary {year}"
macro_drivers: [coal and petcoke prices, housing and infrastructure spending]
```

The values above are an illustration; the card's author did not check them against the industry.

### 2. Finding a stock's lens

One function (suggested: `services/data/context/lenses.py` `lens_for`):

1. The stock's Yahoo industry label, from the same profile the peers step already reads. **One
   lookup per bundle, shared with the peers step; no new network call.**
2. The lens whose `industries` lists that label.
3. No such lens: the **default lens**, whose wording is built from the label itself. Example:
   "India Medical Care Facilities industry demand prices policy news {month} {year}".
4. No label at all: today's sector-search wording.

The September design resolved a lens from NSE's industry field. NSE's per-symbol API has since gone
(FIX-011), so this card uses the Yahoo label.

### 3. The same four web calls, worded from the lens

| Call | Today, general path | With a lens | With the default lens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company news | "{company} {ticker} latest news results guidance outlook {Month} {Year}" | "... latest news {news_terms} {Month} {Year}" | unchanged |
| Sector news | "India stock market {month} {year} sector news policy demand" | the lens's `sector_query` | built from the industry label |
| Macro news | "India generic sector macro economy INR USD crude oil RBI {Month} {Year}" | "India {macro_drivers} economy news {Month} {Year}" | "India economy inflation RBI rupee crude oil news {Month} {Year}" |
| Page extract | "{company_name} NSE {ticker} results outlook policy regulation {year}" | the lens's `policy_query` | unchanged |

No query ever contains the path's name. The number of calls does not change.

### 4. The analyst is told the industry

A short block at the top of the bundle text for general-path stocks, built from the lens:

> Industry: Cement (Yahoo: Building Materials). What usually moves this industry's profits:
> realisation per tonne; sales volume growth; power and fuel cost per tonne; capacity utilisation
> and new capacity. Macro items that matter: coal and petcoke prices; housing and infrastructure
> spending. Judge the stock on these where the bundle has evidence. A number the bundle does not
> contain stays unknown.

This is bundle text, not a change to any prompt file. If it is added as a new bundle section, it
must not count as an essential section for the data gate, and every reader that counts sections
(the data-health row, `has_real_data`) is checked.

### 5. Lenses are written offline and reviewed, never at run time

- A script drafts a lens for an industry with one model call from a fixed brief. The reviewing
  conversation reads every draft. Only committed files are used.
- **Why not let the model word the search during the analysis:** the same stock on the same day
  must get the same searches, and a record that can be audited needs its inputs to change only
  through reviewed commits (the reason live prompt editing was removed, CLEAN-004).
- **Which industries first:** count the Yahoo labels of the last 8 weeks' general-path deep dives
  (stored dives, or the shelf's events). Write lenses until at least 80% of those dives are
  covered; expect 12 to 20 files. Everything else uses the default lens.
- **A coverage line in the weekly discovery summary:** how many of the week's dives had a lens, the
  default, or no label, and which industries lacked a lens. That names the next lens to write.
- **Checked at startup:** required fields present; no industry label in two lenses; queries hold
  only the allowed placeholders.

### 6. The record says which lens was used

Each of the four sections' provenance records `lens: <id>`, `default` or `none`, so the data-health
row has it and PR-017's results can later be split by it.

### 7. Proof before it acts

One setting, `lenses.enabled`, default **off**. A bench first (raw output under `analysis_data/`,
a summary in the receipt): 20 general-path stocks from at least 8 industries, the same day, three
arms:

- **A** today's wording;
- **B** lens searches;
- **C** lens searches and the industry block.

Counted by script, not by eye: results returned per call; the share of results whose title or
snippet contains a lens KPI or news word; results repeated between the sector and macro searches;
the change in verdict and conviction against A.

**The rule, written in advance:**

- B is ready to switch on if the on-industry share rises by 20 points or more and no call returns
  fewer usable results.
- C goes with it unless the block alone moves conviction by more than 0.05 on average on stocks
  where the bundle gained no new evidence. That would be the block talking, not the evidence; the
  block then waits for PR-017's grades.
- "No benefit" is a valid result: the card closes with the setting off.

Also **report, without changing it**: what the page extract would hold at 2,000 characters instead
of 600 for the same 20 stocks. A later card changes the cap if that report supports it.

Switching `lenses.enabled` on in production is the owner's word, after the review.

## Acceptance criteria

- Lens lookup fixtures: a known label gives its lens; an unlisted label gives the default lens; no
  label gives today's sector wording. A malformed lens file, or a label in two lenses, fails at
  startup.
- An invariant test: with the setting on, for a general-path stock no query contains the word
  "generic", and each lens query contains the lens's words.
- With the setting off, every query on every path is byte-for-byte today's.
- The four sector paths' queries are byte-for-byte unchanged with the setting on or off.
- No extra network call per bundle (a test counts the profile lookups).
- The industry block is not an essential section; an analysis without it is still actionable.
- The bench summary, with n for every figure, and the rule's outcome.
- Living documents: KT (the data bundle), `docs/TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md` and its PDF, the testing
  guide, and one line in the lenses design saying its first slice exists.
