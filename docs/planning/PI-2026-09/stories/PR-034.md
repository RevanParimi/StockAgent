# PR-034 — A Sunday report on every open production watch, and no deployed story without one

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **owner request** of 2026-11-16 ("we need a mechanism to trigger weekly
once like on Sunday") · Estimate: **3 points** · Lane: **light** (an operations report; it changes
no buy, sell, size or grade)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependency: [PR-033](PR-033.md) (its
list is this card's input). Profit-path step: **measure**.

## Why

A story is accepted on its tests, deployed, and then "watched". Until PR-033 the watch was a
free-text note with no date, and 38 of them piled up unread ([PR-033](PR-033.md) has the count and
an example). PR-033 reads them once. This card makes the reading happen every week without anyone
remembering, and stops a new story from being deployed without a watch.

The check has to run inside the product. The evidence (trades, discovery runs, review logs) is on
the production volume; a chat session cannot read it without the owner's login.

## What already exists (reuse it)

- The watchdog: job `ops_watchdog`, 06:30 IST daily (`services/scheduler/python/scheduler.py`),
  `core/ops/watchdog/` (registry, checks, engine, runner). It reads `config/milestones.yaml`, runs
  a coded check per entry, and alerts the owner only (`emit_owner_alerts`). An entry past its
  deadline re-alerts every 7 days; a satisfied one reports once and goes quiet.
- `manual_confirmation`: an entry only a person can close.
- STATE `pending_production_checks`: the dated rows PR-033 wrote.

## What to build

1. **Watch entries in the registry.** Each open watch from PR-033's list becomes an entry in
   `config/milestones.yaml` with its story id, `since` date, `needed` occasions and deadline. A
   needs-eyes watch uses `manual_confirmation` with its guide case in the action text.
2. **One helper for the shape.** A watch's check counts **occasions** and **violations** since its
   date and returns the existing `CheckResult`, with both counts in `evidence`:
   - enough occasions and no violation: satisfied (passed);
   - any violation: failed, and the next 06:30 run alerts as critical, without waiting for Sunday;
   - too few occasions: pending (waiting).
   A new watch should be a few lines: which file, what counts as an occasion, what as a violation.
3. **The Sunday report.** One scheduler job at about 20:00 IST on Sunday, after the 18:00 weekly
   review and the 19:00 IPO deep dive. One message to the owner only:
   "Production watches: 21 passed, 14 waiting, 2 need your eyes, 1 failed", then one line per open
   watch with its counts ("FIX-021: 63 events, 0 redirect links, passed"). A watch past its
   deadline with too few occasions asks for a decision: extend with a reason, or close.
   The same content is written to `data/ops/watch_report.json` (counts and symbols only).
4. **Recording the week.** The week's first "continue" conversation records Sunday's report before
   its own phase: a pass becomes `verified` in STATE with the counts, and its registry entry is
   removed in the same commit; a fail becomes a fix card or a backlog entry (AGENTS.md rule). The
   owner pastes the message, or allows one read of the report file. Add this step to AGENTS.md
   "Continuing the active PI", step 1.
5. **The guard.** `scripts/ci/check_watches.py`, run in CI's guards job: it fails when a `done`
   story whose production status is not `verified` or `not_applicable` has neither a registry
   entry nor a dated row in `pending_production_checks`. The registry's own rule ("a new milestone
   lands in the same commit as the work that creates it") is then enforced, not remembered.

## Acceptance criteria

- Table tests for the shape: passed, failed, waiting, past deadline, and a missing or unreadable
  data file (which is `unknown`, never a pass).
- The Sunday message names every open watch once and reaches only the owner. A passed watch is
  reported once, then silent.
- A violation alerts at the next daily run.
- The report and the message hold counts and symbols only: no user id, email or raw log line.
- The guard fails on a fixture story with no watch, and passes on the repository.
- Living documents: KT section 9 (the job table and its count, which `check_kt_docs` compares with
  the scheduler), the KT's watchdog section, a case in `docs/TEAM_TESTING_GUIDE.md`, AGENTS.md
  step 1; rebuild the PDF.

## Rollout

The registry reaches production only on deploy; the `registry_is_current` check watches that. The
new job runs in a window with no other job. Deploy at the owner's word.
