## Why

The owner's paper book has been mostly cash for three months, and the owner calls that the
biggest failure so far. What is known on 17 Nov:

- Until 15 Nov the autopilot could open a position only as a SWITCH out of a holding. Buying with
  idle cash (SA-049) was built and stayed switched off until PR-014.
- **Monday 16 Nov, the first run with the switch on:** it bought one stock, COFORGE, ₹64,896.
  Holdings 2 → 3, invested 15.7% → 22.3%, cash ₹7,63,937 of ₹9,82,897
  ([receipt](../evidence/PR-014-rollout.md), "Step 7").
- **It stopped for lack of ideas.** The shelf had 20 active ideas on 15 Nov. The day's buy list
  (`core/discovery/candidates.py`) offered 2: COFORGE and UNIONBANK, which is already held. The
  other 18 were left out before the buy step, and no record says which rule left each one out.
- The settings are built for a ₹10 L book of 12 to 15 stocks (`config.yaml`):

  | Setting | Today | What it limits |
  |---|---|---|
  | `portfolio.target_holdings` | 12 | a position is 80% ÷ 12 = 6.7% of equity |
  | `portfolio.max_holdings` | 15 | no 16th stock |
  | `autopilot.deploy_max_new_per_day` | 2 | new positions a day |
  | `discovery.shelf_size` | 20 | active buy ideas |
  | `discovery.max_ideas_per_sector` | 3 | active ideas in one sector |
  | `discovery.deep_dive_count` | 20 | companies analysed a week, re-dives and 2 IPO slots included |
  | `discovery.max_candidates` | 40 | companies the weekly screen passes on |
  | `discovery.redive_after_days` | 30 | an older idea waits for its re-dive before it can be bought |
  | `portfolio.max_managed_tickers` | 40 | stocks with a daily review; a holding beyond it has none |
  | `events.max_symbols` | 60 | stocks whose filings and news are read twice a day |

The owner wants ₹1 crore of paper capital in 20 to 30 holdings, each with its analysis. The
ceilings above are known. Three numbers are not, and PR-036 and PR-037 need them.

## What to do

1. **One read-only probe**, `analysis_data/pr035/invest_probe.py` (ignored folder), counts,
   symbols and the paper book's own figures only; no user ids, emails, raw logs or environment.
   Dry-run it on fixtures, then run it once through `railway ssh` at the owner's word in that
   conversation (or hand the owner the command). It reads:
   - **The door.** For every review session since 13 Nov: each active shelf idea and the reason
     the buy list left it out, recomputed with the list's own rules from `shelf.json` (deep dive
     older than 30 days; dived after the session's close; verdict not a BUY; no sector key named;
     sold since) or its outcome at the buy step from the digest's `deployment` record (bought,
     above entry band, at or below invalidation, surveillance, no room, waiting for the daily
     limit). This is `PR-014-D1`'s amended read, brought forward; D1 itself stays on 1 Dec.
   - **The entry band** ([BACKLOG](../BACKLOG.md) B-3). Of the ideas that were BUYs with a fresh
     deep dive: how many sat above the top of their band (2% over the close the dive read), for
     how many sessions, and what each has returned since its dive.
   - **The yield.** For the last 8 Saturday discovery runs: companies screened, deep dives made
     (re-dives, IPO slots, new), how many reached the 0.55 floor as a BUY, how many were placed
     on the shelf, and how many were turned away by the shelf's size or the sector cap.
   - **The load.** Stocks in the daily review today (held, watched, original entries) against the
     cap of 40; the model and search cost of one deep dive and of one daily review, from the cost
     records in `telemetry.db`.
2. **Write the numbers into PR-036 and PR-037** in place of their provisional values: the pace,
   the shelf size, the weekly deep dives and what the wider settings cost a month.
3. **Name the door that kept the most money in cash**, in rupees, with one example idea. If it
   is the entry band, [PR-038](PR-038.md) moves ahead of PR-037.
4. **Give the owner dates:** from the measured yield, how many sessions to 20 holdings and to 25.

## Acceptance criteria

- The receipt lists, per session, every active idea with its reason or outcome, and the totals by
  reason; the yield of 8 Saturdays; the load and the two unit costs.
- PR-036 and PR-037 carry measured values, each marked measured or still provisional.
- Nothing in production is changed. Code inspection, the production read and inference are
  labelled apart.
- The full suite is not required (no code changes); the project guards that read documents pass.

## Out of scope

Changing any setting, rule or ledger. Those are PR-036, PR-037 and PR-038.
