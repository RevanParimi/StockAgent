# PR-036 — ₹1 crore of paper capital, 25 positions aimed for, 30 at most

Priority: **P0** · Scope: **owner request** of 2026-11-17 ("i want the investment to go 1 cr now,
with at least 20-30 investments") · Estimate: **3 points** · Lane: **the implementing conversation
names it.** Proposed: **light** (a rollout of numbers the owner set; the sizing and deployment
code was reviewed at these scales under SA-049, SA-050 and FIX-025 and does not change). If any
code beyond settings and tests has to change, it is the **decision path**.

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Depends on [PR-035](PR-035.md) (its
numbers replace the provisional ones below). Profit-path step: **invest**.

**New conversation? Read first:** PR-035's receipt, `core/portfolio/deployment.py` (its opening
text states the sizing rule), and `config.yaml` sections `portfolio`, `autopilot` and `advisor`.

## Why

The book is ₹10 L and its rules stop at 15 stocks of 6.7% each. The owner wants ₹1 crore in 20 to
30 stocks. On 6 Nov 2026 the owner kept the maximum of 15 only "for now", to be raised when
money is added; this is that moment.

## What changes

**1. The settings** (`config.yaml`; PR-035's read of 18 Nov marks each value measured, the
owner's figure, or still provisional):

| Setting | Today | New | Why | PR-035's read (18 Nov) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `portfolio.target_holdings` | 12 | **25** | a position asks for 80% ÷ 25 = 3.2% of equity | the owner's figure |
| `portfolio.max_holdings` | 15 | **30** | the owner's upper figure | the owner's figure |
| `portfolio.min_holdings` | 10 | **20** | the owner's lower figure; reported when not met, never forces a buy | the owner's figure |
| `autopilot.deploy_max_new_per_day` | 2 | **5** | 25 positions in 5 sessions at the earliest, so the money still goes in over a week of prices, not one day's | **measured need, chosen value:** on 16 Nov 8 ideas qualified, 2 were bought and 6 waited for the limit; a Saturday places about 7 new ideas (about 11 with PR-037), which 5 a day buys in 2 sessions |
| `portfolio.max_managed_tickers` | 40 | **60** | every holding needs its daily review; today 30 holdings plus the watched stocks would pass 40, and a holding beyond the cap has no review and so no sale | **measured:** 21 of 40 today (16 original entries, 5 held); 30 holdings and the 16 entries make 46 |
| `events.max_symbols` | 60 | **90** (provisional) | 30 holdings plus the shelf's ideas | **still provisional:** not read |

**Example.** Equity ₹1,00,00,000 in a calm regime: the target is ₹80,00,000 invested. A position
asks for ₹3,20,000, so 320 shares of a ₹1,000 stock. Twenty-five such positions reach the target;
whole-share rounding leaves it a little short, so a 26th may open, well inside the 30. In
`RISK_OFF` the target is ₹50,00,000, so buying stops at about 16 positions of the same size.
The position cap (10% of equity) and the sector cap (30%, nine positions of 3.2%) do not bind at
these sizes. The screen's liquidity floor is ₹5 crore traded a day, so ₹3.2 L is under 1% of a
day's trade in the thinnest stock it admits.

**2. The capital.** One `DEPOSIT` of **₹90,00,000** through the owner-only route
`POST /portfolio/deposit` (reference "paper capital to 1 crore, 17 Nov 2026 request"), so
`capital_in` reads ₹1,00,00,000. The route is idempotent on date, amount and reference, and the
Nifty comparison buys the same money's Nifty units at the close the first valuation holding it
reviews (`results.capital_flows`), so the book is judged fairly on the new money.

**3. The order of the rollout.** Settings first, deposit second. Example of the wrong order: the
deposit under today's settings makes a position 6.7% of ₹1 crore = ₹6,66,000, and twelve of them
fill the book before the new settings arrive; nothing would sell them down.

## Checks before the settings ship

- **The daily review's run time.** On 16 Nov the 16:30 job reviewed 18 stocks and finished at
  17:01. Read how long 40 to 60 would take against the jobs that follow it, and state the
  monthly model cost at 60 (PR-035's unit cost).
  **Measured by PR-035 (18 Nov):** about 1.7 minutes of wall time a stock (31 minutes for 18 on
  16 Nov; 36 minutes with 21 enabled on 17 Nov), so 60 would end about 18:15 IST if it scales
  in a line; a review costs a median $0.0189 (mean $0.0156), about $20 a month at 60 a day.
- **Every test that pins a shipped value** is updated on purpose, not loosened: the breadth
  settings in `tests/unit/test_deploy_idle_cash_sa049.py` and
  `tests/unit/test_deployment_follows_money_fix025.py`, `tests/unit/test_portfolio_settings.py`
  (the 40) and any other the suite names.
- **An independent invariant test at the new scale**, not shaped like the implementation: over
  random idea lists and prices at ₹1 crore, with the shipped settings read from `config.yaml`,
  after any run: no more than 30 holdings; no more than 5 new positions a day; no position over
  10% of equity and no sector over 30%; cash never under the floor; invested passes the regime's
  target by at most one position; nothing is ever sold to get there.
- Whether anything prints or prompts a fixed number of holdings (the brief, the digest, the home
  page, the advisor's narration): 30 rows must read correctly.

## Acceptance criteria

- The settings above are in `config.yaml` with the reason beside each; the checks are in the
  receipt with their results; the full suite and the project guards pass.
- KT sections 1, 6 and 11, AUTOPILOT_GUIDE and the testing guide describe a ₹1 crore book of 20
  to 30 holdings as the current design; the PDF is regenerated.
- **Rollout, each step at the owner's word:** push in a job-free window; after the deploy, the
  deposit (the owner runs the one command the receipt gives, or says so in that conversation);
  the next 16:30 run's `deployment` record is read: position ₹3.2 L, the day's limit 5.
- New dated rows in STATE `pending_production_checks`: the share invested and the holdings at 5
  and at 10 sessions after the deposit. `PR-014-D1`, `R1` and `R2` keep their dates; their
  baseline note says capital changed on the deposit day.

## Rollback

Set the old numbers and push; nothing is sold. A deposit is not reversed by a setting: the route
has no withdrawal, so the capital stays unless the owner asks for a dated withdrawal to be built.
