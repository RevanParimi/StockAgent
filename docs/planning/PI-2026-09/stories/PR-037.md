## Why

Every buy comes from a deep dive: the weekly screen ranks the market, the analyst studies the top
companies, and a company rated a BUY at 0.55 or more goes on the shelf, where the autopilot buys
from. That path is sized for 12 holdings:

- 20 analyst calls a week, and re-dives of ideas already on the shelf are served first, then 2
  IPO slots, then the screen's new companies;
- at most 20 active ideas, at most 3 in one sector;
- an idea older than 30 days cannot be bought until it is re-dived.

Twenty ideas cannot keep 25 to 30 positions filled. On 16 Nov only 2 of the 20 reached the buy
step. The fix is to analyse more companies, **not to lower the bar**: a stock still needs its own
deep dive and a BUY at the same 0.55.

## What changes (values provisional until PR-035's read)

| Setting | Today | New | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `discovery.shelf_size` | 20 | **45** | 30 holdings plus 15 ideas waiting |
| `discovery.max_ideas_per_sector` | 3 | **6** | PR-036's sector cap (30% of equity) allows nine positions of 3.2% |
| `discovery.deep_dive_count` | 20 | **45** | about 11 re-dives a week keep 45 ideas under 30 days old; 2 IPO slots; about 30 new companies |
| `discovery.max_candidates` | 40 | **60** | the screen must pass on more than the dives use |
| `discovery.shortlist_size` | 80 | **120** | the guard checks run on the shortlist before the cut to 60 |

**Example.** A Saturday with 45 ideas on the shelf, 12 of them older than 30 days and 1 sold
since its dive: 13 re-dives, 2 IPO slots, 30 new companies from the screen's ranks. If PR-035
measures that one new dive in four ends as a BUY, that Saturday adds about 7 ideas.

1. **New dives are not starved by re-dives.** Today re-dives may take the whole budget less the
   IPO slots. Reserve a floor for new companies (provisional: a third of the budget), and report
   the re-dives it defers, oldest first the following week. An idea deferred past 30 days stays
   unbuyable until its re-dive, as now.
2. **One catch-up run.** After the deploy, one discovery run at the owner's word
   (`POST /discovery/run`, the same entry point as Saturday's job) so the shelf fills in the same
   week, not over three Saturdays. Its dives are offered from the review of the next session's
   close, by the existing rule.
3. **The cost, stated before the deploy.** Deep dives a week × PR-035's unit cost, a month, at 20
   and at 45; the same for the daily reviews PR-036 adds.
4. **Tests.** The pinned values in `tests/unit/test_discovery_settings.py` and
   `tests/unit/test_idea_supply_pr004.py` change on purpose. Independent invariants: the week's
   analyst calls never pass the budget; new companies get their floor whenever the screen offers
   that many; no sector holds more than its cap of active ideas; an idea reaches the shelf only
   from its own deep dive at or above the floor with a BUY verdict; a re-dive that turns a stock
   down removes it.

## Acceptance criteria

  project guards pass; a fresh-session review accepts it.
- The receipt states the weekly and monthly cost at the new settings, measured unit cost times
  count, and the shelf's expected size after two Saturdays from the measured yield.
- KT section 8, PRODUCT_MAP and the testing guide describe the wider path; the PDF is regenerated.
- **Rollout at the owner's word:** push before a Saturday run where possible, then the one
  catch-up run. A dated row in STATE `pending_production_checks`: after two Saturdays, active
  ideas on the shelf, ideas offered a day, holdings.
