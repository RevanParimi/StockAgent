## Why

A deep dive sets an entry band from the close it read: 97% to 102% of that close
(`entry_levels`). The band then stays fixed until the idea's next deep dive, up to 30 days. The
autopilot buys only inside it (`entry_check`).

**Example.** A dive on Saturday reads a close of ₹1,000: the band is ₹970 to ₹1,020. The stock
closes at ₹1,035 on Tuesday and ₹1,120 two weeks later. Every run refuses it, "above entry
band", for as long as it keeps rising. A second idea that slips to ₹975 is bought. The weekly
screen ranks stocks by momentum, so the ideas it finds are the ones most likely to keep rising:
the band works against the screen. This is code inspection; how often it happened is PR-035's
count, and it has not been measured before.

## What to do

1. **Measure first, from the paper lane** (the shelf's ideas graded against the NIFTY 50, PR-014
   step 5's record): split every graded idea by where its price stood against the band on each
   session it was a fresh BUY. Compare the 10, 30 and 60 session returns, own and against the
   Nifty, of ideas that were above the band with those inside it, with counts.
2. **Predeclare the rule before reading the split.** Proposed: the band's top moves with the
   stock. A fresh BUY idea may be bought at up to **10% above the close its dive read**; beyond
   that it waits for its re-dive, which re-reads the price and the case. The bottom of the band
   and the invalidation level do not change. The owner may choose another limit from the table
   the measurement gives (2%, 5%, 10%, 15%, none): each row shows how many more ideas would have
   been bought and what they returned.
3. **Decide.** If ideas above the band did no worse than ideas inside it (count and mean, at 30
   sessions, n ≥ 15 each side), the wider limit ships. If they did worse, the band stays and the
   card closes with the table: "no benefit" is a valid result.
4. **Build** the chosen limit as one setting (`discovery.entry_band_top_pct`, default the chosen
   value; 2.0 restores today's rule), with the buy's note stating the price against the dive's
   close ("bought at ₹1,035, 3.5% above the ₹1,000 its analysis read").
5. **Tests.** Independent invariants: no buy above the limit; no buy at or below invalidation;
   the limit never changes an idea's verdict, conviction or freshness; with the setting at 2.0
   every decision equals today's on a recorded set of sessions.

## Acceptance criteria

- The receipt holds the split table with counts, the rule as predeclared, and the decision.
- If built: the setting, the tests above, the full suite, the guards, and a fresh-session review.
- KT section 6 and AUTOPILOT_GUIDE describe the entry rule as built; the PDF is regenerated.
- A dated row in STATE `pending_production_checks`: four weeks after the deploy, the ideas bought
  above the old 2% line and their return against those bought inside it.
