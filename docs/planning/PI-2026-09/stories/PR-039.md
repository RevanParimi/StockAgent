# PR-039 — Where the funnel lost the stocks that rose

## Why

The owner sees stocks that rose while the book sat in cash. Nothing in the product can say, for
one such stock, where it was lost. There are five places:

1. **Outside the screen's floors** (traded under ₹5 crore a day, free float under ₹500 crore,
   price under ₹20, pledge, circuit streak, SME).
2. **Inside the floors, ranked below the cut** by the screen's signals.
3. **Ranked, not analysed:** the week's 20 deep dives were used up.
4. **Analysed and turned down** (below 0.55, or not a BUY), or turned away by the shelf's size or
   the sector cap.
5. **On the shelf, never bought:** the buy list or the buy step refused it (PR-035's doors), or
   the switch was off.

Each place has a different fix. Without the split, widening the wrong one costs money and finds
nothing.

## What to do

1. **One report**, `scripts/missed_risers.py` (read-only; prices from the stored daily
   bhavcopy, the weekly screens under `data/discovery/screens/`, the dive memo, `shelf.json` and
   its events, the ledger). For a window (default: the last 60 sessions) it takes the 50 stocks
   that rose most among those inside the screen's floors at the window's start, and for each one
   names the first place above where it was lost, with the date and the reason on record.
2. **The same for the 50 that fell most**, so the report shows what the funnel kept the book out
   of as well as what it missed. A funnel that turns away both alike is not choosing.
3. **Totals by place**, and the rupees a 3.2% position in each missed riser would have made.
4. Run it once on production at the owner's word (read-only), and record the table. If the stored
   screens do not go back 60 sessions, say how far they go and use that.

## Acceptance criteria

- The receipt holds both tables and the totals by place, with the window, the counts and what
  could not be determined.
- The report states its limits in its own output: it is hindsight; a stock named as "missed"
  was not known to rise on the day it was lost; the window is one market period.
- Each place that lost more than a quarter of the risers gets one line naming the card that
  addresses it (PR-037 for places 3 and 4's size limits, PR-038 for place 5's band, PR-017 for
  the analyst's verdicts), or a new card with a money example if none does.
- The full suite and the project guards pass; PRODUCT_MAP lists the report.

## Out of scope

Changing the screen's signals, weights or floors. A change follows only from this table, as its
own card.
