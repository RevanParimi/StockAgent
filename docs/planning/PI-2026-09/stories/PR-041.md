# PR-041 — Grade the screen's cut forward before it is widened

Priority: **P2** · Scope: **planned**, written by [PR-039](PR-039.md)'s rule (a place 1 or 2 that
loses more than a quarter of the risers gets its own card, with a money example), 19 Nov 2026 ·

it. A change to the screen's signals, weights or floors would be on the decision path, as its own

candidates that were not dived; this card grades the stocks the screen never named. Profit-path

## Why

PR-039's report, run on production on 19 Nov 2026 (read-only), placed the 50 stocks that rose
most from 20 Aug to 18 Nov 2026. **19 of them were never among the screen's 40 candidates**
(place 2, "inside the floors, ranked below the cut"): 38%, over the report's quarter.

**Money example (hindsight, read).** A ₹3,20,000 position (3.2% of ₹1 crore) in each of those 19
would have made ₹24,31,810 (median +37.5%; RAMBHAJO +84.0%, MILKYMIST +62.8%, PARAGMILK +60.7%).
The obvious reaction is to widen the cut or reweight the screen. By the same hindsight that would
have lost money:

- The same place held **42 of the 50 stocks that fell most** (₹60,42,766 lost at one position
  each; JSWDULUX −91.6%, CORDELIA −90.9%, both with a restated close, so possibly a corporate
  action). Without the 301 stocks whose close was restated in the window, 43 of the 50 fallers
  and 23 of the 50 risers.
- Place 2 held **770 of the 1,012 stocks inside the floors (76%)**. They fell a median 9.6%, a
  mean 9.2%: about **₹29,400 lost on each ₹3,20,000 position** had the cut let one in at random.
- The stocks the screen did name did better after it first named them: median −5.9% (ranked,
  not analysed, n 112), −2.5% (analysed and turned down, n 71), +2.5% (shelved, never bought,
  n 36), against −9.7% for place 2. The NIFTY 50 rose 4.8% over the window (20 Aug to 17 Nov).

So in this window the cut chose: it kept out fallers about twice as often as risers. But that read
is hindsight over one period, and its horizons differ (place 2 from 21 Aug, the others from the
week the screen named them). A momentum screen also names a stock after it has risen. Nothing yet
grades the cut **forward**, from each screen's own date, so the question "should the cut be
wider?" has no clean answer, and a wrong answer costs about ₹29,400 a position.

## What to do

1. **One report**, read-only, beside PR-039's (`scripts/missed_risers.py --cut`, or its own
   script reusing `load_sources`, `floors_out` and `chained_return`). For each stored weekly
   screen (`data/discovery/screens/<date>_screen.json`, from 10 Jul 2026):
   - **the cut**: its 40 `candidates`;
   - **the rest**: every stock inside the floors as of the screen's date (`floors_out`, as PR-039
     reads them) that was neither a candidate nor rejected by the guards;
   - shown, not tested: the guards' `rejected`, and the candidates' rank halves (1 to 20, 21 to
     40).
2. **Entry** at the close of the first session after the Saturday run; returns chained on NSE's
   previous close (a split or bonus is not a move); horizons 10, 30 and 60 sessions, each counted
   only when matured. Both groups share their dates, so the market cancels; no index is needed.
3. **Per screen**, the mean and median return of each group and the cut's lead (cut minus rest);
   then, over the screens, the mean and median lead and the share of screens the cut led. Every
   figure with its n.
4. **Run it once on production at the owner's word** (read-only) and record the table in the
   receipt.

## The rule, fixed before the data (read at 30 sessions, every screen weighted equally)

- **The cut chooses** if the mean lead and the median lead are each above +1 point and the cut
  led in at least 60% of the screens. Close the card: the risers in place 2 are the price of a cut
  that keeps more fallers out, and the screen stays as it is.
- **The cut does not choose** if the mean lead is at or below 0. Write the card on the screen's
  signals, naming the signals (`signal_ranks`) that carried the candidates that trailed, with a
  money example.
- **Not yet decidable** in between, or with fewer than 8 screens matured at 30 sessions: re-read
  four weeks later, with the same rule.

## Acceptance criteria

- The receipt holds the per-screen table and the pooled leads at 10, 30 and 60 sessions, with
  the counts and the decision by the rule above.
- An invariant test on a made-up market: a cut that picks the stocks that later rise leads; a
  cut that picks at random does not; a stock rejected by the guards is in neither group; a bonus
  is not read as a fall.
- The report writes nothing and makes no network or model call (as PR-039's test holds it).
- The report states its limits in its own output (one market period; the floors outside the
  shortlist are the bhavcopy's and the listing's, as PR-039 says).
- The full suite and the project guards pass; PRODUCT_MAP lists the report.

## Out of scope

Changing the screen's signals, weights, floors or the number of candidates. A change follows only
from "the cut does not choose", as its own card on the decision path.
