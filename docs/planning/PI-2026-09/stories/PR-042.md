# PR-042 — The data gate stops actions built on incomplete data

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **planned**, the owner's word of 19 Nov 2026, about 11:35 IST ("decision
gate mode, do as per recommendation, feel like its good") · Estimate: **1 point** · Lane:
**light** (a rollout that changes one setting the owner has authorized, AGENTS.md step 4)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. No dependency. Profit-path step:
**invest** (no buy on data that did not arrive). Receipt: [evidence/PR-042.md](../evidence/PR-042.md).

## Why

[SA-003](SA-003.md) built the data gate and shipped it in `record`: it computes, for every
analysis, whether its prices, financials and peer valuations arrived usable, and records what it
would stop. On 1 Oct the switch to `enforce` was a NO-GO: 25 of 78 analyses (32%) would have
abstained, most of them four banks whose good results the fetcher misread
([the decision](../evidence/SA-003-enforce-decision-2026-10-01.md)). FIX-003 and FIX-002 fixed
those causes and are verified.

**Example.** On 14 Nov a deep dive rated JPL a BUY although its technicals and peer valuations
had failed to load. In `record` that verdict stands; in `enforce` it reads INSUFFICIENT DATA and
no shelf idea is made from it. Exits and trims are never blocked.

## The decision, by the 1 Oct rules

- **(a) More than 20% would abstain:** not met. 20 Oct to 19 Nov, 4 of 653 analyses (0.6%) had an
  essential input unusable.
- **(b) A spot check finds an abstain whose data was fine:** not met. All five recent abstains
  lacked the data at analysis time; the causes are upstream (BACKLOG B-18).
- 5 to 19 Nov, `enforce` would have stopped 2 BUY verdicts, 3 shelf ideas and 6 learning
  bookkeeping steps. Every active shelf idea (20) carries an actionable gate, so no switch
  destination is blocked by the switch-on.

## What was done

The Railway variable `DECISION_GATE_MODE=enforce` was set with `--skip-deploys` on 19 Nov at
12:41 IST. It takes effect at the next deploy: the runner's push in the Fri 20 Nov 00:10 window.
`config.yaml` keeps `record` as the repository default.

**Rollback:** set the variable to `record` (or delete it); setting it redeploys, so in a
job-free window.
