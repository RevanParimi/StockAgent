# PR-043 — Exa beside Serper: two weeks recorded, not read

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **planned**, the owner's word of 19 Nov 2026 ("add them as new task and
re order them based on priority, so the autopilot can track"; Exa through the OpenRouter account,
checked by the owner) · Estimate: **3 points** · Lane: **light** (it records a second search
beside the first; no input the analysis reads changes, and no buy, sell, size or grade. If the
implementation must touch what the analyst reads, it is the decision path)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. No dependency. **Order:** before
PR-033: the two weeks start at its deploy. Profit-path step: **measure** (for invest).
[PR-044](PR-044.md) follows only if this card's read passes.

## Why

Each analysis reads three Serper searches (titles and two-line snippets from the last month) and
one Tavily page cut to 600 characters. PR-039's report (19 Nov): of the top 50 risers from 20 Aug
to 18 Nov, 13 were analysed and turned down (11 NEUTRAL), while the screen's cut chose well. A
fuller read of a company's news might change such verdicts. Before the analysis reads anything
new, record what each source would have given at the moment of each decision.

**Example.** A small company wins a ₹400 crore order on Tuesday. Serper's snippet says "shares
jump 8%" and names no order. Exa's excerpt carries the order's size and customer. If Exa finds
items like this for a third of decisions and Serper does not, it is worth reading; if not, the
test cost about $3.

## What exists

- `services/data/fetchers/news.py`: `company_news_query(ticker)`, `search_serper`;
  `services/data/context/bundle_builder.py` `_fetch_company_news` (one Serper search, five
  results, last month). Nothing stores the news text an analysis read; `data_health.jsonl` keeps
  only each section's status.
- Production volume: Serper about 3,900 calls a month (2,454 on 1-19 Nov); Tavily 124 (free
  tier).
- OpenRouter's server tool `openrouter:web_search`, `engine: "exa"`, `mode: "auto"`: $0.007 a
  search for up to 10 results, billed to OpenRouter credits (docs read 19 Nov 2026; the tool is
  marked beta and the older `web` plugin is deprecated). Results come back as `url_citation`
  annotations: URL, title and excerpts. The docs do not say whether uncited results or publish
  dates come back, and document no date filter for Exa.

## What to build

1. **Probe: done** (19 Nov, [evidence/PR-043.md](../evidence/PR-043.md)). Through OpenRouter
   every call returns all 10 results (URL, title, excerpts), cited or not, at about $0.0075 a
   call; no result carries a publish date and there is no date filter for Exa.
2. **Dates, our own** (owner, 19 Nov: "why do you need separate account", so no second account;
   everything bills to OpenRouter). For each Exa result, one plain request to its page, after the
   search, and the date taken in this order. **Strong:** the page's metadata
   (`article:published_time`, `datePublished` and their kin); a date in the URL; an NSE filing
   name's time (`_DDMMYYYYhhmmss_`). **Weak, kept apart:** a month in the path; the server's
   `Last-Modified`; a date in Exa's own excerpt (reported beside, not counted). A refusal or
   time-out leaves the result undated. Tested on the probe's 103
   links: 86 dated (news sites 28 of 28, exchange filings 16 of 16, company or broker pages 40 of
   45, quote pages 2 of 14), 68 of them strong (receipt, "Dates read from the pages").
   The end-to-end test on ten shelf ideas (receipt, "One end-to-end test"): 53 of 98 strong,
   22 more from the excerpt; every stock had a strong-dated, right-company item Serper's five
   lacked.
3. **Shadow:** in every deep dive and every daily review of a held stock, after the Serper
   company search, the same query through Exa (10 results), within a weekly budget of 200 calls.
   One row per decision in `data/logs/search_shadow.jsonl`: run id, ticker, time, Serper's five
   results (title, link, date, snippet) and Exa's (URL, title, excerpts, the date and where it
   came from). It never raises and never delays the analysis: it runs after the analysis call
   returns. A failure is a row with its reason. The analysis reads nothing new.
4. **A switch**, `search_shadow.enabled` in `config.yaml`, on at deploy; off is the rollback.
5. **Row `PR-043-W2`** in STATE: the read, 14 days after the deploy.

## The read (`PR-043-W2`), its rule fixed before any data

For each recorded decision: does Exa carry at least one item that is about the right company,
published in the 30 days before the decision by a strong date (step 2), and material (results, orders, guidance,
management, regulator, rating, capacity, a deal) that Serper's five did not? Ten decisions are
checked by hand against the pages.

- **Pass:** such an item in at least 30% of decisions; Exa's wrong-company rate at most 5 points
  above Serper's; cost under $10 a month. Then PR-044 may start.
- **Otherwise:** "no benefit" is a valid result (roadmap rule 5); switch the shadow off.
- **Under 30 decisions:** "too few to judge"; extend by one week.
- Items with only a weak date or none are reported beside, never counted toward the pass.

## Acceptance criteria

- The probe's answers are in the receipt, with the cost per call (done 19 Nov).
- Tests for the date reader: each strong and weak source on fixed pages and URLs, a refusal, a
  time-out, a page with two dates (the published one wins), no network in tests.
- Tests: the analysis prompt is byte-identical with the shadow on and off; a failed Exa call
  writes a row and raises nothing; the budget stops calls; no key or page text beyond the
  excerpts is written.
- The KT section on data gathering names the shadow, its switch and its file.
- After the deploy, the first Saturday run's rows are read: count, cost per call, failures.
