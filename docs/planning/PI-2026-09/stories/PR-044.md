# PR-044 — The deep dive reads the kept search engine's excerpts, graded before and after

Priority: **P2** · Scope: **planned**, the owner's word of 19 Nov 2026 (with PR-043) · Estimate:
**5 points** · Lane: **decision path** (it changes inputs the conviction is computed from;
fresh-session review)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Depends on [PR-043](PR-043.md) and
[PR-017](PR-017.md). **Starts when** `PR-043-W2` has passed its rule. PR-017 must be done so
the dives can be graded at their issued conviction before and after. Profit-path step:

## Why

If PR-043's read shows a second search engine finds dated, right-company, material news that
Serper's snippets miss, the deep dive should read it. The engine is the one `PR-043-W2` keeps:
Parallel `fast` or Exa, both through OpenRouter (PR-043's card, "The read"; renamed from "Exa's
excerpts" on 19 Nov, when Parallel joined the comparison). The 13 risers analysed and turned down in PR-039's window are the
kind of call it could change.

**Example.** A stock is rated NEUTRAL at 0.48 because the snippets show only a price jump. With
the order's size and customer in front of it, the analyst may rate it BUY at 0.60, over the
0.55 bar. Whether that helps is a grading question, not a feeling.

## What to build

1. The deep dive's company section reads the kept engine's top excerpts, each with its date and URL, beside
   Serper's snippets, within a fixed character cap. A result without a date, or dated after the
   decision, is not read.
2. The stored record keeps exactly what the analyst read, so a later reader can check any call.
3. PR-017's grades are split before and after the switch, with their counts.
4. Only after the deep dive: the daily review of held stocks, as a second step.

## Acceptance criteria

- The grades before and after, with n, on one page; under 30 a side, "too few to judge".
- "No benefit" ends the card (roadmap rule 5) and the switch goes off.
- Tests: an undated or later-dated item is never read; the character cap holds; the prompt with
  the switch off is byte-identical to before.
