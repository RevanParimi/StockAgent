# PR-045 — The trades on the portfolio screen: price, result and reason, and why a BUY was not bought

Priority: **P1** · Scope: **owner request** of 2026-11-21 (a manual session on the code-server,
about 05:40 IST) · Estimate: **2 points** · Lane: **light** (a screen; no route, setting or rule
changes)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: [SA-049](SA-049.md) (the
idle-cash record in the digest) and [SA-051](SA-051.md) (the trades' dates and the closed-trade
card). Profit-path step: **measure** (the owner sees what was bought and sold, at what price, with
what result and why).

## Why

The owner, 21 Nov 2026, from a phone: "i dont think any new buy was done on 20 Nov? But i see the
recent changes in analysed stocks have buy and sell on 20 Nov. Also i dont see the history on
stocks which rate were bought and sold, loss / profit, along with reason for it in the UI".

Three things on the Portfolio screen caused this:

1. The "Recent verdict changes" cards ("WAAREEENER: NEUTRAL → BUY", 20 Nov) are re-analyses, not
   trades, and nothing on them said so. A BUY verdict buys nothing by itself (KT section 6).
2. Why the review bought nothing (or only one stock) was kept every day in the digest's
   `deployment` record and sent in the digest's text, but never shown on a screen.
3. The trade list existed ("Recent activity": price, average cost, realized P/L, costs, a "why?"
   toggle), but at the bottom of the page, under ten tall verdict cards, with the reason hidden.

## What it does

On the Portfolio page, in this order under the holdings:

- **New buys at the last review:** the latest digest's `deployment`: bought what (or nothing), in
  stocks before and after against the regime's target, holdings before and after, why it stopped,
  and each idea looked at with its outcome label and sentence (six, then "Show all").
- **Trade history:** every trade from `GET /portfolio/transactions`, newest first: price with the
  date of the session it was booked at, "made" with the time; for a sale the average cost and
  "Profit"/"Loss" in rupees and per cent before costs; "Why bought:" or "Why sold:" with the
  ledger's own `reason` in view (three lines, "more" for the rest and the rule note). Tapping a
  stock lists only its trades and what the sales listed made.
- The closed-trade card, then the verdict changes, which now say under their count that a verdict
  is the analysis's view of a stock, not a trade.

## Acceptance criteria

- Browser tests: the card's figures, reasons and ideas from a digest that bought nothing, one that
  bought, one that failed, and none; the trade rows' prices, results and reasons, the honest
  lines with no reason kept, the per-stock filter, and the order on a phone.
- Every figure on screen is one an API sent; the existing portfolio tests pass unchanged.
- Living documents: KT sections 7, 10 and 12; testing guide case 11-N.
