# PR-046 — "Ask why" asks the card's own question; text cut short says so

Priority: **P2** · Scope: **owner request** of 2026-11-21 (the PR-045 conversation, about 06:15
IST) · Estimate: **1 point** · Lane: **light** (screens and the chat's stock tool; no buy, sell, size or grade reads either)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependency: [PR-045](PR-045.md).
Profit-path step: **measure** (the owner can ask about a verdict and read a trade's reason whole).

## Why

The owner, 21 Nov 2026: "on the stocks buy sell analysis, there seems 'ask why' but when i click,
it goes to that chatbot without any sync on what i clicked for"; and, on the trade reasons, "it
ends abrupt". A verdict card's button called `openChat()` with nothing, so the chat opened with
its greeting. A shelf idea's thesis is stored cut at 800 characters and a verdict card's at 160,
so both could stop mid-word ("… can reverse a").

## What it does

- `openChat(question)` (`index.html`) opens the chat and passes the question to `ChatOverlay`
  (`sphere.jsx`), which sends it once, as if typed. "Ask why" on a verdict card sends "Why did the
  analysis change OLAELEC's verdict from NEUTRAL to SELL on 20 Nov 2026? Explain in plain words what
  changed." (a forecast miss: "Explain the forecast miss on X on <date> in plain words."). Buttons
  that pass their click event still open the chat empty.
- The chat's stock tool, `get_stock_analysis`, returned only the recent verdicts and scores, so an
  answer could say the score moved but not why. It now adds the latest thesis and the thesis of
  the last run with another verdict (700 characters each, cut at a word), and its description
  says so. Cost: only when a person asks, about 400 more tokens a question.
- Text that does not end a sentence (a cut thesis) ends with "…" on the verdict cards and in the
  trade history.
- The service worker's version moves to v12, so phones drop the cached screens from before
  PR-045.
