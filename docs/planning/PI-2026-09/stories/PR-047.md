## Why

- **The band over the model.** With `rl.hard_bind_verdict_enabled`, the verdict is the composite's
  band (KT section 4, step 6). `verdict_wait` reads it; the shelf's floor reads the model's
  `final_score` (0.55). A model NEUTRAL at 0.62 passes both. In 60 days of
  `verdict_shadow.jsonl` the band said BUY 538 times; the model said NEUTRAL in 220 and SELL in 2.
- **The price.** `entry_levels` sets the band at 3% below to 2% above the dive day's close. The
  analysis's own buy zone is prose and nothing reads it.
