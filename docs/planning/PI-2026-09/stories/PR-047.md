## What it costs

Of the ten new positions bought from 13 to 18 Nov, besides DEEPA (FIX-033):

- **NITINSPIN** (₹683.65; the thesis: accumulate "on dips toward Rs 529-550"), **SOUTHBANK**

Together with DEEPA, ₹10.86 L of the ₹21 L invested. Whether these do worse is not yet known
(two to three sessions old); what is known is that each was bought against its own analysis.

## Why

- **The band over the model.** With `rl.hard_bind_verdict_enabled`, the verdict is the composite's
  band (KT section 4, step 6). `verdict_wait` reads it; the shelf's floor reads the model's
  `final_score` (0.55). A model NEUTRAL at 0.62 passes both. In 60 days of
  `verdict_shadow.jsonl` the band said BUY 538 times; the model said NEUTRAL in 220 and SELL in 2.
- **The price.** `entry_levels` sets the band at 3% below to 2% above the dive day's close. The
  analysis's own buy zone is prose and nothing reads it.

## The decision (21 Nov 2026)

The owner: "A now seems good but i want your analysis … I cant be watching always to do anything
manually. For now don't take me as a user." The analysis given in that conversation:

- **A fits what the product is.** Its edge, and what it shows a firm like Zerodha, is research that
  explains each buy. A buy whose own analysis says NEUTRAL ("the asymmetry is unfavourable at
  current levels") is the first thing a reviewer would open, and it contradicts the product's own
  record.
- **The band's override has lost its reason.** The verdict was bound to the composite (AUD-077) so
  that learned weights would drive it. Learning is observe-only and its removal was approved
  (CLEAN-003), so the band now re-weighs the same analysts with fixed default weights and
  overrules the conclusion the analysis reached with them.
- **A costs few buys.** Requiring both is an AND: it cannot add a BUY the model alone wanted (the
  free-form-verdict problem AUD-077 fixed stays fixed). Of the 10 buys of 13–18 Nov it removes 1.
  The 41% of band BUYs that are model NEUTRAL across all analyses mostly score below the shelf's
  0.55 anyway (an inference, to be counted in step 1).
- **B is right in spirit but untested in money.** A named "accumulate at" price is prose, often a
  support level the stock may not revisit; enforcing it can hold cash for weeks while the
  comparison counts that cash against the Nifty, in a book already 20.8% invested for lack of
  ideas. PR-038's read found these ideas trailed the Nifty wherever the price stood in its band.
  So B, and a staged-entry variant (half now, half at the named price; sizing, so PR-029's
  territory), are measured first.

**Decided:** option **A**, after FIX-033 (step 2 on `story/PR-047`, fresh review); **B measured
first** (step 1) and decided at `PR-014-R1` (14 Dec). Everything automatic: no step waits for a
hand.
