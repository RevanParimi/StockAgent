# SA-004 — Count daily-review outcomes truthfully

Priority: **P1** · Sprint: **1** · Provisional estimate: **3 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: none. Evidence: F09 in the [September audit](../../../audit/2026-09-10-repository-production-review.md).

## Intended result

Returning a no_envelope dictionary currently increments produced/succeeded counts. Separate completed, skipped, degraded and failed outcomes.

Introduce an explicit result status and aggregate required work separately from attempted work. Update completion logs and watchdog checks together.

## Starting points

- `services/scheduler/python/scheduler.py`
- `core/intelligence/rl/workflows/daily_review.py`
- `core/ops/watchdog`

## Acceptance criteria

- 20 attempts with one no_envelope yield 19 completed, one skipped and an incomplete required cohort.
- Exceptions and malformed results cannot produce pipeline_ok=true.
- Retries and intentional excluded symbols are reported without double-counting completion.
