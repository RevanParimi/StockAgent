`outbox.last_error`, an HTTPS Resend transport behind `EMAIL_TRANSPORT=auto`,
and per-account recipients via `resolve_recipient()`. **This card is not
accepted.** The work had no fresh-session review against the criteria above.

  loaded from `.env`. Two outbox tests fail without it; that isolation defect
  is routed to SA-005 (2026-09-22 DOC-001 review, F5). Re-establish the
  delivery test evidence in an environment without `.env`.

  bears directly on the "recipient isolation" evidence above.
- Resend's default shared sender (`RESEND_FROM`) delivers only to the Resend
  account owner's address. Per-account delivery needs a verified-domain sender.
