# SA-020 — Compute effective samples from trading-session overlap

Priority: **P1** · Sprint: **5** · Provisional estimate: **3 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: [SA-013](SA-013.md). Evidence: F15 in the [September audit](../../../audit/2026-09-10-repository-production-review.md).

## Intended result

Calendar-day spacing currently substitutes for trading-day horizons and can count overlapping outcomes as independent.

Use actual target start/end sessions for overlap filtering; report dependence by repeated instrument/pair and shared market period. State the assumptions of any interval or paired test.

## Acceptance criteria

- September 1 to 11 cannot certify independence for two 10-trading-session outcomes.
- Exact boundary, missing end date and exchange holiday behavior are explicit.
- n_effective never exceeds the admissible unique cohort and is not labeled fully independent without justification.
