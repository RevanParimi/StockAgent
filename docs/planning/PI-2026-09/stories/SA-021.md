# SA-021 — Validate calibration and weight recovery with minimum evidence

Priority: **P1** · Sprint: **5** · Provisional estimate: **5 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: [SA-016](SA-016.md), [SA-018](SA-018.md), [SA-020](SA-020.md). Evidence: F04, F16 in the [September audit](../../../audit/2026-09-10-repository-production-review.md).

## Intended result

Complex calibration/recovery modifiers are active without trustworthy comparative evidence. Validate behavior without promising improved accuracy.

Specify minimum effective samples, proper probabilistic scores where probabilities exist, interval coverage, recovery bounds and ablation comparisons. Keep unsupported modifiers disabled/dark under an explicit policy.

- `core/intelligence/rl/eval`

Paths are starting points; inspect current callers before editing. Keep this task bounded. If the implementation cannot fit a focused review, record a split with acceptance criteria in STATE.json before taking on another scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- One calibration observation cannot certify reliability or justify policy promotion.
- A zero-weight signal can recover only under the declared evidence and bounded-update rule.
- Results report losses and insufficient evidence as first-class outcomes.

## Unit and integration evidence

- Synthetic calibrated, overconfident, constant, sparse and regime-shift fixtures.
- Verify recovery cannot bypass SA-015 constraints and evaluation never treats arbitrary confidence scores as calibrated probabilities.

Use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports. Record exact commands, environment, results and any unsupported platform in the evidence receipt. Relevant existing tests plus these independent invariants are required; do not merely assert current implementation outputs.

## Hard review

Review independence assumptions and whether each complexity earns measurable incremental value. Reject tuning against the evaluation holdout.

Apply [REVIEW.md](../REVIEW.md) in a fresh conversation after implementation. No unresolved critical/high defect may be signed off. If the reviewer changes behavior, return to implementation and retest; a self-review is not the fresh-session review.
