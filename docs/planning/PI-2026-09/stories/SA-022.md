### Promotion gates for leaving `observe` (owner design, 2026-09-26)

The control is the default policy: the equal factor table (1/6 each) that `observe` uses once the factor engine is live. The treatment is [SA-043](SA-043.md)'s pooled shadow learner. The evidence is SA-043's paired records. **Only disagreements count**: when both policies give the same verdict from the same factor scores, neither can be right where the other is wrong. Each disagreement is graded with SA-012's target once it matures. Duplicates are collapsed with SA-020's effective-sample rule, so overlapping horizons on one ticker count once.

The report recommends leaving `observe` only when **all four** gates pass:

| Gate | Rule (predeclared; not retuned after outcomes are seen) |
|---|---|
| Enough evidence | At least **100 effective disagreements**, over at least **3 calendar months** since `evidence_start` and at least **3 lenses** |
| Better | The shadow is right on at least **60%** of effective disagreements. At n = 100 that is about two standard errors above a coin flip. |
| Consistent | The shadow wins in at least **2 of the last 3 months**, and dropping its best lens does not flip the result |
| Sane weights | No factor stays at its lower bound, or below half its default, for 4 consecutive weeks (the `technical` = 0.0 pattern); weights stay inside SA-015's bounds; the week-over-week change stays under a predeclared limit |

**Peeking guard.** Look at most once a month, on a fixed date, with the thresholds fixed in advance. Record every look. A pass must hold at **two consecutive monthly looks**.

**If rarely different.** If the policies seldom disagree, learning barely changes decisions. The report says so, and `observe` stays.

**On a pass.** The report recommends promotion; it never auto-promotes. The owner decides. Promotion happens at a month start (the monthly forecast runs on the 1st at 09:00 IST). `adapt` then resumes from the **shadow's** weights, which started at the defaults and learned on the corrected target, and never from the pre-fix stored weights, which are archived for forensics. That is a reviewed, owner-authorized data migration. This settles the SA-039 card's open question: `adapt` does not resume from the stored weights.

**After promotion: reverse tally.** The defaults become the comparison. If the adapted weights are right on fewer than **45%** of at least 30 effective disagreements in the first full month, return to `observe`. With fewer than 30, extend the check by one month.

Example: on 12 Nov TATAPOWER's factors are all 0.5 except Momentum at 0.78. The default weights give NEUTRAL (composite 0.547). The shadow, with Momentum at 0.25, gives BUY (0.570). The price rises past the target's threshold, so the shadow wins that disagreement. The monthly report adds up such results per lens and checks the gates.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every experiment observation links policy version and issue cutoff to one matured outcome.
- No holdout retuning, cherry-picked ticker deletion or repeated uncorrected promotion peeking.
- A report can conclude no benefit, harm or insufficient data without auto-promoting.
- The monthly report states each gate above with its numbers: effective disagreements, months, lenses, shadow win rate, per-month wins, the best-lens-removed result and the weight-sanity checks. It records the look date, and it recommends promotion only after two consecutive passing looks.
- Records from before SA-043's `evidence_start`, and pairs graded with any target other than SA-012's, are excluded and counted as excluded.

## Unit and integration evidence

- Small deterministic end-to-end experiment exercises issue -> mature -> pair -> score -> decision.
- Test missing lane, shared inputs, unfavorable result, low effective sample and rollback threshold.
- Gate fixtures derived by hand:
  - 100 effective disagreements with 60 shadow wins over 3 months and 3 lenses passes. With 59 wins it fails.
  - 100 disagreements confined to one lens fails.
  - 150 raw disagreements that SA-020 collapses to 80 effective fails.
  - A shadow factor weight held at 0.0 for 4 weeks fails, whatever its win rate.
  - One passing look alone does not recommend promotion.
  - The reverse tally: 13 of 30 (43%) returns to `observe`; 14 of 30 does not.

Use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports. Record exact commands, environment, results and any unsupported platform in the evidence receipt. Relevant existing tests plus these independent invariants are required; do not merely assert current implementation outputs.
