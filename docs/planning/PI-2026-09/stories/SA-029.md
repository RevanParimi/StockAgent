## Intended result

Health is unconditional and no Railway healthcheck path is configured. The localhost-port singleton is scoped to one container and does not guarantee takeover.

Keep the single-replica topology; add useful readiness/job-freshness signals and tested owner-loss recovery. Document graceful shutdown, persistent-volume checks and deployment health configuration.

## Acceptance criteria

- Readiness distinguishes booting/unusable storage from a working request process.
- Loss of the scheduler owner is detected and has a tested bounded recovery path.
- Replica scaling remains explicitly unsupported until cross-container exclusivity is proven.

## Unit and integration evidence

- Process-level test kills the owner while another worker survives, then verifies recovery without duplicate jobs.
- Test volume unavailable, stale heartbeat, startup race, shutdown and healthcheck behavior.

Use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports. Record exact commands, environment, results and any unsupported platform in the evidence receipt. Relevant existing tests plus these independent invariants are required; do not merely assert current implementation outputs.

## Hard review
