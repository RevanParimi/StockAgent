# SA-034 — Detect silent degradation in the watchdog

Priority: **P1** · Sprint: **2** · Provisional estimate: **5 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: SA-036. Evidence: §2.3(2) in the [2026-09-23 production assurance review](../../../audit/2026-09-23-production-assurance-review.md). Added 2026-09-23 at the user's request.

## Intended result

On 2026-09-23 the watchdog reported healthy while production was degraded, in three ways:
- The IPO refresh logged `degraded=False` although every bid-ladder fetch had failed.
- NSE and SONA captures went two days stale. `ipo_signals_accruing` fires only when an open issue has *no* snapshot.
- The Serper counter passed its budget. `serper_counter_current_month` checks only the month rollover.

Add checks that detect degradation, not just absence.

- `core/ops/watchdog/checks.py`
- `services/data/fetchers/ipo.py` (`refresh_ipo_cache` summary)
- `services/data/stores/api_usage.py`

Paths are starting points; inspect current callers before editing. Keep this task bounded. If the implementation cannot fit a focused review, record a split with acceptance criteria in STATE.json before taking on another scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- The IPO refresh reports degraded, with counts, when ladder fetches fail for open issues.
- An open issue whose newest capture is older than a configured number of refresh cycles raises a warning.
- A budget check warns at a configured percentage of each provider's monthly budget (Serper, Tavily).
- Critical-job-ran invariants: a due run of the brief, daily review, backup or IPO refresh with no SA-036 outcome raises a warning.
- Normal quiet states (no open IPO; a non-trading day; an issue's first morning) raise nothing. The weekly repeat cadence for lapsed items is reviewed for alarm fatigue.

## Unit and integration evidence

- Replay fixtures shaped like 2026-09-19..23: stale capture, 100% ladder failure, counter 2,590 against 2,500. Each raises exactly one alert.
- The quiet-month and opening-morning fixtures raise none.
- Thresholds come from `config.yaml` via `cfg()`.

Use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports. Record exact commands, environment, results and any unsupported platform in the evidence receipt. Relevant existing tests plus these independent invariants are required; do not merely assert current implementation outputs.
