# SA-034 — Detect silent degradation in the watchdog

Priority: **P1** · Sprint: **2** · Provisional estimate: **6 points** (5 → 6 on 2026-09-24, see "Widened" below)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: SA-036, SA-040. Evidence: §2.3(2) in the [2026-09-23 production assurance review](../../../audit/2026-09-23-production-assurance-review.md). Added 2026-09-23 at the user's request.

## Intended result

On 2026-09-23 the watchdog reported healthy while production was degraded, in three ways:
- The IPO refresh logged `degraded=False` although every bid-ladder fetch had failed.
- NSE and SONA captures went two days stale. `ipo_signals_accruing` fires only when an open issue has *no* snapshot.
- The Serper counter passed its budget. `serper_counter_current_month` checks only the month rollover.

Add checks that detect degradation, not just absence.

## Acceptance criteria

- The IPO refresh reports degraded, with counts, when ladder fetches fail for open issues.
- An open issue whose newest capture is older than a configured number of refresh cycles raises a warning.
- A budget check warns at a configured percentage of each provider's monthly budget (Serper, Tavily).
- Critical-job-ran invariants: a due run of the brief, daily review, backup or IPO refresh with no SA-036 outcome raises a warning.
- Normal quiet states (no open IPO; a non-trading day; an issue's first morning) raise nothing. The weekly repeat cadence for lapsed items is reviewed for alarm fatigue.

### Widened 2026-09-24 — production observability design (owner decision D5)

Per §5.2 of the [production observability design](../../../superpowers/specs/2026-09-24-production-observability-design.md), this story is the single evaluator for the ops ledger:

- `source_failing`: a source's success ratio from SA-040 is below a configured threshold over at least N attempts. For example, `smtp` at 0 of 12 today.
- `new_error_signature`: a WARNING+ signature from SA-040 appears for the first time in 30 days from a watched logger. At most one alert per signature per day.
- `credit_runway`: remaining credit ÷ average daily spend, from SA-035's `budget_readings`, falls below the configured days. A dark reading gives `unknown`, which warns and never reads as "$0".
- **Post-job evaluation**: when a critical job finishes, the SA-036 wrapper evaluates the checks scoped to that job at once. Detection then takes minutes, not the next 06:30 run. Example: the 17:45 IPO refresh evaluates `job_output`, `source_failing(nse.*)` and IPO staleness at about 17:45:10. The 06:30 run still evaluates everything, and both share the existing dedupe.
- Every alert names what broke, since when, and the first action to take.
