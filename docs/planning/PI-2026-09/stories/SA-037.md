# SA-037 — Keep deploys from dropping scheduled jobs

Priority: **P2** · Sprint: **2** · Provisional estimate: **3 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: SA-036. Evidence: §2.3(4) in the [2026-09-23 production assurance review](../../../audit/2026-09-23-production-assurance-review.md). Added 2026-09-23 at the user's request.

## Intended result

Every push redeploys, docs-only pushes included, because there is no watch-path rule. The scheduler uses APScheduler's in-memory jobstore, so a job whose fire time falls inside a deploy is lost. This was observed on 22 Sep, when `ipo_deep_dive` was due at 19:00 and the new container came up at 19:02. Builds take about 8 minutes or more (CUDA wheels; see SA-028).

Stop unnecessary deploys and recover or report missed critical runs.

## Starting points

- `railway.json` (new; watch patterns)
- `services/scheduler/python/scheduler.py` (startup)
- SA-036 outcomes

Paths are starting points; inspect current callers before editing. Keep this task bounded. If the implementation cannot fit a focused review, record a split with acceptance criteria in STATE.json before taking on another scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- A docs-only commit does not trigger a deploy; verify with one real docs-only push.
- At startup, a critical job whose fire time fell within its grace window, with no recorded outcome, runs once. Outside the window it is reported, not run.
- No double run when the old container already ran the job, and no duplicate brief or alert (respect the delivery dedupe).
- Deploy-window guidance around the critical job times is documented.

## Unit and integration evidence

- Unit tests for the catch-up decision: inside grace with no outcome, inside grace with an outcome, and outside grace.
- An idempotency test against delivery dedupe.

Use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports. Record exact commands, environment, results and any unsupported platform in the evidence receipt. Relevant existing tests plus these independent invariants are required; do not merely assert current implementation outputs.

## Hard review

A catch-up that can double-send a brief is worse than a missed run; prove it cannot.
