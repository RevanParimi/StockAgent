# SA-045 — Compute the five universal factors deterministically

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: [SA-026](SA-026.md) (lenses, peers, and SA-010's benchmark through it). ## Intended result

Today the LLM turns computed numbers back into scores. RSI, MACD, 52-week position and peer P/E are calculated, rendered as text, and then scored 0–1 by the model. This story computes five universal factors in code, the same way for every stock, **in shadow**: they are recorded beside every decision and used by none.

| Factor | Inputs available today | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **Value** | P/E, P/B, peer P/E | 50% lens-peer percentile, 50% own 5-year percentile, both inverted |
| **Quality** | EBITDA margin level and stability | Peer percentile. The factor is marked **partial** until RoE, leverage or cash flow are fetched; add them from yfinance statements if available, otherwise record the gap. |
| **Growth** | Revenue YoY/QoQ, EBITDA trend | Peer percentile |
| **Momentum** | 10-year daily prices, 52-week position, RSI/MACD, bulk deals, FII/DII | 6- and 12-month return relative to the **lens benchmark** (peer percentile), plus trend against the 200-day average |
| **Risk** (higher = safer) | Volatility, drawdown, surveillance flags, promoter holding | Inverse percentile of volatility and drawdown; a surveillance flag caps the score |

Each factor is a 0–1 score with `inputs`, `as_of` (the oldest input date used), `peer_count`, `partial` and `coverage`. With fewer than three usable peers, only the own-history percentile is used; with neither, the factor is **missing**, never 0.5. Inputs older than the lens's staleness limit make the factor missing.

## Acceptance criteria

- The same inputs and `as_of` always give identical factor records (no LLM, no randomness).
- Every factor uses its lens's peers and benchmark. A stock is never compared with another lens's peers, and never with the automobile index by default.
- A missing or stale input gives a missing factor with a reason, never a neutral 0.5.
- For each analysis the factor record is stored beside the decision, keyed by the decision's ID and issue cutoff. It uses no input dated after the cutoff (SA-013/SA-014).
- No live decision, verdict or report changes. Shadow only.

## Unit and integration evidence

- Hand-derived percentiles for small fixtures:
  - a stock with P/E 20 among peers at 10, 15, 25 and 30 gets Value from rank 3 of 5, inverted;
  - a 6-month return of +8% against a benchmark at +3%, among peers at 0%, 2%, 6% and 10%.
- A look-ahead guard: a price or result dated after the cutoff is ignored.
- Missing peers, missing history and stale inputs each produce the documented missing reason.
- Partial Quality is flagged, and coverage reflects it.
- A determinism test: two runs on the same frozen inputs are byte-identical.

Use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports. Record exact commands, environment, results and any unsupported platform in the evidence receipt. Relevant existing tests plus these independent invariants are required; do not merely assert current implementation outputs.

## Hard review
