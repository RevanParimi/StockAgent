## Intended result

This story turns the six factors into a decision, explains it, and proves the result against today's analyst before anything switches.

- **Decide:**
  - The composite is Σ wᶠ·sᶠ over the factors present, divided by Σ wᶠ over the same factors.
  - The default weights are **equal, 1/6 each**.
  - **Coverage below 4 of 6 factors gives no directional verdict** ("insufficient data").
  - Confidence is coverage × freshness.
  - The verdict comes from the existing `score_thresholds` bands, which are unchanged.
- **Explain:** the LLM receives the factor table, events and verdict, and writes the report. It cannot change the verdict. Quoted numbers must match the table; a check rejects invented ones.
- **Shadow comparison:** for every issue-time decision, store today's analyst verdict and the engine verdict side by side. Grade both with SA-012's target and score **only disagreements** with SA-020's effective counts.
- **Switch gate (non-inferiority):**
  - at least 100 effective disagreements over at least 2 months and at least 3 lenses;
  - the engine is right on **at least 50%** of them;
  - no lens with at least 20 disagreements is **below 40%**;
  - two consecutive monthly looks.

  The report recommends; the owner switches at a month start.
- **At the switch:**
  - `rl.learning_mode: observe` means the equal factor table; `adapt` means SA-022's promoted pooled table.
  - Envelopes and the review carry factor names.
  - Per-ticker `WeightMemory` files become history only.
  - Rollback is a flag back to today's analyst, which is kept until SA-027.

## Acceptance criteria

- The engine's verdict is a pure function of the stored factor records and weights. The narrator cannot alter it.
- Coverage below 4 of 6 factors produces the typed "insufficient data" result through both the API and scheduled paths.
- Paired records use the same issue-time decision for both engines, and no input after the cutoff.
- The monthly report shows every gate's numbers per lens and recommends only after two consecutive passes. It never switches anything itself.
- After a switch, forecast, review re-scoring and Step 7b run on factor names. A test pins that RL code handles the new keys: no automobile-named fallback and no 0.5 filling for missing names.
- Rollback to today's analyst restores the previous behaviour exactly.

## Unit and integration evidence

- The spec's worked example: factor scores 0.30, 0.62, 0.58, 0.72, 0.66 and 0.68 give composite 0.593, a BUY, computed by hand.
- Coverage: 3 of 6 factors present gives "insufficient data"; 4 of 6 gives a renormalised composite.
- Gate fixtures:
  - 100 effective disagreements with 50 engine wins over 2 months and 3 lenses passes;
  - 49 wins fails;
  - one lens at 7 of 20 (35%) fails;
  - a single passing look does not recommend.
- A narrator fixture with one invented number is rejected.
