# SA-048 — Charge realistic trading costs and tax in paper P&L

Priority: **P1** · Sprint: **3** · Provisional estimate: **3 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: none. Added 2026-09-27 at the owner's request, after reviewing the live portfolio screen: are the paper profits real?

## Intended result

The paper portfolio charges nothing to trade. `core/portfolio` has no brokerage, STT, stamp duty, exchange fee, GST, DP charge or tax (checked 2026-09-27: no match in the package). Realized P&L is the price difference × quantity. So every paper result reads better than a real account would, and quick in-and-out trades look free.

**Example, ACMESOLAR** (the owner's 27 Sep screen): 509 shares bought at ₹372.88 and sold at ₹446.65, realized **+₹37,550.79**. A real delivery round trip pays about:

| Charge | Basis | About |
|---|---|---:|
| STT | 0.1% of buy and of sell value | ₹417 |
| Stamp duty | 0.015% of buy value | ₹28 |
| Exchange + SEBI fees, GST on them | ≈0.003% of both, 18% GST | ₹15 |
| DP charge | per sell, per scrip | ₹16 |
| **Costs** | | **≈₹477** |
| Short-term capital gains tax | 20% (held under 12 months) | ≈₹7,415 |
| **In hand** | | **≈₹29,660**, not ₹37,551 |

These rates are this card's estimate. The implementation confirms current rates and puts them in config, each with the date it was checked.

- **Costs at trade time.** Each virtual BUY and SELL records its estimated costs and takes them from cash, as a broker would.
- **Realized P&L shows gross and net of costs.**
- **Tax is an estimate, shown separately and not taken from cash.** Short-term (under 12 months) and long-term gains are netted with losses within the financial year, and the long-term exemption is applied.
- **History is not rewritten.** Transactions made before this story get their costs estimated when read, and are labelled "estimated".

## Acceptance criteria

- The ACMESOLAR round trip above, computed by hand from the configured rates, matches the recorded costs to the paisa.
- Cash after a trade = cash before ± trade value − costs; equity reconciles.
- Realized P&L exposes `gross` and `net_of_costs`, and the old field keeps its meaning or is clearly renamed (additive).
- The tax estimate for a financial year nets short-term gains and losses, applies the long-term exemption once, and is 0 when the year's net is a loss.
- Every rate comes from config with an as-of date; changing a rate never changes past recorded costs.
- A pre-SA-048 transaction reads as "costs estimated", never as zero.

## Unit and integration evidence

- A hand-computed fixture per charge, one round trip, and a financial year with mixed short-term and long-term gains and losses, including a year that ends in a loss.
- A property test: over random buy/sell sequences, equity = cash + holdings, and total costs ≥ 0.
- The performance endpoint on a fixture ledger returns gross, net and tax, and they match the hand figures.

Use deterministic fixtures and blocked external transports. Record exact commands, environment, results and any unsupported platform in the evidence receipt. Relevant existing tests plus these independent invariants are required; do not merely assert current implementation outputs.

## Hard review

Check the rates against a primary source (exchange and depository circulars, the Finance Act), dated. Reject a hard-coded rate. Confirm a crash between the cost write and the cash write cannot charge twice (the same transaction-id rule as today).

## Rollout and rollback

Additive: new fields, and costs applied from the deploy on. Expect every future P&L to be a little lower; that is the point. Rollback is a revert: past records keep their recorded costs, labelled.

Local implementation completion and production verification are separate. Read-only inspection is authorized; this card does not authorize sending notifications, deploying, changing production variables, enabling policies, or applying destructive migrations.
