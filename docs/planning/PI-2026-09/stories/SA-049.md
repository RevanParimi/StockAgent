## Owner decision (2026-09-27)

> "Let's not just leave cash that way. There are multiple opportunities and that's what our product is all about. If it's some war and we have to sell most of them, I can understand, but when the market is decent and we are not able to buy anything and make profit out of it … then it's really disappointing."

So: **in a normal market, idle cash goes into the best available ideas. Cash is held on purpose only in a crisis**, or when no idea passes the checks, and then the reason is recorded.

## Owner request (2026-10-11): a breadth floor

> "The portfolio is very diluted, only 2 stocks are present … I need at least 10 stocks to be invested and the rotation should not be less than 10L investment on current portfolio. Make these 10 stocks and 10 L configurable in config file … (not strict 10, it is 10+ at least)."

Source: section 4 of the [product results review](../../../audit/2026-10-11-product-results-review.md). The deployment step also works toward a **floor**, configurable, with an optional per-portfolio override for users with other needs:

```yaml
portfolio:
  min_holdings: 10          # at least, not exactly
  target_holdings: 12       # what deployment aims for (owner, 12 Oct)
  max_holdings: 15          # stops over-dilution
  min_invested_inr: 1000000 # ₹10 L working in a normal market
```

- **Target position size** = `min_invested_inr / target_holdings` (₹83,000 with the 12 Oct decisions below), always within `max_position_pct` and the sector cap (SA-050).
- **In NORMAL-like regimes** the deployment step buys until holdings ≥ `min_holdings` **and** invested ≥ `min_invested_inr`, then continues toward the regime's target share. The per-day limit N still applies.
- **RISK_OFF:** the floor relaxes to the regime target. **MACRO_CRISIS:** no new positions, floor suspended, reason recorded.
- **An unreachable floor is recorded, never forced.** Examples: "only 6 qualifying ideas"; "equity ₹9.9 L below floor plus cash buffer". No cap is breached to meet it.
- **The arithmetic (decided 12 Oct: A; see Owner decisions below).** Capital is about ₹10 L, so ₹10 L invested would mean 100% invested with no cash for rotation or the floor. **(A, recommended)** add ₹2 L of paper capital (₹12 L): 10 × ₹1 L invested (83%), ₹2 L for rotation, each position 8.3%. SA-051 counts the deposit on its own date. **(B)** keep ₹10 L and set `min_invested_inr: 900000`.
- **Idea supply** comes from [PR-004](PR-004.md) (20-idea shelf, 3 per sector, watchlist BUYs); order it before this story.
- **Churn watch.** The current exit rule sells on price-path misses (review section 1). With 10+ holdings, the weekly scorecard ([PR-002](PR-002.md)) and the exit shadow ([PR-009](PR-009.md)) get enough exits to judge that rule within weeks. Breadth is also how the product gets evidence.

## Intended result

Today (code inspection, `core/portfolio/autopilot.py`) the autopilot can open a new position only through a SWITCH. It sells a holding and puts that sale's proceeds into a shelf idea. An ADD only tops up a stock already held. A plain EXIT sends its proceeds to cash, and nothing ever reinvests them, so each plain exit shrinks the invested part.

**Observed 27 Sep** (the owner's screen): ₹8,09,297 of ₹9,99,611 was cash (81%), with 2 holdings. ACMESOLAR's `exit_full` alone moved about ₹2.27L to cash.

Add a deployment step to the autopilot, after sells and switches:

- **The market regime sets how much may be invested.** The sticky regime label (the same hysteresis the daily review uses) maps to a target invested share, from config:

  | Regime | Target invested share (proposal; the owner confirms at implementation) |
  |---|---|
  | NORMAL, RISK_ON, MOMENTUM_EXTENDED, OVERSOLD | 80% |
  | RISK_OFF | 50% |
  | MACRO_CRISIS | no new positions (hold cash) |

- **Candidates.** Active discovery-shelf ideas that are not already held. Each must pass:
  - SA-003's gate: an actionable deep dive and a close dated the session;
  - its own levels: price at or below `entry_high` and above `invalidation_level`.

  Candidates are ranked by conviction. Whether watchlist names with an actionable BUY also qualify is an open question for the owner at implementation.
- **Sizing: SA-050's rule.** Each new position is at most `max_position_pct` (10%), each sector at most its cap, and the cash floor is kept.
- **Staged entry.** At most N new positions per day (proposal: 2), so cash goes in over days rather than all at once.
- **Every buy says why.** The transaction note is `deploy_idle_cash`, with the idea, conviction, regime, and target vs actual invested share. The digest lists it.
- **Every non-buy says why.** When cash stays idle below target, the run records the reasons. Example: "regime RISK_OFF, target reached; 3 ideas above entry band; 1 idea abstained". Idle cash is then explained, never silent.
- **Behind a switch.** `autopilot.deploy_idle_cash` ships off. The owner switches it on at rollout.

**What this does and does not change.** It changes how much of the portfolio is working. It does not make the picks better: a bad idea bought with idle cash is still a bad idea. Pick quality is the analysis track (SA-003 enforce, SA-044–SA-047). SA-051 shows whether deployed cash beats the Nifty. On paper money, more deployment also brings closed trades sooner, which is the evidence the product needs either way.

## Acceptance criteria

- **The 27 Sep shape, in NORMAL.** 81% cash, 3 qualifying ideas, N = 2: the run buys 2 positions of at most 10% each, keeps the floor, and records the target (80%) and the new invested share. The next day it buys the third. Quantities are derived by hand.
- **MACRO_CRISIS:** no new-position buys, and the reason is recorded. EXIT and TRIM are unaffected.
- **Target reached:** no buy, and "target reached" is recorded.
- **No qualifying idea:** no buy, and each idea's reason is recorded (above band, invalidated, abstained, stale close, already held, cap).
- **SA-003 in enforce:** an abstained or unverified idea is never bought. In record mode, the note says what enforce would have withheld.
- **The same review date twice:** buys nothing twice (transaction ids, as today).
- **Switch off:** byte-identical behaviour to before.

## Routed input — 2026-10-18 SA-050 review (R1, R2)

This story reuses SA-050's `size_buy` and its skip reasons, so fix two small gaps here.

- **R1: whole shares at an exact boundary.** `size_buy` (`core/portfolio/sizing.py`) floors
  `budget / price` in floating point, then drops a share when `qty × price` exceeds the budget
  by rounding alone. An ADD of 144 shares at ₹6,358.80 asks for ₹2,28,916.80, exactly 36
  shares, and buys 35. Before SA-050 the floor alone already bought one share short in about
  2.1% of ADDs; the guard adds 0.2%. It never breaches a cap or the floor. Compare in paisa (or
  with a one-paisa tolerance) and add a test where the request is a whole number of shares.
- **R2: a second switch into the same stock is silent.** When two holdings exit on one day and
  both pick the same destination, the second buy leg is usually stopped by the position cap.
  Its SELL's note says why, but the pipeline's `switch_buy_skipped` alert treats any BUY of
  that symbol as "bought", so no alert fires. Match the buy leg to its own SELL (the shared
  `advice_ref`), or alert whenever the SELL's note carries a skip reason. Add a test with two
  exits and one destination.

See [the SA-050 review](../evidence/SA-050-review.md), findings R1 and R2.

## Routed input — 2026-10-19 SA-051 review (R1)

This story adds the dated `DEPOSIT` that SA-051's Nifty comparison dates money by, so close
one gap in how money in is valued here.

- **R1: a holding added while the 16:30 run is under way is valued two ways.**
  - The pipeline takes its closes from the portfolio it loaded at the start
    (`core/portfolio/pipeline.py`). The value point, written later under the lock, marks a
    holding with no close at its cost (`sizing.marked_value`).
  - `results.capital_flows` takes the same arrival in at the day's close.
  - **Example.** On a Friday, 1,000 shares bought at ₹50 and worth ₹150 are added at 16:35.
    The week reads −8.70%; the truth is 0.0%. Typed in at ₹150 and worth ₹50, they read
    +9.52%: ahead when the portfolio is behind.
  - The next valuation reverses it on the screen, but a weekly scorecard row is stored once.
    The same happens when an arrival's own close fails in its first run.
- **Fix, either of:**
  - the pipeline reloads the portfolio just before the value point and fetches closes for any
    holding that arrived during the run, outside the lock;
  - or the point records the holdings it marked at cost, and `capital_flows` treats such an
    arrival as the point does: at cost that day, with the jump to its first close counted as
    money in, never as gain.

  Either way, an arrival whose close is never found stays "could not check".
- **Test.** RV3's shape (`analysis_data/sa051_review/test_rv_trace.py`, the real pipeline with
  an add during narration) reads 0.0% against +2.56% for that week. The control, an add before
  the run, already does.

See [the SA-051 review](../evidence/SA-051-review.md), finding R1.

## Rollout and rollback

1. **Before switching on**, read the paper lane's record for shelf ideas: how the ideas did on paper against the Nifty. That is the best evidence available of what deployment will do.
2. **The owner switches on** `autopilot.deploy_idle_cash`, and confirms the regime targets and N.
3. **After one month**, read SA-051's comparison. Deployed cash is judged against the Nifty, not against the cash it replaced.
4. **Rollback:** switch off. Positions already bought stay, and normal EXIT and TRIM rules manage them.
