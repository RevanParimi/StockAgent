# SA-050 — Apply one sizing rule to every autopilot buy

Priority: **P1** · Sprint: **2** · Provisional estimate: **3 points** (2 → 3 on 2026-10-12: the denominator and entry-band additions below)

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: none. Added 2026-09-27 at the owner's request, after reviewing the live portfolio screen.

## Intended result

`core/portfolio/autopilot._execute_buys` sizes two kinds of buy differently:

- An **ADD** is capped at `advisor.max_position_pct` (10%) of the portfolio after the trade, and keeps the cash floor.
- A **SWITCH** buy puts the whole sale's proceeds into the new stock: `budget = min(proceeds, cash − floor)`. No position cap, no sector check.

**Example.** A holding worth ₹2.27L (22.7% of a ₹10L portfolio) is sold and switched. The new stock is then 22.7% of the portfolio, more than twice the cap. `advisor.sector_concentration_warn_pct` (30%) only warns and never limits a buy.

**Observed 27 Sep** (the owner's screen): FEDERALBNK is ₹1,30,732 of ₹9,99,611, **13.1%**, above the 10% cap. The cause is not verified; it may be a manual add.

Make one sizing rule for every autopilot buy: ADD, SWITCH, and SA-049's new-position buys.

- **Caps after the trade:** the position at most `max_position_pct`, and the sector at most a new `autopilot.max_sector_pct` (proposed 30).
- **The cash floor** is kept.
- **Leftover proceeds stay in cash,** with a note saying how much was capped and why. SA-049 then decides what cash does.
- **Manual adds are out of scope.** A manual add is the user's own choice and is not capped here. Nothing is force-sold: an existing over-cap position is left to the TRIM rules.

## Additions from the production baseline (2026-10-12)

Source: section 11 of the [product results review](../../../audit/2026-10-11-product-results-review.md), from the owner's read-only probe of 11 Oct.

**1. Measure weight against the whole portfolio.**

- Today the advisor computes a holding's weight against the **holdings' total cost**
  (`advisor.build_signals`). The autopilot's ADD headroom uses the **holdings' market value**
  (`_portfolio_market_value`). Neither counts cash.
- **Observed:** FEDERALBNK is about 13.4% of equity, but the advisor sees 72.9%. So ADD can never fire
  while the portfolio holds two stocks, and `SECTOR_CONCENTRATION_HIGH` was noted 18 times since
  20 Sep on a portfolio that is 81% cash.
- **Change:** every cap and note uses **total equity** (holdings at market plus cash) as the
  denominator: the ADD check in `decide`, the post-trade caps here, and the concentration note.
- The note splits in two: `POSITION_LARGE` (one holding over the position cap) and
  `SECTOR_CONCENTRATION_HIGH` (one sector over the sector cap, summed across holdings).

**2. One entry rule for every buy.**

- **Observed:** WELCORP was shelved on 15 Aug at ₹1,873, inside its band of ₹1,817–₹1,911.
  A SWITCH bought it on 1 Sep at ₹2,553.60, after a 36% run, **34% above the band**. The SWITCH
  path never reads the idea's band.
- **Change:** every autopilot buy that opens a new position (SWITCH destination, SA-049 deploy)
  needs a price at or below the idea's `entry_high` and above its `invalidation_level`.
  - Above the band: skip, with the reason "x% above entry band; needs a fresh deep dive". The
    proceeds stay in cash for SA-049 to place.
  - Below the band but above invalidation: allowed, because it is cheaper than planned. The note
    says so.

Added acceptance:

- The 11 Oct shape: FEDERALBNK (406 × ₹330) and WELCORP (21 × ₹2,640.40) with ₹8,09,297 cash.
  The advisor's position weights are 13.4% and 5.6% of equity (₹9,98,725). A bullish, healthy WELCORP may
  ADD; FEDERALBNK, over the 10% cap, may not.
- The WELCORP switch: a destination 34% above its band is not bought; the reason and the
  amount left in cash are recorded.
- A property test: no autopilot buy lands above its idea's `entry_high` or below its
  `invalidation_level`.

## Acceptance criteria

- The ₹2.27L switch above buys at most the 10% position, derived by hand, and leaves the rest in cash with a `capped` note.
- A buy that would push its sector over the cap is reduced, or skipped when under 1 share, with the reason recorded.
- ADD keeps today's behaviour when no cap binds (a characterization test).
- A property test over random portfolios: after any autopilot run, no position bought or added by the autopilot exceeds either cap, and cash stays at or above the floor.
