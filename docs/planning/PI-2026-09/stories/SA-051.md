# SA-051 — Show the portfolio against the Nifty, with honest labels

Priority: **P1** · Sprint: **2** · Provisional estimate: **3 points**

Status lives in [STATE.json](../STATE.json), not this card. Dependencies: none. Added 2026-09-27 at the owner's request, after reviewing the live portfolio screen.

## Intended result

The owner's portfolio screen of 27 Sep answers "how much?" but not "is this good?". It also mislabels its largest number.

- **"Invested ₹10,00,000"** is `capital_in`, the total mock money ever put in. It moves only when money is added, never with trades. It reads as "money in stocks", which was about ₹1.9L (19%).
- **"Total return −₹389 (−0.0%)"** has nothing to compare with. If the Nifty rose 3% over the same weeks, flat is a loss against holding the index; if it fell 3%, flat is a win.
- **Prices without dates.** WELCORP showed ₹2,832.40 in the table and "closed at ₹2,692.20" in its agent card, with no date on either.
- **No trade record.** Winners and losers are visible one at a time in "recent activity", but there is no win rate, average win or average loss.

Add, on the portfolio screen and in `/portfolio/performance`:

- **Labels.** "Invested" becomes **"Capital added"**. Add **"In stocks ₹X (Y%)"** beside **"Cash ₹Z (W%)"**. Every price shows its date.
- **"If this had been in the Nifty".** Each deposit of capital buys Nifty 50 units at that day's close. The line on the value chart is those units × today's Nifty, with the gap in rupees and percent. It uses `core/audit/benchmark.BenchmarkSeries` (`^NSEI`). A missing benchmark says "unavailable", and is never drawn as a flat line.
- **Closed-trade record.** Number of trades, win rate, average win, average loss, and expectancy per trade:
  win rate × average win − loss rate × average loss.
  - It is labelled "before costs" until SA-048 lands, then net.
  - **Example:** 6 wins of +3% and 4 losses of −10% is a 60% win rate. Expectancy is 0.6×3 − 0.4×10 = −2.2% per trade. A majority of winners can still lose money, so the screen shows both numbers.

**Shared with [PR-002](PR-002.md) (2026-10-11).** The weekly scorecard needs the same money-weighted Nifty function and closed-trade record. Whichever story lands first owns them in one pure module (proposed `core/portfolio/results.py`), and the other reuses it. There must be one implementation, not two.

**Follow-ups from PR-002's review (2026-10-16).** PR-002 landed first, so `core/portfolio/results.py`
owns `deposits_from_values` and `nifty_equivalent`; reuse them. Its fresh review
([receipt](../evidence/PR-002-review.md), F1 and F2) found two gaps in the deposit rule. This
story fixes them for the chart and the weekly scorecard together.

- **F1 (medium): an added holding enters at its cost.** "Add holding" with an earlier buy date
  or a typed price, and a CSV import, raise `capital_in` by qty × the buy price, but the stock
  arrives at its market value. **Example:** 1,000 shares bought at ₹50 and worth ₹150 add
  ₹50,000 of "deposit" and ₹1,50,000 of equity. The ₹1,00,000 gained before the add then counts
  as the portfolio's, and a firing "Behind the Nifty" flag reads clear. Value such an addition
  on both sides at its close on the day it arrives. If that close is unavailable, say "could
  not check" for the windows it touches. **Acceptance:** ₹10 L of idle cash while the Nifty goes
  100 → 110 over 4 weeks, plus that add on a day the Nifty is 105. The 4-week window must read
  0.0% against +9.3%, and the flag must fire.
- **F2 (low, latent until about 2028): retention moves "since the start".** The nightly prune
  keeps the last 400 value points. The first kept point's `capital_in` then becomes an opening
  deposit on a later date. Date deposits from a record that is never pruned, such as SA-049's
  dated `DEPOSIT` transactions, or make retention keep the first point.

## Acceptance criteria

- For a fixture with two deposits on different days and a hand-built Nifty series, the Nifty-equivalent value matches a hand computation.
- For a fixture ledger of closed trades, the win rate, average win, average loss and expectancy match hand figures. An open position is never counted as closed.
- The labels read "Capital added", "In stocks (share)" and "Cash (share)". The two shares add to 100% of equity.
- Every price on the screen shows its as-of date.
- An unavailable benchmark reads "unavailable", with no line and no gap.
