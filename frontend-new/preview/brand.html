Voice — do / don't

✓ Do — Bloomberg-clinical, ticker-first

"MARUTI remains the structural beneficiary of India's four-wheeler penetration story."

✓ Do — Measured conviction

"Agent report generated in 47s · 2 conflicts resolved by LLM."

✓ Do — Address the user directly

"Your Watchlist · 5 tickers · 3 pending refresh."

✗ Don't — salesy or emoji-laden

"🚀 MARUTI is mooning! Our AI loves this stock — you should too!"

✗ Don't — hedged corporate-speak

"Our platform leverages synergies to deliver actionable intelligence."

✗ Don't — first-person plural

"We think Maruti is a buy. We believe fundamentals are strong."

Photography / imagery policy

• No photos. Zero stock photography, office shots, or lifestyle imagery anywhere in the product.
• No decorative gradients. Gradients appear only inside progress bars (cyan→blue) and behind the 3D globe on Landing.
• No illustrations. Data is the imagery. Charts, gauges, radars, sparklines — nothing else.
• Emoji only inside AgentCard icons. 📊 📈 🔍 ⚙️ 💬 📋 🎯 ⚠️ — never in copy, CTAs, or headers.
